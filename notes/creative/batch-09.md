## For David (one minute)

We asked: given the echoes the observer has heard so far, what share of the matching worlds predicts each possible next echo? We counted the worlds exactly on a computer for the simplest crystal universes, 2D and 3D grids. The answer is disappointing but clear. Before the observer has heard enough echoes to pin down every direction of the universe's repeat, almost all matching worlds say "no new echo yet". That share climbs to 100% as more and more worlds are included, so the count never predicts an echo. Once the echoes do pin down every direction, the matching worlds collapse to exactly one, and the next echo is certain. So the count only ever gives "0% or 100%", never an in-between share like a quantum probability. Different ways of listing the worlds (by size, by width, by how simply they're described) agree, but only because they all give this trivial answer. Born-like structure does not appear here.

## Model (sketch)

- **Hosts.** Static flat tori $\mathbb R^n/\Gamma$ carrying a $\mathbb Z^n$-periodic crystal pattern. The pattern makes $G=\mathbb Z^n$ the (discrete) symmetry group and pins the observer at the origin ("pinned by germ", unverified per #355). Γ ranges over the finite-index sublattices of $\mathbb Z^n$.
- **Observer and echoes.** The observer is at rest at the origin. The beacon switches on at $t=0$ (#355 needs a definite switch-on time). The echo via $g\in\Gamma\setminus\{0\}$ first arrives at proper time $|g|_2$, with $c=1$ and lattice unit 1.
- **Record.** $R_\tau(\Gamma)=\{g\in\Gamma\setminus\{0\}: |g|\le\tau\}$, as exact vectors: a fixed frame, which is the non-lossy version (#355 notes the isotropy-angle gauge loses information).
- **Simplifications.** Only first arrivals are recorded, not #355's continuous arrival curves. A static torus, so no expansion.
- **Counting.** Over pointed worlds, i.e. pointed subgroups (see (iii)). Sublattices are enumerated by Hermite normal form.
- **Conditional share.** $P(R'\mid R)=\lim_{N\to\infty}\#\{\Gamma:[\mathbb Z^n:\Gamma]\le N,\ R_\tau=R,\ R_{\tau'}=R'\}\,/\,\#\{\Gamma:[\mathbb Z^n:\Gamma]\le N,\ R_\tau=R\}$.

## (i) Conditional shares (index cutoff)

$\mathbb Z^2$, from $\tau=1$ to $\tau'=2$:

| N | #subgroups | given empty record: share "no new echo" | given one echo pair: share "no further echo" |
|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | 339 | 0.760 | 0.947 |
| 40 | 1342 | 0.880 | 0.974 |
| 80 | 5314 | 0.939 | 0.987 |

$\mathbb Z^3$, from $\tau=1$ to $\tau'=1.5$:

| N | #subgroups | given empty record: "no new echo" | given one echo pair: "no further echo" |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 737 | 0.376 | 0.750 |
| 20 | 5644 | 0.659 | 0.877 |
| 30 | 18868 | 0.766 | 0.919 |

For $\mathbb Z^2$, $1-\text{share}$ falls like $1/N$: $0.240\to0.120\to0.061$ as $N$ doubles. For $\mathbb Z^3$ it falls roughly like $1/N$ too: $0.624\to0.341\to0.234$. **The limit share of "no new echo" is 1 for every record that does not yet span full rank**, which confirms #355's degeneracy (L30) at the level of conditional shares.

**Full-rank records.** Example in $\mathbb Z^2$: at $\tau=2$ the record is $\{(\pm2,0),(0,\pm2)\}$. For every $N\ge8$ exactly **one** world matches ($\Gamma=2\mathbb Z^2$), and the next record is certain (share 1). Sketch of why this holds in general:
- Once $R_\tau$ contains a basis of a full-rank sublattice $L_0$, Γ is an overlattice of $L_0$, and there are only finitely many.
- Any proper overlattice adds a vector no longer than the covering radius of $L_0$. That is $\le\tau$ whenever $\tau$ is at least the reduced basis lengths, for $n\le3$ (unchecked beyond the 2D example).
- So the record then fixes Γ exactly.

**Result.** Every conditional share is 0 or 1 in the limit. Before full rank it is degenerate ("no new echo", share 1); after full rank it is deterministic (one world). No nontrivial share arises in this model.

## (ii) Listing independence (S1)

$\mathbb Z^2$, given an empty record at $\tau=1$, the share "no new echo by $\tau'=2$":

| listing | cutoffs | shares |
|---|---|---|
| index (= volume) | 25 / 100 / 400 | 0.810 / 0.952 / 0.988 |
| covering radius (diameter proxy; exact in 2D) | 3 / 6 / 10 | 0.848 / 0.978 / 0.995 |
| word length, generators $e_1,e_2$ named (Γ generated by elements of $L^1$ norm $\le L$) | 4 / 8 / 12 | 0.689 / 0.941 / 0.980 |

- **Same limit, 1, under all three listings.** But they agree only because the answer is degenerate.
- **At finite cutoffs they differ noticeably**, e.g. 0.81 vs 0.85 vs 0.69 at the smallest cutoffs. So any non-limit use would be listing-dependent.
- Diameter in $\mathbb Z^3$ was not computed (no exact covering-radius routine). Systole is not an exhaustion (#356 S2).

## (iii) $E_{\mathcal W}$ vs $E_M$

From v0.10.1 §4: "A world is $\mathcal W=(M,p)$. $E_{\mathcal W}(O)$ is the set of pointed worlds whose point $p$ is an occurrence of the germ $O$, and $E_M(O)$ is the corresponding collection of manifolds."
- **The count here is over $E_{\mathcal W}$.** Each sublattice Γ, with the observer pinned at the origin, is one pointed world.
- For abelian $G=\mathbb Z^n$, conjugation is trivial, so pointed subgroups and isometry classes of $M$ differ only by the point-group symmetries of the crystal (not quotiented here).
- For non-abelian $G$ the two counts differ (#355). **A non-abelian example was not computed** in this batch: enumerating finite-index subgroups by HNF works for $\mathbb Z^n$ only. That is left open.

## (iv) Standing rule

- **Label.** Conjecture: count gives Born (to be derived, not assumed).
- **How the worlds become countable.** Crystal covers with discrete $G=\mathbb Z^n$ and finite-index Γ, enumerated at each index $N$ (finite sets).
- **What would make the count inevitable.** Listing-independence of the limit shares. It holds here, but only trivially (0/1).
- **(M1)** is unchanged and not used. Under (M1), each record-class at a split would get an equal share regardless of these counts.
- **Born-like structure.** **None appears.** No in-between share exists in this model, so there is nothing to compare with $|a|^2$, and no comparison is made.

## Weak points

1. The model is static and flat with first arrivals only. #355's continuous arrival curves, expansion, and the lossy gauge are not modelled.
2. The degeneracy may be an artefact of abelian, flat crystals: subgroup growth $N^n$ against $N^{n-1}$. Non-abelian or hyperbolic lattices, with different subgroup growth, are untested.
3. "Full-rank record fixes Γ" is checked in one 2D example and sketched for $n\le3$.
4. Pinned-by-germ is unverified (#355).
5. Diameter in 3D and a non-abelian group were not done.

## Lesson (L31)

In crystal covers, counting worlds gives only 0/1 conditional shares: degenerate before the echoes span full rank, deterministic after. The listings agree only in this trivial way. A non-trivial share would need either a host class whose subgroup counts consistent with a record grow at the same rate as the total (not $\mathbb Z^n$), or records that stay ambiguous at full rank.
