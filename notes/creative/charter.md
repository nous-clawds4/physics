~~~text
What it means to be creative pursuit of the goal

The goal:
a clear definition of observer-space (currently: in terms of germs, jets)
a clear definition of movement of the observer through state-space; evolution of the observer in proper time
movement needs to be somewhere between fully deterministic and wild chaos, so we get paths with branching
a clear definition of a world (currently: a spacetime plus a point p)
a clear definition of the full set of all possible worlds, and a definition of all worlds that are consistent with a given observer
a clear method for the observer state to constrain compatible worlds to a subset, but not uniquely; the observer, via introspection, needs to deduce something about the global state, but not everything
a clear notion of the evolution of the ensemble as the observer travels through observer space; 
a clear method to model an experimental test of the Born rule (currently: see old papers for approximating a 4-manifold that is microscopically violent topology with nontrivial fundamental group onto spacetime that is simply connected and mostly flat at the human scale)

The method: iterate through creative ideas; have a team talk about them; consider entire sets of ideas as a set; ok to recycle an old creative idea with a new set of ideas; find a set that matches the above goal

Tentative definitions:
standard definitions of 4-manifold, analytic, equal probability postulate
all possible worlds includes all possible fundamental groups, highly nontrivial 

Terms and ideas that are candidates for creative tweaking
definitions of observer space
equal probability postulate 

Examples of creative ideas to be explored
some sort of lattice network instead of smooth manifold 
maybe its not infinitely smooth, but only differentiable up to k; maybe then we let k approach infinity to recover our starting point
instead of analytic, use Gevrey
maybe require embedding of the manifold into a higher dimensional space
maybe let the observer be higher dimensional than the 4-manifold, like the ant on a surface, so it can measure extrinsic curvature
memoryless or not? Maybe something in-between? 
can we approximate microscopically nontrivial manifolds onto flat manifolds
can we justify an approximation in terms of flatness by asserting curvature approaches 0 within particular realms of k, e.g. flat at the smallest scale, nonflat (and nontrivial topology) microscopically, flat at human scale, then nonflat (maybe curved, but maybe simply connected) again at cosmic scale? Here we are playing with k, curvature, and connectedness all at the same time, using “low energy approximation” as justification for certain assumptions
How else can we creatively apply the low energy approximation to achieve our goal? apply it not just to k, curvature, and connectedness, but something else?

Themes
local versus global. We need local state to tell us something about global state, but not everything.

When doing existence proofs of things like whether the ensemble evolves with the observer, avoid reliance on non-physical examples like extensible manifolds where the observer has been arbitrarily removed

We should periodically make a list of ideas for creative tweaks. Judge them based on: do they have any arbitrary, non-physical assumptions that we cannot justify? If yes, can we envision a path where we could justify in the future? Example: k is an arbitrary integer, but if we let k approach infinity, we no longer need to justify it.
~~~

## Addendum: germ-order formulation (David, 5:00 PM ET 2026-10-13, relayed by Physics Lead, Physics Lead chat, not signed; verbatim)

> One idea I have for formulating the LEA in a mathematically rigorous manner is in terms of the order k of the derivatives of the germ. We might say that flat at human scale means that k should be uniformly 0 (or below some very low number) within some interval that defines that scale; but violently twisted microscopically would be equivalent to k not being uniformly low within some other interval of k that corresponds to the microscopic scale.

Physics Lead's reading (Lead reading, not David's): the order-k term ~|a_k| ℓ^k; human-scale flatness means orders ≥ 2 are negligible at ℓ_H; microscopic twisting means a_k ~ ℓ_P^(−k), with radius of convergence ~ℓ_P; π₁ is global and is reached by the germ only via analytic continuation under the lock.

## Addendum: three regimes (David, 5:03 PM ET 2026-10-13, relayed by Physics Lead, Physics Lead chat, not signed; verbatim)

> when we get smaller than microscopic, it needs to get flat again, because that's one of the defining features of a manifold... at the cosmic scale, we don't know whether it's flat or not, so we should be careful not to assert that it must necessarily be flat.

Lead notes (Physics Lead, not David's): (a) the LEA has three regimes: locally flat for ℓ ≪ ℓ_P (the manifold property), twisted near ℓ_P, and flat across a bounded human-scale window [ℓ_low, ℓ_high]; a radius of convergence near ℓ_P is not non-flatness below ℓ_P. (b) No claim about cosmic scales; the flat projection is needed only over a bounded window containing the emitter and detectors.
