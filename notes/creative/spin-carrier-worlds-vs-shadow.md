## 2. Naming (Geometry reading)

- **Friedman–Sorkin geon:** topology. A spinorial prime summand of space.
- **Dirac–MTW box:** a spin frame in flat space, that is, a frame plus a ±1 lift sign from the double cover of the rotation group. No topology.
- Geometry corrects its own 3:05 PM remark that "FS-geon model" names the box: it does not.

## 4. Two readings for David (Geometry readings; neither is picked)

**(R-geon) Spin from topology.** In Worlds spin comes from topology, so particles must be spinorial primes (e.g. the Poincaré sphere). Handles and lens spaces are excluded (Giulini, Thm 2; Geometry's citation, unchecked). The Dirac box copies the geon's sign and adds none of its own.

**(R-frame) Spin frame on every edge** (Geometry's 4:44 PM confirmation). Worldtube reading: an empty edge carries the frame only; a P edge carries the frame plus a tube whose cross-section is a ball containing P.
- Species = tensorial topology (lens spaces L(p,q), S¹×S²). Fermion vs boson = frame type.
- A spinorial P would add a second, independent ℤ₂ and could flip the composite to boson-like. So species are restricted to tensorial primes, or the reading must say which rotation counts.
- Every edge, empty ones included, carries a fermion-type frame, so bosons need a different frame type. Possible hook for David's arbitrary-spin hope (a hope, not a ruling): spin = which representation the frame carries (Geometry reading).

**Fit with #434's rule.** Gibbons–Hawking conserves only the total count, mod 2, of primes with odd dim H₁(ℤ₂).
- L(p,q): dim H₁(ℤ₂) = 1 for p even, 0 for p odd. S¹×S²: 1. Poincaré sphere: H₁ = 0, so unconstrained.
- So R-geon species face no parity conservation from Gibbons–Hawking at all; R-frame species are partly constrained (even-p lens spaces, S¹×S²).
- In both readings species conservation remains a postulate.
- Neither reading forces (F) or (W) (#436 §4a). Under (W) the empty-edge frame sits idle.

## 5. Open item: clock rate (Geometry reading)

Parallel transport does not turn the frame on a flat edge (#428 R5/Y1). A tick needs one of: holonomy; a tangent-slaved (helicity-type) frame (#428 R3); or a proper-time rate, which needs Planck's constant and was rejected for alignment in #419 R1. φ = θ/2 fixes the relation between phase and angle, not what drives θ.
