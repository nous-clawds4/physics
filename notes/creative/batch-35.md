## Plain summary for David

You asked how to choose a probability rule without cheating. We wrote down six guidelines, as guidelines and not a pass/fail test, as you asked at 4:24 PM:
1. **State the rule first,** before trying it on any two-path or fringe setup.
2. **Use only what one world contains** (its geometry, topology, spin structure and records). No adding phases across worlds, no squaring, nothing that exists only in the shadow spacetime.
3. **Prefer a rule that doesn't depend on how we slice things up**: counting paths, worlds or branch points, and blurring into the shadow spacetime, should ideally agree. Agreement between counting worlds and the observer-space branch structure is a hope, not a requirement. "Slicing" still needs a precise definition.
4. **Stay consistent with (M1)**, or give an independent reason to revisit it, out in the open.
5. **No free numbers.** Constants come from the geometry or from normalisation, never from fitting.
6. **Aim for a rule the geometry forces**, not one it merely allows. List every candidate that fits 1–5; if several survive, say so, and don't pick one because of its answer.

After that, and only after, compare with batch 34's leftover count and report whatever comes out. Your three candidates (count paths, count worlds, branch points with equal probability) are listed but not evaluated. My intuition, since you asked: the most likely place for the geometry to force a choice is guideline 3. If the counts must agree across slicings and across the shadow projection, only a few rules can survive. Whether one is left is open.

## 2. Guidelines (Creative Lead proposal, adopted by David as guidelines, not a test, at 4:24 PM)

- **G1. State the rule blind.** Say what it counts and how it is normalised before applying it to any two-path or fringe setup.
- **G2. World data only.** Metric, topology, spin structure, records. No cross-world phase sums (#419 R5: a sum of e^{iφ} across worlds is a complex weight on W), no squared quantities, nothing defined only in the shadow spacetime.
- **G3. Prefer slicing-independence.** Counting paths, worlds or branch points, and coarse-graining to the shadow spacetime, should give the same answer. Agreement between world counting and observer-space branch structure is a hope, not a requirement (David, 4:24 PM). **Flag:** "slicing" is not yet precisely defined. It may mean the choice of cut τ, the grain of the record, the identification class in the LEA, or the foliation, which with CTCs need not exist globally.
- **G4. Consistent with (M1)**, or give an independent reason to revisit it. Never override it quietly. (M1) stays at L3.
- **G5. No free numbers.** Constants are fixed by geometry or normalisation, never fitted.
- **G6. Forced rather than merely allowed.** List the candidates that fit G1–G5. If several survive, say so; do not choose one by its Born output.
- **Then:** compare the result with batch 34's leftover count (#424: leftover ≥ resultant for exact closure, a floor that survives projection) and report it, whatever it is.
