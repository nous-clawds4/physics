## 2. The proposal (Geometry's paraphrase of David)

1. Every detection event is a pairing of two routes from source to detector, like a virtual pair.
2. A **virtual pair** is glued directly at a vertex. Its phases must match exactly, which is David's exclusion rule (#424 §4a and #427: 3:33/3:40 PM argument, ruling at 3:58 PM), and it leaves no record.
3. A **detection pair** is glued indirectly, through the detector's matter. The detector interaction affects the phases, so they need not match, exactly or approximately. The gluing leaves a record in the detector (ledger model, #388). The mechanism is not yet described.
4. The return route is not the visible particle. There is no which-way measurement: the observer knows detector i fired, not which route was taken.
5. On detection the observer branches in observer space. Right after the branch, the ensemble contains, for every route compatible with the record, at least one world in which that route occurs.
6. David corrected his earlier word "unpaired": the routes are paired, but through the detector.
