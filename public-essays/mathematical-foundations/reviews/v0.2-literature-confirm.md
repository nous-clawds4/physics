## F1. The Bell table: no signalling does not take a model out of the parameter/outcome row (required)

The "Locality, reread" cost says the model is "filed with the parameter/outcome row unless the mapping shows no signalling and no preferred frame". That test is wrong. Suppose measurement independence, single outcomes and no retrocausation all hold under the mapping. Then reproducing the quantum statistics requires Bell's factorisation to fail in spacetime. That failure is the parameter/outcome row, whether or not it signals or picks a frame. Relativistic GRW fails outcome independence with neither signalling nor a preferred frame, and it is still Bell-nonlocal (Tumulka, *J. Stat. Phys.* 125, 821 (2006)). The row is honest once it says the model is nonlocal in spacetime in Bell's sense and local in $V$. Bell does not exclude that, just as it does not exclude Bohm or GRW. This also fits the Problem essay's own wording, "local in observer-space, not in classical spacetime".

**"Locality, reread" cost, replace with:**
> if the other premises hold under the mapping, Bell's factorisation fails in spacetime, so the model is Bell-nonlocal in spacetime and local in $V$, which Bell does not exclude; no signalling and no preferred frame are further costs to check (relativistic GRW incurs neither: Tumulka), not a way out of the parameter/outcome row; no mapping given

**Parameter/outcome row cost, replace with:**
> nonlocal in spacetime in Bell's sense; parameter-independence failure (Bohm) needs a preferred foliation, outcome-independence failure alone need not signal or pick a frame (Tumulka)

**Add to References:** Tumulka, *J. Stat. Phys.* 125, 821 (2006).

## F2. Rideout–Sorkin weights are narrowed, not forced (required)

Discrete general covariance and Bell causality do not fix one measure. They cut the dynamics down to a family with free couplings $t_0,t_1,\dots$, of which transitive percolation is the one-parameter case (Rideout and Sorkin, *Phys. Rev. D* 61, 024002 (2000)). **Replace the §7 row's first two cells with:**
> Weights narrowed by symmetry/causality conditions (Rideout–Sorkin) | axioms cut the choice to a family (couplings $t_n$), not one measure

v0.6's §10 row ("a precedent for weights forced by axioms") has the same slip. It should be fixed in the next framework version along with the "on a given metric" slip in its Gielen–Wise row.
