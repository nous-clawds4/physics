# Mathematical Foundations

This essay lays out the mathematical alternatives the observer-space program could use to meet the aims stated in its companion essay, [*Statement of the Problem*](../statement-of-the-problem/). Its stance is strong opinions, weakly held, and it is expected to change often.

**Current version:** [`versions/v0.1.md`](versions/v0.1.md)

## Change control

1. **Review.** Every version goes through panel review as usual.
2. **Clause named.** Every choice or alternative names the *Statement of the Problem* note (clause) that it serves.
3. **Precedence.** *Statement of the Problem* takes precedence over this essay. Where the two conflict, this essay yields, and the conflict is raised with David.

## Relation to other files

The repo-root file `mathematical-framework.md` stays as it is, with its banner, until David signs off on a change. This essay does not edit or replace it.
