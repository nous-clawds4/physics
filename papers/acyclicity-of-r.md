# Acyclicity of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. After Theorems 20–21 and 31: a pumpable cycle makes $N(p,q)$ infinite, not the *set* of finite walks uncountable. Acyclicity (a DAG) is extra structure for $N(p,q)\in\mathbb{N}$, distinct from unique-tree and from countable out-degree. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/fusion-and-path-counting.md`, `papers/countable-outdegree.md`, `papers/tree-as-structure.md`, `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Hitchhiker $\tau$ is acyclic along $\gamma_O$ (type (i)). Type-(ii) $R$ is not required to be acyclic (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Unique-tree is a stronger extra. Countable out-degree (Theorem 31) makes the set of finite walks from a root countable *with cycles allowed*. Acyclicity is for the *count* $N$ to be finite. Not adopted. Not a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$.

## 1. Two extras, not one

**Countable walk-*set*.** Theorem 31: countable out-degree $\Rightarrow$ countable finite walks from $O_0$. Cycles allowed. Dual, backward: countable in-degree $\Rightarrow$ countable finite walks into $q$; a pumpable cycle does not uncountable that set (Theorem 20 makes $N$ infinite, not the set a continuum).

**Finite walk-*count*.** Theorem 20: a pumpable cycle $\Rightarrow$ $N(p,q)=\infty$. Theorem 21: on a DAG with finitely many walks $p\to q$, $N(p,q)\in\mathbb{N}\cup\{0\}$. Unique-tree is extra on top of a DAG (no fusion).

2008 path-counting as a *relative probability* needs a finite $N$, hence acyclicity or some other extra cut of walks (simple paths: extra, already named). Countability of the typicality *set* does not.

## 2. Not lock-side

In-world geodesics do not reverse $\tau$. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not force $R$ to be a DAG. Chronological future in $P(O)$ does not finite-support $R$ (Theorem 30). Empty $R$ is a DAG; a ray is a DAG; both are maps and not the 2008 job.
