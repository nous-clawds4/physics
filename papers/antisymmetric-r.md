# Antisymmetry of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Asymmetry (`papers/asymmetric-r.md`, on main via #67): $(O,O^\prime)\in R$ implies $(O^\prime,O)\notin R$. That forbids 2-cycles and implies irreflexivity. Antisymmetry is a different extra: $(O,O^\prime)\in R$ and $(O^\prime,O)\in R$ implies $O=O^\prime$. Self-loops are allowed. 2-cycles of *distinct* lumps are forbidden. With irreflexivity it is equivalent to asymmetry; it is not a third independent extra on an already-irreflexive $R$. Distinct from transitivity. Both-ways as lock-side was already killed (Corollary 20.1); this is the extra of *forbidding* both-ways among distinct lumps, not a claim that the lock supplies it. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/asymmetric-r.md`, `papers/irreflexive-r.md`, `papers/transitive-r.md`, `papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`, `papers/fusion-and-path-counting.md` (Theorems 20--21, Corollary 20.1), `papers/type-ii-clock.md` (Theorem 23(1)), `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Hitchhiker $\tau$ is antisymmetric along $\gamma_O$ (type (i)). That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not force $R$ to be antisymmetric. Empty $R$ is antisymmetric. A singleton directed edge is antisymmetric. A self-loop is antisymmetric and not asymmetric. They are maps and not the 2008 job. Not lock-side (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Not a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$. Not adopted.

## 1. Not the same as asymmetry

**Asymmetry.** No 2-cycle, including no self-loop. On main via #67. Implies irreflexivity. Not reopened.

**Irreflexivity.** No $(O,O)$. On main via #64. A 2-cycle of distinct lumps is irreflexive. Not reopened.

**Transitivity.** Shortcuts. On main via #66. Not reopened. Irreflexive + transitive already implies asymmetric (`papers/asymmetric-r.md`).

**Antisymmetry.** If both ways, then $O=O^\prime$. Extra. A self-loop is antisymmetric. A 2-cycle of distinct lumps is not. On an irreflexive $R$, antisymmetry $\Leftrightarrow$ asymmetry, so it is not a third independent extra there.

Theorem 20: a pumpable 2-cycle of distinct lumps makes $N$ infinite. Antisymmetry kills *that* pump, not every cycle (a 3-cycle can be antisymmetric). Acyclicity is still a different extra for finite $N$.

Corollary 20.1 killed both-ways as lock-side. This note does not revive it. Antisymmetry is extra structure on $R$, not a theorem of the jet/patch.

## 2. Not lock-side and not a grain

Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). In-world geodesics do not reverse $\tau$. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)).

Antisymmetry does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. $V$ may remain a continuum. It does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable (Theorem 31 is countable out-degree). It does not finite-support $R$.

**Not claimed.** No antisymmetric $R$ exists. Empty $R$ is antisymmetric. A singleton directed edge is antisymmetric. A self-loop is antisymmetric. They are maps and not the 2008 job.
