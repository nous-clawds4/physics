## 1. One-step vs history-dependent

**Markov.** Definition 9: a pair $(V,R)$ with $V\subseteq\mathrm{Obs}$ and $R\subseteq V\times V$. A one-step extension of a finite walk looks only at the last vertex. Walks on $(V,R)$ are sequences of vertices with consecutive pairs in $R$. 2008 path-counting of finite walks on a graph presupposes that packaging.

**History-dependent, extra-on-extra.** A rule on finite walks that permits a one-step extension of $\pi$ only after looking at the whole of $\pi$, not just its last vertex. That is a relation on $\mathrm{Path}$, not a relation on $V$. Not supplied by the jet/patch, not by hitchhiking, not by Definition 9. Counting those trajectories is a different extra typicality rule, not path-count on Definition 9.

Empty $R$ and $\{O_{\mathrm{Mink}}\}$ are Markov laws; they are maps and not the 2008 job.
