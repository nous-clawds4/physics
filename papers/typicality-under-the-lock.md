# Typicality under the lock

David Strayhorn

What a typicality claim is still allowed to count, given the locked object and Theorems 2–5. This is the 2008 job restated under the lock. It is not a Born derivation. It is not a new hypothesis. It does not write $\Phi$. It does not fold $E$ into $\sim$.

Companions: `papers/cheat-sheet.md`, `papers/analytic-split.md`, `papers/geodesic-lifts.md`, `papers/ensemble-labels.md`, `papers/observer-space-ontology.md`.

**Status.** There is no remaining typicality theorem that does the 2008 job without a new hypothesis. The locked geometry kills the counts that looked like “most observers,” and it does not replace them.

---

## 0. The 2008 job, under the lock

Paper 1 needs a typicality statement: the laws of general relativity and of quantum mechanics appear valid to the vast majority of observers. For that sentence to mean anything, there must be one reasonable measure on what is being counted.

The 2008 illustration counted worlds of a spin-measurement tree, individuated by records, equally. That is not this ontology. The objects here are germs $(j_\infty,u)$, equivalently simply-connected analytic patches. Motion stand-in: geodesic hitchhiking. Distinctness: $\sim$ on curves in $\mathrm{Obs}$. Extra topologies: the ensemble, not a second observer.

The question is only: *what, if anything, is left to count?*

---

## 1. Killed

Each item is a consequence of a named theorem, not a new cut.

| Claim | Status | Pointer |
|---|---|---|
| Delayed $\mathrm{Obs}$-branches of analytic geodesic lifts | killed | Thm 3, Cor. 3.1: two analytic lifts with a thick prefix coincide |
| Typicality as a count of those forks | killed | There are no such forks to count |
| $E(O)$ as extra elementary observers | killed | Def. 1–2: pointed occurrences, not points of $\mathrm{Obs}$; not folded into $\sim$ |
| Treating $E_\gamma$ members as extra curves in $\mathrm{Obs}$ | killed | Prop. 3.2, Thm 4: they share the unique curvelet $\gamma_O$ |
| $M$ or $\lvert E\rvert$ as $\lvert a\rvert^2$ | killed | $M$ is occurrence multiplicity; amplitude does not enter $\sim$ or $E$ |
| An uncountable census of topologies as the typicality set | killed | Thm 5: no complete census (Markov); compact Lorentzian $\Rightarrow\chi=0$ |

Paper 1’s circularity sentence stays: you cannot define the count by Born weight and then call the count a justification of Born weight. Nothing below reopens that.

---

## 2. What remains, as objects

Under the lock, the things $\sim$ can even *see* are curves in $\mathrm{Obs}$. Realized paths are geodesic lifts: analytic maps $I\to\mathrm{Obs}$ of the form $\pi_W(\sigma)$ (Thm 3). Distinct realized paths meet in a discrete set or coincide (Thm 2 applied to Thm 3).

From one elementary observer $O$:

- geodesic hitchhiking produces **one** such path, $\gamma_O$ (Prop. 3.2);
- many $W$ in $E_\gamma(O)$ realize that same path (Thm 4);
- those $W$ are locally invisible along $\gamma_O$ in $\mathrm{int}\,P(O)$.

So a typicality claim that starts at one $O$ and asks “which way did most hitchhikers go after $\tau_*$?” has an empty branch set. A typicality claim that counts $E_\gamma(O)$ as extra hitchhikers is counting labels of one curve, not distinct evolutions.

What $\sim$ *would* count, if the objects cooperate, is distinct realized paths in $\mathrm{Obs}$ — distinct $\sim$-classes of analytic maps $I\to\mathrm{Obs}$ coming from geodesic lifts, possibly from many initial $O$. That is a set of curves, not a set of topologies and not a set of Hilbert-space branches.

---

## 3. There is no theorem that this set is countable

Paper 1’s unique-measure claim is: *if* distinct evolutions are countable, counting is the unique reasonable measure. The antecedent is not supplied here.

- $\mathrm{Obs}$ itself is not identified with a countable set (jet order infinite, Fréchet / inverse limit still open as a home).
- The set of realized paths is a set of analytic curves in that space. Nothing forces it to be countable.
- $E(O)$ is not that set, and countability of $E(O)$ is already open (`papers/ensemble-labels.md`). Even if a later bound made $E(O)$ countable, those members would still not be extra hitchhikers.
- Do not put a presentation-complexity bound in by hand. Do not import $\lvert a\rvert^2$ to cut a continuum down to size.

Without countability, “vast majority” fails in the Bertrand way, as Paper 1 already said. The geometry has not fixed that. It has only removed several wrong sets one might have counted instead.

---

## 4. No replacement count

A temptation: keep the 2008 illustration alive by counting $M$, or $\lvert E_\gamma\rvert$, or compact $\chi=0$ manifolds, or $\pi_1$-presentations of bounded length. Each of those is either killed as a typicality set (it is not a set of distinct evolutions) or is a new hypothesis (a bound not locked). This note does not adopt one.

**Proposition.** Under the locked object, geodesic hitchhiking, Theorems 2–5, and no further hypothesis: there is no theorem that a typical observer, in the sense of a unique reasonable measure on distinct evolutions, sees any particular regularity. The 2008 job is not done. The circle is kept.

That is the report. It is not a failure of honesty to leave typicality conditional. It would be a failure to hang a costume count on $E$ or on a killed fork.

---

## 5. Open

- A specified countable set of $\sim$-classes of realized paths, with a bound that is actually proved, not chosen to make counting work.
- Whether typicality, if that bound appears, recovers anything that looks like GR or QM. Not asked here.
- $\Phi$, if later written, must respect: analytic solution curves in $\mathrm{Obs}$ cannot have thick trunks; from one $O$ the geodesic stand-in is one curvelet.
- Gauge, still open.
- Countability of $E(O)$, still open, and not a substitute.

---

## 6. Report line

- **Killed.** Delayed $\mathrm{Obs}$-branches; typicality as a count of those forks; $E$ as extra observers; $E_\gamma$ as extra curves; $M$ or $\lvert E\rvert$ as $\lvert a\rvert^2$; topology-census as the typicality set.
- **Remains, as objects.** Distinct realized paths in $\mathrm{Obs}$ (analytic geodesic lifts). From one $O$, one such path. $E_\gamma$ shares it.
- **No theorem.** No remaining typicality theorem without a new hypothesis that supplies a countable set of distinct evolutions. Unique-measure stays conditional. Circularity sentence stays.
- **Not done.** $\Phi$; Born; a hand-put bound; a replacement count to save 2008.
