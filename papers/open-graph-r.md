## 1. What is now stateable

Theorem 16 (`papers/obs-as-a-space.md`): the Fermi-slice $/O(3)$ quotient is a Hausdorff topology on $\mathrm{Obs}$. Product topology on $\mathrm{Obs}\times\mathrm{Obs}$ exists. $R\subset\mathrm{Obs}\times\mathrm{Obs}$ is then a subset of a topological space. Openness of that subset in the product, or openness of each out-star $\mathrm{succ}(O)=\{O^\prime:(O,O^\prime)\in R\}$ in the Fermi-slice topology, is a well-defined extra rule. It is not written. Do not claim the Fermi-slice topology is Polish or standard Borel. That is not locked. Open graph is extra even without it.

Closed-graph $R$ (`papers/closed-graph-r.md`, on main) is closed graph in the product, or closed out-stars. Extra and different. Dual of this note. Already named. Not reopened here as if new.

Locality (`papers/local-law-of-r.md`, on main) is a neighborhood-constraint $\mathrm{succ}(O)\subseteq U$ for some Fermi-slice open $U\ni O$. Extra and different. An open star is a neighborhood-constraint of a special kind; locality does not require the star itself to be open. Do not collapse the two. Already named. Not reopened here.

Measurability (`papers/measurable-r.md`, on main) is Borel (etc.) graph of $R$ in the product. Extra and different. Open implies Borel in a metric/Hausdorff setting if that is already locked. Still a different extra. Do not invent Polish structure. Already named. Not reopened here as if new.

Compact-valued stars (`papers/compact-stars.md`, on main) and properness (`papers/proper-r.md`, in flight on #58) are further extras. Distinct. This note does not wait on #58.

Open graph is distinct from a neighborhood-law, from a mere set of pairs, from Borel measurability, from closed graph, from compact-valued stars, and from properness.

## 2. Open-graph $R$ is not lock-side and is not a grain

Proposition 13: locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges. Theorem 19: named functors do not supply $R$. An open graph is therefore extra relative to the lock, same as the law of $R$ itself.

A nonempty open set in a Fermi-slice neighborhood is typically a continuum (Theorem 17, `papers/neighborhood-uncountable.md`). Open graph does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. It does not finite-support $R$. It does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable. Theorem 31 (countable out-degree) is a different extra.

**Not claimed.** No open-graph $R$ exists. Empty $R$ is open. Discrete $R$ with isolated points. A full product is the 2008 job. Empty $R$ is not open unless $\mathrm{Obs}$ is empty: do not claim empty is open. Discrete $R$ with isolated points would need isolated lumps, which the Fermi-slice topology does not supply (Theorem 17). Do not claim no open-graph $R$ exists: a full product is open; that map is not the 2008 job.
