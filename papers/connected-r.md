# Weak connectedness of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Totality (`papers/total-r.md`, on main via #68): every distinct pair is comparable one way or the other. That makes the underlying undirected graph complete, hence connected. Weak connectedness is a different extra: the underlying undirected graph of $(V,R)$ is connected. Weaker than totality. Distinct from no dead ends (`papers/dead-ends-and-rays.md`): seriality is local; connectedness is global. Distinct from countable out-degree (Theorem 31): a countable star from one root need not join the rest of $V$. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/total-r.md`, `papers/dead-ends-and-rays.md`, `papers/countable-outdegree.md` (Theorem 31), `papers/type-ii-clock.md` (Theorem 23(1)), `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Hitchhiker $\tau$ connects $\gamma_O$ (type (i)). That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not force $R$ to be weakly connected on $\mathrm{Obs}$. Empty $R$ is not weakly connected if $|V|>1$. A singleton edge on two points is weakly connected and is a map, not the 2008 job. Not lock-side (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Not a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$. Not adopted.

## 1. Distinct from totality and from no dead ends

**Totality.** Every distinct pair comparable. On main via #68. Implies weak connectedness. Extra-on-extra relative to this leftover. Not reopened.

**Strong connectedness.** A directed path $O\to O^\prime$ and $O^\prime\to O$ for every pair. Further extra, not this leftover.

**No dead ends.** Every lump has a successor. Extra. A disjoint union of rays is serial and not weakly connected. Not reopened.

**Weak connectedness.** The underlying undirected graph is connected. Extra. Does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. Does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable (Theorem 31 is countable out-degree). A connected $R$ on a continuum of lumps is still extra.

## 2. Not lock-side and not a grain

Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). In-world geodesics do not reverse $\tau$. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)).

Weak connectedness does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. $V$ may remain a continuum.

**Not claimed.** No weakly connected $R$ exists. Empty $R$ is not weakly connected if $|V|>1$. A singleton edge on two points is weakly connected. That map is not the 2008 job.
