# Remainder inventory

David Strayhorn

Not a new theorem. What is locked, what is killed, and what would still have to be assumed to do the 2008 typicality job on this object. Do not adopt $\Phi$, $1/N$, a grain, or a measure. Do not reopen the object. Do not rewrite Paper 1.

**Status.** Under the lock, typicality of branches from one $O$ is empty. The named leftover motion is type-(ii) links on $\mathrm{Obs}$ (2005 / Strayhorn3), not the 2007 geodesic-generated tree. Engulfing-in-$P(O)$ is not that leftover. None of this is adopted.

---

## Locked

- Elementary observer $O=(j_\infty,u)$, equivalently the unique simply-connected real-analytic patch $P(O)$. Local continuation only, not a completed $4$-manifold $W$.
- Extra topologies are the ensemble $E(O)$, not extra observers. $E$ is not folded into $\sim$.
- $\mathrm{Obs}=\mathcal{P}/{\sim_L}$ as a *set* of lumps (Theorem 10). Gauge as a *space* is still open.
- Motion stand-in: geodesic hitchhiking. One curvelet $\gamma_O$ per $O$ (Prop. 3.2). $E_\gamma$ shares it (Theorem 4). No $\sim$-split at $\partial P$ (Theorem 7). Lifetime $\tau_\partial$ may be finite or infinite (Theorem 6).
- Analytic geodesic lifts cannot delayed-fork (Theorems 2–3, 9). Typicality of branches from one $O$ is empty (Theorem 8). The 2007 across-worlds tree is not a theorem of that layer (Theorem 11).
- Hitchhiking is a restriction of 2007, not a faithful reading.

Paper 1’s ontology cut is unchanged.

---

## Killed (on this object, under hitchhiking)

- Reconstructing a unique cosmos $W$ from $O$.
- Finite $k$-jets or $C^\infty$ as the elementary object (Borel).
- Delayed forks of analytic geodesic lifts, including at $\partial P$.
- Restoring “agree then diverge” as a theorem of leaving the patch.
- Typicality over $\sim$-branches from one $O$ without a new hypothesis.
- Reading hitchhiking as 2007. Reading the 2007 geodesic-generated tree as a theorem of the geodesic-analytic layer.
- Writing a geodesic $\Phi$ to save 2008 (analytic-curve $\Phi$ cannot delayed-fork).
- Raw coordinate jets, or $\mathrm{Met}(M)/\mathrm{Diff}(M)$, as $\mathrm{Obs}$. Collapsing $\mathrm{Obs}$ into $E(O)$.
- Union-of-ensembles as Hilbert space. $|a|^2$ as $M$. $\chi$ / $4$-geon / spin-foam as this program.
- Restricted Minkowski as a finite-lifetime example.
- Engulfing-in-$P(O)$ as the leftover motion (PR #15): it coarsens $\gamma_O$, it does not supply type-(ii) edges.
- Locking a grain, $1/N$, a measure on $\mathrm{Obs}$, or $\Phi$, in the notes that named them.

## What the 2008 typicality job still needs

2008 wanted a unique measure from typicality of a countable set of distinct evolutions. On the locked object, hitchhiking does not supply those evolutions from one $O$.

To do that job *here*, one would still have to assume at least:

1. **Leftover motion (named, not adopted).** Type-(ii) links on $\mathrm{Obs}$: extra edges of a transition structure that are *not* geodesic lifts. That is the 2005 random walk / Strayhorn3 transition matrix on lumps, not the 2007 tree generated by running geodesics in many $W$ and superposing them (Theorem 11 killed that as a theorem of the in-world layer). Definition 7 is the structure; type (ii) is the content. Still not $\Phi$. Still no $1/N$ on the edges.
2. **Countability.** A grain, or some other hypothesis, that makes the set of type-(ii) paths from one $O$ a countable set one can typicalize. Grain is open. $d$-decimal is combinatorial, not physical, and not put in by hand. Engulfing-in-$P(O)$ is not this countability (it is not type (ii)).
3. **Typicality of many $O$, if wanted instead (or after).** A measure on $\mathrm{Obs}$ as a set. Gauge as a space is open, so a measure written now would still be a measure on an unidentified *space*. Not written.

Those three are assumptions, not theorems. Unique-measure stays conditional on them. Circularity of “count first, then typicality, then the measure that was used to count” is not resolved here.
