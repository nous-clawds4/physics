# Remainder inventory

David Strayhorn

Not a new theorem. What is locked, what is killed, and what would still have to be assumed to do the 2008 typicality job on this object. Do not adopt $\Phi$, $1/N$, a grain, or a measure. Do not reopen the object. Do not rewrite Paper 1.

Inventory matches Theorems 19–26 and Definition 9 on main, with Theorem 23(2b) as restated (Lorentzian distance / supremum).

**Status.** Under the lock, typicality of branches from one $O$ is empty. Type-(ii) links on $\mathrm{Obs}$ (2005 / Strayhorn3) are working across-worlds motion, abandonable; not the 2007 geodesic-generated tree. Engulfing-in-$P(O)$ is not that leftover. The law of $R$ is extra relative to the named lock-side functors (Theorem 19). Walk-count is well-defined on a DAG and infinite on a pumpable cycle (Theorems 20–21). Named non-slices fail to make path-count a probability (Theorem 22); not a closed “nothing supplies a slice.” Hitchhiker $\tau$ does not timestamp type (ii); extra $W$ is $E$-smuggling; in-patch Lorentzian distance (sup) is lock-side, not unique in $(O,O^\prime)$, not a general type-(ii) clock (Theorem 23, restated: unique given a fixed $q$; leftover non-uniqueness is only multiple occurrence). Fermi-slice distance is a continuous scale (Theorem 24). Continuous $y$ does not finite-support $R$ (Theorem 25). Combinatorial $y$ presupposes a grain or a law of $R$ plus a slice (Theorem 26). Grain, $1/N$, a measure, and $\Phi$ are not adopted. Definition 9 is named, not adopted.

## Locked

- Elementary observer $O=(j_\infty,u)$, equivalently the unique simply-connected real-analytic patch $P(O)$. Local continuation only, not a completed $4$-manifold $W$.
- Extra topologies are the ensemble $E(O)$, not extra observers. $E$ is not folded into $\sim$.
- $\mathrm{Obs}=\mathcal{P}/{\sim_L}$ as a *set* of lumps (Theorem 10). Fermi-slice $/O(3)$ is a Hausdorff topology on that set (Theorem 16); gauge as a *named* space (Gielen–Wise, a jet bundle of a $W$) stays open.
- Motion stand-in: geodesic hitchhiking. One curvelet $\gamma_O$ per $O$ (Prop. 3.2). $E_\gamma$ shares it (Theorem 4). No $\sim$-split at $\partial P$ (Theorem 7). Lifetime $\tau_\partial$ may be finite or infinite (Theorem 6).
- Analytic geodesic lifts cannot delayed-fork (Theorems 2–3, 9). Typicality of branches from one $O$ is empty (Theorem 8). The 2007 across-worlds tree is not a theorem of that layer (Theorem 11).
- Hitchhiking is a restriction of 2007, not a faithful reading.

Paper 1’s ontology cut is unchanged.

## Killed (on this object, under hitchhiking)

- Reconstructing a unique cosmos $W$ from $O$.
- Finite $k$-jets or $C^\infty$ as the elementary object (Borel).
- Delayed forks of analytic geodesic lifts, including at $\partial P$.
- Restoring “agree then diverge” as a theorem of leaving the patch.
- Typicality over $\sim$-branches from one $O$ without a new hypothesis.
- Reading hitchhiking as 2007. Reading the 2007 geodesic-generated tree as a theorem of the geodesic-analytic layer.
- Writing a geodesic $\Phi$ to save 2008 (analytic-curve $\Phi$ cannot delayed-fork).
- Raw coordinate jets, or $\mathrm{Met}(M)/\mathrm{Diff}(M)$, as $\mathrm{Obs}$. Collapsing $\mathrm{Obs}$ into $E(O)$.
- Union-of-ensembles as Hilbert space. $|a|^2$ as $M$. $\chi$ / $4$-geon / spin-foam as this program.
- Restricted Minkowski as a finite-lifetime example.
- Engulfing-in-$P(O)$ as the leftover motion (PR #15): it coarsens $\gamma_O$, it does not supply type-(ii) edges.
- Locking a grain, $1/N$, a measure on $\mathrm{Obs}$, or $\Phi$, in the notes that named them.
- Neighborhoods as a countable $N$ (Theorem 17): a Fermi-slice open ball minus $\gamma_O$ is uncountable.
- A countable grain read off finitely many continuous invariants (Theorem 18, connected opens). Rounding is a mesh, the same kind of choice as $d$. Grain is extra structure, not a theorem of the jet/patch, and is not adopted.
- Named lock-side functors as a law of $R$ (Theorem 19): listed data at $O$, open neighborhoods, continuous $f:\mathrm{Obs}\to\mathbb{R}^n$ do not supply a nonempty countable typicality set of delayed forks. Not a blanket “no map” (empty and $\{O_{\mathrm{Mink}}\}$ are maps). Not a ban on nonempty proper subsets of a neighborhood (Ricci-flat / Weyl $=0$ / Petrov walls). Discrete algebraic invariants (Petrov type, $\dim$ Killing) fail to grain; not a fourth functor in Theorem 19.
- Walk-count as a finite relative probability on a graph with a pumpable cycle (Theorem 20). Strayhorn3 both-ways, as walks, is ill-defined (Corollary 20.1). Grain or $|a|^2$ as a lock-side fix of the count.
- Named candidates as a lock-side slice (Theorem 22): hitchhiker $\tau$, Fermi balls, continuous $f$, grain. Not a closed “nothing supplies a finite slice.” Locally finite one-step out-neighborhoods are finite slices and come from a law of $R$ (extra). Unique-tree as a theorem of Definition 7. $1/N$ flow as path-count (they disagree except on special graphs).
- Extra $W$ from $E(O)$ as a lock-side clock (Theorem 23(2a)): $E$-smuggling. Hitchhiker $\tau$ as a type-(ii) timestamp (Theorem 23(1)). In-patch Lorentzian distance as a *general* type-(ii) clock, or as a unique function of $(O,O^\prime)$ when $O^\prime$ occurs more than once (Theorem 23(2b): unique given a fixed $q$). Infimum among causal curves is typically $0$, not a clock.
- Fermi-slice distance as a lock-side slice or generation (Theorem 24): a continuous scale; rounding is a grain. Not a claim that no metric exists. Theorem 18 controls *values* of $d(O,\cdot)$, not fibers; metric spheres inherit Theorem 17’s continuum except at isolated extrema.
- Continuous $y$ as a lock-side finite-support of $R$ (Theorem 25). Integer $y\in[0,Y]$ is a grain. $|a|^2$ as $y$. $L$ is not written.
- Combinatorial $y$ (edge count, Euler, out-degree) as lock-side finite-support of $R$ (Theorem 26): it presupposes a finite diagram (a grain, or a locally finite law of $R$ plus a slice). Compact Lorentzian $\chi=0$ (Poincaré–Hopf) does not select type-(ii) edges; that is not Theorem 5 (Markov no-census).

## What the 2008 typicality job still needs

2008 wanted a unique measure from typicality of a countable set of distinct evolutions. On the locked object, hitchhiking does not supply those evolutions from one $O$.

To do that job *here*, one would still have to assume at least:

1. **Leftover motion (working, abandonable).** Type-(ii) links on $\mathrm{Obs}$: extra edges of a transition structure that are *not* geodesic lifts. 2005 / Strayhorn3, not the 2007 geodesic-generated tree (Theorem 11). Definition 7 is the structure. Definition 9 (not adopted): the same pair $(V,R)$ on lumps; a *matrix* only once $V$ is discrete. The law of $R$ is extra relative to the named lock-side functors (Theorem 19). Still not $\Phi$. Still no $1/N$ on the edges.
2. **Countability.** A grain, or some other hypothesis, that makes the set of type-(ii) paths from one $O$ a nonempty countable typicality set of delayed forks. Grain is extra structure, not adopted. Neighborhoods do not count (Theorem 17). Continuous invariants do not grain (Theorem 18). Named functors of Theorem 19 do not supply that set. $d$-decimal is combinatorial, not physical, and not put in by hand. Engulfing-in-$P(O)$ is not this countability (it is not type (ii)).
3. **A well-defined count, if path-counting is the typicality rule.** Unique-tree is extra. On a DAG, walk-count $N(p,q)$ is well-defined and fusion adds walks (Theorem 21). On a cycle it is not (Theorem 20). $1/N$ flow is a different extra rule.
4. **A slice / clock.** Named non-slices fail (Theorem 22). Hitchhiker $\tau$ does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)). Extra $W$ is $E$-smuggling (2a). In-patch Lorentzian distance (sup) is lock-side when $O^\prime$ occurs in $P(O)$, not unique in $(O,O^\prime)$, not general (2b). Fermi-slice distance is a continuous scale (Theorem 24). A locally finite one-step out-neighborhood is a finite slice and comes from a law of $R$ (extra).
5. **A support filter, if least-action is the constraint.** Named, not adopted: only diagrams that extremize some $y$ appear. Continuous $y$ does not finite-support $R$ (Theorem 25). Combinatorial $y$ presupposes a grain or a law of $R$ plus a slice (Theorem 26). No $L$. Not $\Phi$.
6. **Typicality of many $O$, if wanted instead (or after).** A measure on $\mathrm{Obs}$ as a set. Fermi-slice neighborhoods exist (Theorem 16) and do not count (Theorem 17). A measure is not written.

Those are assumptions, not theorems. Unique-measure stays conditional on them. Circularity of “count first, then typicality, then the measure that was used to count” is not resolved here.
