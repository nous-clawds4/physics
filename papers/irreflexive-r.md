# Irreflexivity of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Acyclicity of $R$ (`papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`, on main) is extra for finite $N$, not for a countable walk-set (Theorem 31 allows cycles). Irreflexivity is a weaker extra: no self-loops, $(O,O)\notin R$. A cycle of length $\ge 2$ can still be irreflexive. Distinct from acyclicity, from unique-tree, from leftover (1), from a measurable selection. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`, `papers/fusion-and-path-counting.md` (Theorems 20--21), `papers/countable-outdegree.md` (Theorem 31), `papers/type-ii-clock.md` (Theorem 23), `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Hitchhiker $\tau$ does not sit along $\gamma_O$ (type (i)). That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not force $R$ to be irreflexive. Empty $R$ is irreflexive. A ray is irreflexive. They are maps and not the 2008 job. Not lock-side (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Not a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$. Not adopted.

## 1. Two extras, not one

**Acyclicity.** A DAG. Extra for $N(p,q)\in\mathbb{N}$ (Theorem 20: a pumpable cycle $\Rightarrow N=\infty$). Theorem 31 allows cycles for a countable walk-*set*. Already named (`papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`). Not reopened here.

**Irreflexivity.** $(O,O)\notin R$ for all $O$. Weaker. A 2-cycle is irreflexive and not a DAG. Pumping a 2-cycle still makes $N$ infinite (Theorem 20). Irreflexivity does not restore finite $N$.

Both-ways / symmetry of $R$ is a different extra, already killed as lock-side (Corollary 20.1). Not reopened here.

---

## 2. Not lock-side and not a grain

In-world geodesics do not reverse $\tau$ and do not sit. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not force type-(ii) $R$ to omit $(O,O)$. Chronological future in $P(O)$ does not finite-support $R$ (Theorem 30). Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19).

Irreflexivity does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. $V$ may remain a continuum. It does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable (Theorem 31 is countable out-degree). A self-loop is one walk of length $1$, not a continuum of lumps.

**Not claimed.** No irreflexive $R$ exists. Empty $R$ is irreflexive. A singleton edge $(O,O^\prime)$ with $O\neq O^\prime$ is irreflexive. They are maps and not the 2008 job.
