# Finite in-degree is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Countable in-degree (`papers/countable-indegree.md`, Theorem 33): $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$ countable. Finite in-degree is a different extra: $|\mathrm{pred}(O)|<\infty$ at every $O$. Stronger than countable. Dual of finite out-degree (`papers/finite-outdegree.md`, leftover, not this PR). Distinct from finite $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$ (`papers/finite-ancestors.md`): an infinite descending chain with in-degree $1$ has finite in-stars and infinite ancestors. Distinct from leftover (1) (`papers/proper-r.md`): backward compactness of sources that hit a compact target is not a finite in-star. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

## 1. Distinct from countable in-degree and from finite $\mathrm{Anc}$

**Countable in-degree.** Theorem 33, dual of Theorem 31. Extra. Finite is stronger. Not reopened.

**Finite $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$.** Extra for $N\in\mathbb{N}$. Finite in-stars do not imply a finite past. Not reopened.

**Finite out-degree.** Dual forward cut. Leftover, not this PR. Not reopened.

**Leftover (1).** $\{O:\mathrm{succ}(O)\cap K\neq\emptyset\}$ compact for compact $K$. Extra. Compact $\neq$ finite (Theorem 17). Not reopened.

**Finite in-degree.** Extra. Does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. Does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable (that is Theorem 31, out-degree). Does not finite-support $R$ globally.
