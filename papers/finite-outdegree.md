# Finite out-degree is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Countable out-degree (`papers/countable-outdegree.md`, Theorem 31): $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ is countable. Finite out-degree is a different extra: $|\mathrm{succ}(O)|<\infty$ at every $O$ (or every $O$ reachable from a named root). Stronger than countable. Distinct from finite $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ (`papers/finite-descendants.md`, leftover, not this PR): an infinite ray with out-degree $1$ has finite stars and infinite descendants. Distinct from compact-valued stars (`papers/compact-stars.md`): a compact set in the Fermi-slice topology is typically a continuum (Theorem 17), not a finite set. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

## 1. Distinct from countable out-degree and from finite $\mathrm{Desc}$

**Countable out-degree.** Theorem 31. Extra for countable $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$. Finite is stronger. Not reopened.

**Finite $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$.** Extra. Leftover, not this PR. Finite stars do not imply a finite future. Not reopened.

**Compact-valued stars.** Extra. Compact $\neq$ finite (Theorem 17). Not reopened.

**No dead ends / König.** Locally finite plus infinite reachable $\Rightarrow$ an infinite ray. Finite out-degree is local finiteness of *out*-stars, still extra, and with no dead ends still leaves infinitely many finite walks (prefixes of the ray). Not reopened.

**Finite out-degree.** Extra. Does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. Does not finite-support $R$ globally ($V$ may be a continuum of finite stars). Does not replace a slice (Theorem 22).

---

## 2. Not lock-side and not a grain

Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). In-world geodesics do not reverse $\tau$. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)).

Finite out-degree does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. Theorem 31(3): $V$ may remain a continuum with $|\mathrm{succ}(v)|\le 2$. Finite is the same cut, stricter cardinality.

**Not claimed.** No finite-out-degree $R$ exists. Empty $R$ has out-degree $0$. A singleton directed edge has finite out-degree. They are maps and not the 2008 job.
