## 1. What is now stateable

Theorem 16 (`papers/obs-as-a-space.md`): the Fermi-slice $/O(3)$ quotient is a Hausdorff topology on $\mathrm{Obs}$. Stars are out-neighborhoods in $(V,R)$: $\mathrm{succ}(O)=\{O^\prime:(O,O^\prime)\in R\}$. The Borel $\sigma$-algebra of the Fermi-slice topology is then stateable. A map $s:\mathrm{Obs}\to\mathrm{Obs}$ that is Borel (or universally measurable) with respect to it, and that satisfies $(O,s(O))\in R$ whenever $\mathrm{succ}(O)\neq\emptyset$, is a well-defined extra rule. It is not written.

Do not invent a Polish or standard Borel structure on $\mathrm{Obs}$. That is not locked. Name the extra without that. Whether a measurable selection is even a well-formed sentence depends on a measurable structure on $\mathrm{Obs}$; the Fermi-slice topology exists and is Hausdorff, so the Borel $\sigma$-algebra of that topology is stateable. Extra, not lock-side.

Measurability of the graph (`papers/measurable-r.md`, on main) is Borel (etc.) as a subset of $\mathrm{Obs}\times\mathrm{Obs}$. Extra and different. Already named. Not reopened here. A selection is a function, not the graph.

Closed-graph plus compact-valued plus leftover (1) (`papers/proper-r.md`, leftover (1), on main via #58) as a bundle for upper hemicontinuity / selection theorems is extra-on-extra, already named. Do not write a selection theorem as a law of $R$.

## 2. A selection is not lock-side and is not a grain

Proposition 13: locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges. Theorem 19: named functors do not supply $R$. A measurable selection is therefore extra relative to the lock, same as the law of $R$ itself. Not supplied by the jet/patch. Not by hitchhiking. Not by a Borel graph.

A continuous selection would typically give a continuum of lumps (Theorems 17--18: `papers/neighborhood-uncountable.md`, `papers/grain-not-from-invariants.md`). A measurable selection still does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. Borel images can be continua. It does not finite-support $R$. It does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable. Theorem 31 (countable out-degree) is a different extra.

**Not claimed.** Empty $R$ has no nonempty-star selection. A constant map to a singleton star is a selection. They are maps and not the 2008 job. Do not claim no measurable selection exists.
