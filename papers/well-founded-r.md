# Well-foundedness of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Acyclicity (`papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`): no finite directed cycle. Finite ancestors (`papers/finite-ancestors.md`): $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$ is a finite set. Well-foundedness is a different extra: no infinite descending chain. There is no sequence $(O_n)_{n\in\mathbb{N}}$ with $(O_{n+1},O_n)\in R$ for all $n$. Acyclicity does not imply it (the integers with $n\,R\,n{+}1$ are acyclic). Finite $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$ implies well-foundedness on walks into $q$; the converse fails (infinitely many immediate predecessors of one $q$, each $R$-minimal). Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`, `papers/finite-ancestors.md`, `papers/dead-ends-and-rays.md`, `papers/fusion-and-path-counting.md` (Theorems 20--21), `papers/type-ii-clock.md` (Theorem 23(1)), `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Hitchhiker $\tau$ is well-founded along $\gamma_O$ (type (i)). That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not force $R$ to be well-founded. Empty $R$ is well-founded. A finite DAG is well-founded. They are maps and not the 2008 job. Not lock-side (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Not a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$. Not adopted.

## 1. Distinct from acyclicity and from finite $\mathrm{Anc}$

**Acyclicity.** No finite directed cycle. Extra for finite $N$. A DAG may have an infinite descending chain. Not reopened.

**Finite $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$.** Extra for $N\in\mathbb{N}$ (`papers/finite-ancestors.md`). Stronger than well-foundedness on the backward graph at $q$. Not reopened.

**No dead ends / König.** Extra for infinite *forward* rays (`papers/dead-ends-and-rays.md`). Dual cut, not this one. Not reopened.

**Well-foundedness.** No infinite descending chain. Extra. Implies acyclicity (a cycle loops into an infinite descending walk). Does not imply finite $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$. Does not finite-support $R$. Does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable (Theorem 31 is countable out-degree).

Theorem 20: a pumpable cycle makes $N$ infinite. Well-foundedness kills *that* pump by killing every cycle, and also kills infinite backward rays. Finite $N$ still needs more (finite $\mathrm{Anc}$, or a slice, or finite depth). All extra.

## 2. Not lock-side and not a grain

Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). In-world geodesics do not reverse $\tau$. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)).

Well-foundedness does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. $V$ may remain a continuum. A well-founded $R$ on a continuum of lumps is still extra, not a countable grain.

**Not claimed.** No well-founded $R$ exists. Empty $R$ is well-founded. A finite DAG is well-founded. They are maps and not the 2008 job.
