# Asymmetry of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Irreflexivity (`papers/irreflexive-r.md`, on main via #64): no self-loops. Transitivity (`papers/transitive-r.md`, on main via #66): shortcuts. Asymmetry is a different extra: $(O,O^\prime)\in R$ implies $(O^\prime,O)\notin R$. That forbids 2-cycles and implies irreflexivity. Irreflexivity does not: a 2-cycle is irreflexive and symmetric. Distinct from both. Both-ways as lock-side was already killed (Corollary 20.1); this is the extra of *forbidding* both-ways, not a claim that the lock supplies it. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/irreflexive-r.md`, `papers/transitive-r.md`, `papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`, `papers/fusion-and-path-counting.md` (Theorems 20--21, Corollary 20.1), `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Hitchhiker $\tau$ is asymmetric along $\gamma_O$ (type (i)). That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not force $R$ to be asymmetric. Empty $R$ is asymmetric. A singleton directed edge is asymmetric. They are maps and not the 2008 job. Not lock-side (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Not a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$. Not adopted.

## 1. Three extras, not one

**Irreflexivity.** No $(O,O)$. On main via #64. A 2-cycle is irreflexive. Not reopened.

**Transitivity.** Shortcuts. On main via #66. A symmetric pair can be transitive (complete on two points). Not reopened.

**Asymmetry.** No 2-cycle. Implies irreflexivity. Extra. A strict partial order is irreflexive + transitive + asymmetric (the third follows from the first two on a transitive relation); putting all three in is extra-on-extra, not adopted here.

Theorem 20: a pumpable 2-cycle makes $N$ infinite. Asymmetry kills *that* pump, not every cycle (a 3-cycle can be asymmetric). Acyclicity is still a different extra for finite $N$.

Corollary 20.1 killed both-ways as lock-side. This note does not revive it. Asymmetry is extra structure on $R$, not a theorem of the jet/patch.

## 2. Not lock-side and not a grain

Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). In-world geodesics do not reverse $\tau$. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)).

Asymmetry does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. $V$ may remain a continuum. It does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable (Theorem 31 is countable out-degree). It does not finite-support $R$.

**Not claimed.** No asymmetric $R$ exists. Empty $R$ is asymmetric. A singleton directed edge is asymmetric. They are maps and not the 2008 job.
