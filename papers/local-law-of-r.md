# Locality of $R$ in the Fermi-slice topology is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. After Theorems 31–32: 2005 / Strayhorn3 wanted the next step determined by the local structure of the tree in an infinitesimal ball around $p$. That locality is now *stateable* (Theorem 16: Fermi-slice topology). It does not make stars countable (Theorem 17). Extra. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/law-of-r.md`, `papers/obs-as-a-space.md`, `papers/neighborhood-uncountable.md`, `papers/countable-outdegree.md`, `papers/uniform-law-of-r.md`.

**Status.** A neighborhood-constraint $\mathrm{succ}(O)\subseteq U$ for some Fermi-slice open $U\ni O$ is extra. It does not imply countable out-degree (Theorem 31’s hypothesis). Locality of the *rule* is a different extra from uniformity (`papers/uniform-law-of-r.md`) and from branching (Theorem 32). Named, not adopted.

---

## 1. What is now stateable

Proposition 13 blocked a neighborhood-law until $\mathrm{Obs}$ was a space. Theorem 16: Fermi-slice $/O(3)$ descends. Gauge as a *named* home (Gielen–Wise, a jet bundle of a $W$) stays open. A Fermi-slice neighborhood-constraint is nevertheless a well-defined extra rule.

Strayhorn3: evolution determined by the local structure of the tree in an infinitesimal ball around $p$. That is this candidate.

---

## 2. Locality does not replace Theorem 31

**Not a theorem number.** Already Theorem 17: $U\setminus\gamma_O$ is uncountable. The constraint $\mathrm{succ}(O)\subseteq U$ still allows a continuum of successors. Countable out-degree remains extra (Theorem 31). Finite out-degree remains extra (Theorem 22).

Uniformity (one rule at every lump) plus locality (stars inside a ball) is still extra twice. Neither grains $\mathrm{Obs}$.

**Not claimed.** No local law exists. Empty $R$ is local. $\{O_{\mathrm{Mink}}\}$ is local if that lump lies in $U$, and is a map.

Fermi-slice locality of stars. Extra. Not lock-side. Not a grain. Not adopted. Does not supply countable out-degree or branching.
