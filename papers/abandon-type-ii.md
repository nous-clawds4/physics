# What abandoning type-(ii) would leave

David Strayhorn

Name the remainder of the ontology if type-(ii) links are dropped. Layer (i) plus $E(O)$ only. Do not abandon here unless this is a clean kill. It is not: type (ii) is consistent with the lock; it is extra. No $\Phi$, no $|a|^2$, no $L$, no $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/type-ii-adopted.md`, `papers/original-two-layer.md`, `papers/extra-structure.md`, `papers/ensemble-labels.md`.

**Status.** Packaging of Theorems 4, 7, 8, and 11: if $R$ contains only geodesic-lift pairs, typicality of delayed forks from one $O$ is empty, and $E(O)$ does not restore it. Unique-measure from that typicality is then unavailable. Named leftover: hitchhiking plus ensemble labels. Not a kill of type (ii). Not adopted as abandonment.

---

## 1. What would remain

**Layer (i).** Geodesic hitchhiking on the locked object. One curvelet $\gamma_O$ from one $O$ (Prop. 3.2). Analytic lifts cannot delayed-fork (Theorems 2–3, 7, 9). Typicality of $\sim$-distinct hitchhiking evolutions from one $O$ has cardinality one (Theorem 8). The 2007 across-worlds tree is not a theorem of this layer (Theorem 11).

**$E(O)$.** Pointed analytic occurrences of the germ, not extra observers and not extra curves. $E_\gamma$ members share $\gamma_O$ in $\mathrm{int}\,P(O)$ (Theorem 4). No $\sim$-split at $\partial P$ among those members (Theorem 7). Countability of $E(O)$ is open (Theorem 5, Markov). Ensemble labels are not delayed forks.

That is the lock plus hitchhiking, with type (ii) off. Delayed-fork typicality from one $O$ is empty. A measure on many $O$ (item 5 of `papers/extra-structure.md`) is still extra, and is a different job.

---

## 2. Not a clean kill

Type-(ii) edges are extra structure, not a contradiction. The lock does not forbid them (Proposition 13: locked data do not determine them; it does not say they cannot exist). Abandoning them is a choice to drop leftover motion. It does not become mandatory because the support well is dry (Theorem 30): that well was a search for a *lock-side* law of $R$, not a proof that $R$ is empty.

**Killed as a reading.** “Layer (i) plus $E(O)$ already does 2008.” It does not. Theorem 8. “$E(O)$ is a Hilbert space of branches.” Union-of-ensembles is not Hilbert space.

**Not killed.** Type (ii) as working, abandonable motion. The option to abandon later.

---

## 3. Named leftover, not adopted

Abandon type (ii): named as an option. Not taken here. What would remain is layer (i) plus $E(O)$: one curvelet, ensemble labels, empty delayed-fork typicality from one $O$. Unique-measure from that typicality unavailable. A measure on $\mathrm{Obs}$ still extra.

---

## 4. Report line

- **Packaged.** Theorems 4, 7, 8, 11: without type (ii), delayed-fork typicality from one $O$ is empty; $E(O)$ does not restore it.
- **Named, not adopted.** Abandonment of type (ii).
- **Not a clean kill** of type (ii). Extra, not contradictory.
- **Killed.** Layer (i)+$E(O)$ as a 2008 typicality theorem. Union-of-ensembles as Hilbert space.
- **Open.** Whether to abandon; the law of $R$ if not.
