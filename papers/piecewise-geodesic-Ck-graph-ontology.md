## 2. Related opens (§5) — proposed answers

### 2.1 Law of $V^*$ / “edge of at least one”

**Propose (working, not forced):** $V^*$ is a **chosen countable (or finite) subset** of oriented germs that appear as patch-edge events for ensemble members under a *named selection rule still to be written*. Until that rule exists, treat “edge of at least one” as a **candidate pool**, not as $V^*$.

**Do not claim:** that the raw pool is already a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$, or that locked data at $O$ force a unique $V^*$ (Prop. 13 pattern).

**Continuum risk:** name it. Sibling anchors, continuum ensemble labels, and weak incompleteness nominations overgenerate. Selection must be extra and abandonable — same honesty as public grain-not-locked.

### 2.2 Law of $E^*$

**Propose:** Same Prop. 13 cost as public type-(ii) $R$. Allowed edges are piecewise analytic-geodesic segments between vertices of $V^*$, each labelled by a hosting $W$ for that piece. **Which** segments exist is not determined by the germ alone. No $1/N$ on edges.

### 2.3 Fixed $k$ stance

**Propose:** Work at a **fixed finite $k\ge 1$** (include first derivative / velocity matching of $u$ and $j^1 g$ at least). Prefer stating $k$ as a package parameter, not as physics derived from the jet.

- Do **not** take $k\to\infty$ as harmless (deferred; may recover the HOLE).
- Do **not** treat larger $k$ as “closer to truth” without a separate argument.
- $k=0$ (value matching only) is allowed as a weaker package but is a poorer fit for “orientation does not jump” rhetoric; Ontology prefers $k\ge 1$ as the default proposal.

### 2.4 $B$ equivalence

**Propose primary:** **graph out-star** — arms are distinct outgoing edges of $E^*$ at $O^{u*}$.

**Secondary (optional, for later):** refine within an edge by $j^{\le k}$ data only if a controlled continuum appears inside one combinatorial edge (should not, if edges are discrete). Do **not** use infinite-order path-jet equivalence while joins are only $C^k$.

### 2.5 Cross-world identification at the vertex

**Propose:** At $O^{u*}$, identify incoming/outgoing truncated jets and $u$ using the **germ’s pointed analytic isometries / isotropy** (public v0.3 already flags isotropy). The shared object is the oriented lump, not a glued non-analytic spacetime.

- Ensemble members that realize the same vertex remain occurrences of the **same** $O^{u*}$ (relative ensemble), not new vertices.
- Do **not** invent a new germ by $C^k$ metric glue across a hypersurface (Prop. 7.3).

### 2.6 Relation to public v0.3 $R$

**Propose:** This hybrid package is a **refinement / specialization** of the public type-(ii) launching pad, not an immediate replacement of `v0.3-prose.md`.

- Public $R$: abstract graph edges (abandonable).
- Hybrid: edges that are geodesic-labelled analytic pieces with finite-$k$ joins and a discrete skeleton $V^*$.
- Keep v0.3 as the public statement until a controlled discrete $|B|\ge 2$ example exists under an explicit $(V^*,E^*)$. Then consider promoting the hybrid into a public v0.4 outline.
- Prop. 13, no $1/N$, grain-not-locked, abandonable: unchanged.
