# A finite descendant set is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Finite ancestors (`papers/finite-ancestors.md`): $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$ is a finite set, extra for $N\in\mathbb{N}$. Converse well-foundedness (`papers/converse-well-founded-r.md`, on main via #73): no infinite ascending chain. A finite descendant set is a different extra: $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ is finite, where $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ is the vertices reachable from $p$ by a finite walk, including $p$. Finite $\mathrm{Anc}$ looks backward. This looks forward. Countable out-degree makes $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ countable (Theorem 31), not finite. Finite $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ implies no infinite *injective* ascending chain from $p$ (only finitely many vertices reachable). It does not imply converse well-foundedness if cycles are allowed: a 2-cycle has finite $\mathrm{Desc}$ and an infinite ascending walk. Converse well-foundedness still needs acyclicity, already a different extra. Converse well-foundedness does not imply finite $\mathrm{Desc}$ (infinitely many immediate successors of one $p$, each $R$-maximal). Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/finite-ancestors.md`, `papers/converse-well-founded-r.md`, `papers/well-founded-r.md`, `papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`, `papers/countable-outdegree.md` (Theorem 31), `papers/dead-ends-and-rays.md`, `papers/type-ii-clock.md` (Theorem 23(1)), `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Hitchhiker $\tau$ along $\gamma_O$ has infinite $\mathrm{Desc}$ in the type-(i) parameter if the geodesic continues. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not force $\mathrm{Desc}(O)$ finite in $R$. Empty $R$ has $\mathrm{Desc}(p)=\{p\}$. A finite DAG has finite $\mathrm{Desc}$. They are maps and not the 2008 job. Not lock-side (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Not a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$: $V\setminus\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ may remain a continuum. Not adopted.

## 1. Distinct from finite $\mathrm{Anc}$ and from converse well-foundedness

**Finite $\mathrm{Anc}(q)$.** Backward. Extra for $N\in\mathbb{N}$. On main. Not reopened.

**Converse well-foundedness.** No infinite ascending chain. On main via #73. Finite $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ implies no infinite *injective* ascending chain from $p$. It does not imply converse well-foundedness: a 2-cycle has finite $\mathrm{Desc}$ and an infinite (repeating) ascending walk. Converse well-foundedness still needs acyclicity (`papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`), already a different extra. The converse fails even on DAGs: infinitely many immediate successors of one $p$, each $R$-maximal. Not reopened.

**Well-foundedness.** Dual descending cut. On main via #71. Not reopened.

**Acyclicity.** Extra for finite $N$. Not implied by finite $\mathrm{Desc}$. Not reopened.

**Countable out-degree.** Theorem 31: $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable, $\mathrm{Desc}(O_0)$ countable. Not finite. Not reopened.

**No dead ends.** Seriality. Extra. Serial plus finite $\mathrm{Desc}$ forces a cycle. That bundle is extra-on-extra, not this leftover.

**Finite $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$.** Extra. Does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. Does not make $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ countable by itself (Theorem 31 is still countable out-degree). A finite future from $p$ is a restriction on $R$, not a slice (Theorem 22).

## 2. Not lock-side and not a grain

Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). In-world geodesics do not reverse $\tau$. That does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)).

Finite $\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. $V\setminus\mathrm{Desc}(p)$ may remain a continuum.

**Not claimed.** No finite $\mathrm{Desc}$ exists. Empty $R$ has $\mathrm{Desc}(p)=\{p\}$. A finite DAG is a map. They are not the 2008 job.
