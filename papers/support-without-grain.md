# Named leftover support constraints do not finite-support $R$ without a grain

David Strayhorn

After Theorems 19 and 25–29: what else could lock-side-constrain the support of type-(ii) $R$ without a grain? Chronological future in $P(O)$, Einstein/vacuum, energy conditions. Prove or kill. If the well is dry, name that. Do not adopt. No $\Phi$, no $|a|^2$, no $L$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/law-of-r.md`, `papers/countable-in-patch.md`, `papers/in-patch-support.md`, `papers/least-action-support.md`.

**Status.** Theorem 30: the named leftover lock-side support constraints — $I^+(p)$ (or $J^+(p)$) in $P(O)$, Ricci-flat / Einstein, energy conditions on the $2$-jet — each fail to supply a nonempty finite typicality set of delayed forks without a grain. They are extra, a continuum after $q\mapsto O_q$, or not shown discrete. Named remainder: as of this inventory the well of lock-side support constraints without a grain is dry. Not a closed “no constraint exists.” Empty and $\{O_{\mathrm{Mink}}\}$ remain maps.

---

## 1. Theorem 30. Leftover named support constraints

Let $\varphi:P(O)\to\mathrm{Obs}$ be $q\mapsto O_q$ with Fermi-transported $u$, as in Theorem 28 §0. $S(O)=\varphi(P(O))\setminus\gamma_O$.

**Theorem 30.** None of the following, from the locked object, supplies a nonempty finite typicality set of delayed forks as the support of $R$ at $O$, without a grain:

1. *Chronological (or causal) future in the patch.* Targets restricted to $\varphi\bigl(I^+(p)\cap P(O)\bigr)\setminus\gamma_O$, or to $J^+(p)$.
2. *Einstein / vacuum.* Targets restricted to Ricci-flat germs, or to germs satisfying $G=8\pi T$ for some named $T$.
3. *Energy conditions.* Targets restricted to germs whose $2$-jet satisfies the weak, null, or dominant energy condition.

**Proof.** (1) $I^+(p)\cap P(O)$ is lock-side. It is open in the $4$-manifold (when nonempty). After $\varphi$: if $\varphi$ is constant, $S$ may be empty (Theorem 28 §0). If not, the image is one class or a continuum (Theorems 27–28). Not a finite typicality set. $J^+(p)$ is larger, same alternative.

(2) Ricci-flatness is a closed condition on the $2$-jet. It is not a named functor of Theorem 19 (the third disjunct — canonical nonempty proper subset of a neighborhood — was dropped; Ricci-flat / Weyl $=0$ / Petrov walls are not a ban). The set of real-analytic Ricci-flat germs near a given vacuum germ is not shown to be finite; one should not assume it discrete (Theorem 17, vacuum paragraph). A further cut would be a grain. Einstein with a named $T$ is extra data unless $T$ is written from the lock (it is not).

(3) Energy conditions are conditions on the $2$-jet. Where they hold on a nonempty open of jets they supply a continuum of lumps in $\mathrm{Obs}$ (Theorem 17), not a finite $N$. Restricted to $S(O)$, same alternative as (1). They do not grain.

$\square$

**Not claimed.** No support constraint on $R$ exists. The empty law and the constant law $\{O_{\mathrm{Mink}}\}$ are constraints. A grain, or a law of $R$ plus a slice, would finite-support. Neighborhoods as a *further* constraint (targets in $U$, plus a discrete cut) were already named and not adopted (Theorem 17).

**Killed.** $I^+$ as a finite support. Vacuum/Einstein as a lock-side finite $N$. Energy conditions as a grain. $|a|^2$. $L$ unwritten.

## 2. Named remainder: the well is dry

Lock-side support constraints on $R$ without a grain, as inventoried:

- data at $O$, open neighborhoods, continuous $f$ (Theorem 19);
- continuous $y$ (Theorem 25), combinatorial $y$ (Theorem 26);
- $\mathrm{Occ}(O)\setminus\gamma_O$ (Theorem 27), named subsets of it (Theorem 28), Morse / conjugate / cut (Theorem 29);
- $I^+$ / $J^+$, Einstein/vacuum, energy conditions (Theorem 30).

Each is extra, a continuum (or empty/singleton), or not shown discrete. No further lock-side support constraint without a grain is identified here. The law of $R$ remains extra relative to the named functors. This is an inventory, not a closed “nothing constrains support.”

---

## 3. Report line

- **Proved.** Theorem 30: chronological future in $P(O)$, Einstein/vacuum, and energy conditions do not finite-support $R$ without a grain.
- **Named remainder.** The well of lock-side support constraints without a grain is dry as of this inventory. Law of $R$ extra.
- **Not claimed.** No map. No constraint exists.
- **Killed.** Those three as lock-side finite $N$. $|a|^2$. $L$.
- **Open.** The law of $R$; whether to put in a grain.
