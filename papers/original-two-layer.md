# Original two-layer dynamics vs the lock

David Strayhorn

What 2005–2007 actually wrote, against what the lock has proved. $\Phi$ is not written. $L$ is not written. $1/N$ is not adopted. Born is not derived. The jet/patch object is not reopened. The phrase “hitchhiking” does not occur in the corpus; geodesic hitchhiking is a later gloss.

Corpus, as filed: `old-manuscripts/2007-10-20-OutcomeCounting.pdf`, `old-manuscripts/Strayhorn3.pdf`, `old-manuscripts/ActionPrinciple.pdf`, `old-manuscripts/2005-01-05-RHF-I.pdf` (and II, III), `old-manuscripts/200704-AFLB2.pdf`.

**Status.** 2007 has two layers: in-world geodesic motion, and an across-worlds Everettian tree with $1/N$. The lock’s geodesic-analytic stand-in is a *restriction* of the first layer, not a faithful reading of 2007. OutcomeCounting footnote 3 wanted delayed forks; Theorems 2–8 kill those forks as a theorem of the restricted layer. The across-worlds tree (and the 2005 random walk on observer-state space) is the remaining motion hypothesis that matches the corpus. It is named, not adopted.

---

## 0. What this note is not

It is not an equation of motion. It is not a Lagrangian. It is not a revival of $\chi$, spin-foam, or a $4$-geon. It does not treat union-of-ensembles as Hilbert space. It does not fold $E$ into $\sim$.

---

## 1. Two layers, as written (2007)

OutcomeCounting, §2–§3 and the closing procedure:

- An elementary observer $O$ is an analytic jet of $g$ at a point, plus a future unit $u$ (fn. 2). That is the ancestor of the locked object.
- Given $O$, a collection $M_O$ of worlds is determined by $O$ (analytic continuation through “all possible $4$-topologies”). Nothing other than $O$ is required to specify $M_O$.
- **In a world.** Evolution of $O$ inside one spacetime is geodesic motion, written as the parallel-transport / geodesic equation of GR (eq. (1) there).
- **Across worlds.** For each element of $M_O$ there is a distinct evolution $g_{ij}(\tau)$. Drawn together they are “the Everettian tree.” For any $O$ there is a unique such tree rooted at that point. Probability is outcome counting: $1/N$ at an $N$-way branch (Fig. 1).
- Procedure: $O_1$ $\to$ $M_{O_1}$ $\to$ geodesics $\to$ tree $\to$ count. Evolution $O_1\to O_2$ has nonzero probability iff at least one geodesic connects them.

Footnote 3 is explicit about delayed forks:

> Typically, any two evolutions $g_{ij}'(\tau)$ and $g_{ij}''(\tau)$ may be identical up to some time $\tau_b$, \ldots but nonidentical after.

That is “agree on a finite interval, then diverge,” written as a typical feature of the tree, not as an accident.

The word “hitchhiking” does not appear.

## 2. Hitchhiking is a restriction of 2007, not a faithful reading

The lock’s motion stand-in is geodesic hitchhiking: read the germ along a geodesic of a real-analytic $W$. That uses the *in-world* sentence of OutcomeCounting §3 (geodesic in a given spacetime). It does not use the across-worlds tree, $1/N$, or $M_O$ as extra $\sim$-classes.

It is not even a faithful reading of the in-world layer as 2007 used it. 2007 ran the geodesic equation *once per world* and then superimposed those histories as delayed forks (fn. 3). Hitchhiking, under Theorems 2–3 and Prop. 3.2, is one analytic curvelet $\gamma_O$ from one $O$, not a star. Footnote 3’s delayed forks are exactly what Theorem 7 kills for analytic lifts, including at $\partial P$.

So: hitchhiking $\subsetneq$ 2007 in-world-as-used $\subsetneq$ 2007 (both layers). Treating hitchhiking as “what 2007 said” is a misread.

## 3. Theorem 11. The 2007 across-worlds tree is not a theorem of the geodesic-analytic layer

**Theorem 11.** Assume the locked object and geodesic hitchhiking in real-analytic $W$ (the restricted in-world layer). Then the 2007 across-worlds tree of $\sim$-distinct delayed forks from one $O$, with outcome counting $1/N$ on that tree, is not a theorem of that layer.

**Proof.** Prop. 3.2: one curvelet $\gamma_O$ from one $O$, not a star. Theorem 3: each geodesic lift is an analytic curve in $\mathrm{Obs}$. Theorem 2: two analytic curves that share a positive-length prefix coincide. Theorem 4: $E_\gamma$ members share $\gamma_O$ in $\mathrm{int}\,P(O)$; they are labels, not extra curves. Theorem 7: no $\sim$-split of next germs among those members at $\partial P$. Theorem 8: the set of $\sim$-distinct hitchhiking evolutions from one $O$ has cardinality one, so typicality of branches from one $O$ is empty. OutcomeCounting fn. 3 requires distinct evolutions that agree on $[0,\tau_b]$ and disagree after. That set is empty in this layer. There is then no $N$ for $1/N$. $\square$

The in-world geodesic sentence survives as the stand-in (restricted). The across-worlds tree does not survive as a *theorem of that sentence*. EKT 1991 remains global non-uniqueness of a different kind, not fn. 3.

## 4. Strayhorn3: two geodesic sentences, not one

Strayhorn3 discretizes observer-state space (first $d$ decimal places of each jet component), draws directed links, and calls the rooted collection of paths the *transition matrix*. Outcome counting on that matrix is the probability rule. Then:

> In the limit as $d$ approaches infinity, state space becomes continuous, and each path becomes a geodesic.

The geodesic equation written next is the same display as OutcomeCounting eq. (1). The corpus runs two identifications together:

1. In-world: parallel transport of a point along a geodesic *of a spacetime $W$*.
2. Continuum limit of the transition matrix: paths in *observer-state space* become geodesics of that space.

Those are not the same sentence. Hitchhiking is (1), restricted. The $d\to\infty$ claim is (2): a continuum limit of the across-worlds / state-space tree. It is not a derivation of (1) from (2), and it is not adopted here as $\Phi$.

## 5. Least action is a tree pattern, not $\Phi$

ActionPrinciple.pdf does not write $L$. Exact $S$ is deferred: the paper is a heuristic that *patterns* in a state-transition diagram can extremize an observable $y$ (Observation 1, Figs. 4–5; $k/y/x$ as records). A specific action principle “is not provided in this paper, but is the topic of future work.” Minimization is claimed as a *consequence* of outcome counting on those patterns, not as an independent law on $\mathrm{Obs}$.

Do not write $L$. Do not take a tree-pattern for least action as $\Phi$.

---

## 6. 2005 RHF is a different object

RHF (2005): the observer is a bounded $3$-manifold; dynamics on $O$-state space is a random walk / tree current, with $1/N$ at branch points. It is not a geodesic of a point in a space of worlds, and it is not the locked jet/patch.

The locked object remains $O=(j_\infty,u)\equiv P(O)$. 2005 $3$-manifold observers are not restored. The random walk is the same *across-worlds* motion hypothesis as the 2007 tree, on a different object. Naming it does not reopen the object.

AFLB2’s spin-foam $D_\ell$ is a third, separate model. It is not $\Phi$ and is not this note.

---

## 7. Named, not adopted

**Remaining motion hypothesis that matches the corpus.** An across-worlds law on observer-state space: the 2007 Everettian tree with $1/N$, equivalently the 2005 random walk / tree current, equivalently Strayhorn3’s transition matrix (with or without a $d\to\infty$ slogan). This is the layer Theorems 2–8 do not supply. It is the historical identity of the “remaining motion revision” of `papers/phi-straits.md` (a non-analytic, or not-only-geodesic, law that could restore delayed forks).

Do not adopt it. Do not put $1/N$ in as $M$. Do not put $|a|^2$ in as $M$. Do not derive Born from it.

## 9. Report line

- **Recorded.** 2007 two layers: in-world geodesic, across-worlds $1/N$ tree. Hitchhiking is a later gloss and a restriction of 2007, not a faithful reading. Fn. 3 wanted delayed forks. Least action is a tree pattern; $L$ was never written. 2005 observers are $3$-manifolds; not the locked object. Strayhorn3 $d\to\infty$ is the tree’s continuum slogan, not the in-$W$ parallel-transport sentence.
- **Proved.** Theorem 11: the 2007 across-worlds tree is not a theorem of the locked geodesic-analytic layer (Theorems 2–8).
- **Named, not adopted.** Across-worlds tree / 2005 random walk / transition matrix, as the remaining motion hypothesis that matches the corpus.
- **Killed.** Reading hitchhiking as 2007. Reading fn. 3 delayed forks as theorems of analytic geodesic lifts. Writing $L$ or $1/N$ as locked.
- **Open.** Whether to adopt that remaining hypothesis. $\Phi$; $L$; Born.
