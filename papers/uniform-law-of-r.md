# Uniformity of the law of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. After Theorem 19 and the dry support well (Theorem 30): is $R$ one relation on $\mathrm{Obs}$, or a field of stars $R_O$? Name uniformity. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. Do not adopt a circularity resolver. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/law-of-r.md`, `papers/transition-matrix-on-lumps.md`, `papers/type-ii-adopted.md`.

**Status.** A *uniform* law (one $R\subseteq\mathrm{Obs}\times\mathrm{Obs}$ at every lump) is the 2005 / Strayhorn3 matrix shape. A *field* of laws (a possibly different out-star at each $O$) is also extra. Neither is lock-side (Theorem 19). Uniformity does not grain: a uniform rule on a continuum still has uncountable stars unless a further discrete cut. Named, not adopted.

---

## 1. Two shapes of extra structure

Definition 7 already: a transition structure is a pair $(V,R)$ with $V\subseteq\mathrm{Obs}$ and $R$ a set of ordered pairs. That *can* be read as one relation (uniform). It can also be read as the bundle of out-stars $O\mapsto\{O':(O,O')\in R\}$, which may vary with $O$ (a field). The lock does not pick.

2005’s transition matrix is a uniform law *after* $V$ is discrete (Definition 9). Uniformity is still extra on the locked continuum.

**Not claimed.** No law of $R$ exists. Empty and $\{O_{\mathrm{Mink}}\}$ are uniform laws; they are not the 2008 job.

---

## 2. Uniformity does not replace a grain

Suppose $R$ is uniform and determined by some named invariant of the germ (curvature scalars, Petrov type, Fermi-slice distance). Theorems 18, 19, 24, 28–30 already: those functors give one class or a continuum, or a mesh. Uniformity of the *rule* does not make the *stars* countable.

A locally finite uniform $R$ is a law that already includes a discrete cut (Theorem 22). That cut is extra.

---

## 3. Named leftover, not adopted

Uniform law of $R$ (one relation). Field of laws (star depending on $O$). Both extra. Neither adopted. Grain still extra. Circularity resolver not adopted.
