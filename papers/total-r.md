# Totality of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Irreflexivity (`papers/irreflexive-r.md`, on main): no self-loops. Transitivity (`papers/transitive-r.md`, on main): shortcuts. Asymmetry (`papers/asymmetric-r.md`, leftover, not this PR): no 2-cycles. Totality is a different extra: for all distinct $O,O^\prime$, at least one of $(O,O^\prime)$ and $(O^\prime,O)$ lies in $R$. A tournament is asymmetric plus total; that bundle is extra-on-extra, not adopted here. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/irreflexive-r.md`, `papers/transitive-r.md`, `papers/asymmetric-r.md`, `papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`, `papers/countable-outdegree.md` (Theorem 31), `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Totality on a continuum of lumps forces uncountable out-degree at every $O$ with $\mathrm{Obs}\setminus\{O\}$ a continuum of comparabilities (Theorem 17: Fermi-slice neighborhoods are continua). Theorem 31: uncountable out-degree at the root $\Rightarrow$ uncountable one-step evolutions. So totality fights countable $\mathrm{Path}$ unless a grain cuts $V$ first. That is not a reason to invent a grain. Empty $R$ is not total ($|\mathrm{Obs}|>1$). A tournament on two points is total and is a map, not the 2008 job. Not lock-side (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Not adopted.

## 1. Distinct from the order extras

**Irreflexivity / transitivity / acyclicity.** Already named. Totality does not imply any of them (a complete symmetric pair is total, reflexive, and has $N=\infty$).

**Asymmetry.** Forbids both-ways. Extra, in flight as its own leftover. Totality plus asymmetry is a tournament. Extra-on-extra. Not adopted here.

**A linear order** would be total + transitive + asymmetric (or total + strict partial order). Further extra-on-extra. Not adopted.

---

## 2. Not lock-side, not a grain, and in tension with countable Path

Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Hitchhiker $\tau$ linearly orders $\gamma_O$ (type (i)); that does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not totalize $R$ on $\mathrm{Obs}$.

Totality does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. If $V$ is a continuum and $R$ is total, each star meets a continuum of lumps. That is the opposite of a grain. Theorem 31 then makes one-step evolutions from such an $O$ uncountable. Countable $\mathrm{Path}(O_0)$ still needs countable out-degree, hence a grain or a different extra on $R$. Totality is not that extra. Do not invent a grain to save totality.

**Not claimed.** No total $R$ exists. A tournament on two points is total. That map is not the 2008 job. Empty $R$ is not total.
