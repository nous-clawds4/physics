# Totality of $R$ is extra

David Strayhorn

Live hole is the law of $R$, not a grain. Irreflexivity (`papers/irreflexive-r.md`, on main via #64): no self-loops. Transitivity (`papers/transitive-r.md`, on main via #66): shortcuts. Asymmetry (`papers/asymmetric-r.md`, on main via #67): no 2-cycles. Totality is a different extra: for all distinct $O,O^\prime$, at least one of $(O,O^\prime)$ and $(O^\prime,O)$ lies in $R$. Equivalently, $\mathrm{pred}(O)\cup\mathrm{succ}(O)=\mathrm{Obs}\setminus\{O\}$. A tournament is asymmetric plus total; that bundle is extra-on-extra, not adopted here. A linear order is further extra-on-extra. Do not adopt. Do not invent a grain. Not $\Phi$. No $|a|^2$. No $L$. No $1/N$. Paper 1 not rewritten.

Companions: `papers/irreflexive-r.md`, `papers/transitive-r.md`, `papers/asymmetric-r.md`, `papers/acyclicity-of-r.md`, `papers/countable-outdegree.md` (Theorem 31), `papers/countable-indegree.md` (Theorem 33), `papers/type-ii-clock.md` (Theorem 23(1)), `papers/extra-structure.md`.

**Status.** Named leftover. Hitchhiker $\tau$ does not totalize $R$ (Theorem 23(1)). Empty $R$ is not total if $|\mathrm{Obs}|>1$. A tournament on two points is a map, not the 2008 job. Totality does not force uncountable out-degree: if $\mathrm{Obs}\setminus\{O\}$ is a continuum, at least one of $\mathrm{pred}(O)$ and $\mathrm{succ}(O)$ is a continuum, not necessarily $\mathrm{succ}$. A reverse well-order on $\omega_1$ is total, asymmetric, and has countable out-degree at every vertex. Totality does not fight Theorem 31 by itself. Totality plus countable out-degree plus countable in-degree would force $V$ countable; that bundle is extra-on-extra, not totality. Not a grain. Do not invent a grain to save a total $R$ with countable stars. Not lock-side (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Not adopted.

## 1. Distinct from the order extras

**Irreflexivity / transitivity / acyclicity.** Already named. Totality does not imply any of them (a complete symmetric pair is total, reflexive, and has $N=\infty$).

**Asymmetry.** Forbids both-ways. On main via #67. Totality plus asymmetry is a tournament. Extra-on-extra. Not adopted here.

**A linear order** would be total + transitive + asymmetric (or total + strict partial order). Further extra-on-extra. Not adopted.

---

## 2. Not lock-side, not a grain, and not a fight with Theorem 31

Locked data at $O$ do not determine type-(ii) edges (Proposition 13, Theorem 19). Hitchhiker $\tau$ linearly orders $\gamma_O$ (type (i)); that does not timestamp type (ii) (Theorem 23(1)) and does not totalize $R$ on $\mathrm{Obs}$.

Totality does not grain $\mathrm{Obs}$. $V$ may remain a continuum. Comparability under an extra $R$ is not a Fermi-slice neighborhood; Theorem 17 (open neighborhoods uncountable) is not a chart that totality fills.

If $\mathrm{Obs}\setminus\{O\}$ is a continuum, $\mathrm{pred}(O)\cup\mathrm{succ}(O)$ is a continuum, so at least one of $\mathrm{pred}$, $\mathrm{succ}$ is a continuum. That need not be $\mathrm{succ}$. Reverse well-order on $\omega_1$: total, asymmetric, countable out-degree at every vertex. Totality does not force uncountable out-degree and does not fight Theorem 31 by itself. Tension with countable $\mathrm{Path}$ is only after a further extra that puts a continuum into $\mathrm{succ}$, not $\mathrm{pred}$. Totality + countable out-degree + countable in-degree would force $V$ countable; that bundle is extra-on-extra, not totality. Do not invent a grain to save a total $R$ with countable stars.

**Not claimed.** No total $R$ exists. Empty $R$ is not total if $|\mathrm{Obs}|>1$. A tournament on two points is total. That map is not the 2008 job.
