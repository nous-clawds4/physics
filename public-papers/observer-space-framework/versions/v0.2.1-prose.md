## 1. Abstract / purpose

This paper states an ontology of observers as analytic metric jets, equivalently unique simply-connected analytic patches, oriented as $O^u$ when branching is discussed. Alongside the elementary object it introduces *relative* ensembles of worlds and spacetimes compatible with a germ. It does not reconstruct a whole spacetime $W$ from an observer $O$.

It states a working type-(ii) motion law on observer space: Continuous World Switching (CWS). It defines a branch set $B(O^u)$ so that an equal-probability postulate has something definite to act on. It separates **EPP1** (equal weight on arms of $B(O^u)$) from **EPP2** (weight proportional to the size of post-branch world or spacetime ensembles) and names a product ansatz as the *hoped* hinge between them — a hope, not a theorem.

It offers a no-no-go sketch, as illustration only: Obs-local branching need not be a Bell-local hidden-variable theory, and non-50-50 trees can be drawn on $\mathrm{Obs}$. That sketch is not a Born derivation and is not a claim that Bell is refuted.

Born and Einstein remain goals for later versions. They are not theorems of v0.2.1.

## 2. Ontology

Precise working definitions live in `mathematical-framework.md`. This section states the public convention that clears the Ontology review of the v0.1 outline.

### Public convention

Paper 1's locked elementary observer is an infinite jet of a real-analytic metric **plus** a future time direction, equivalently the unique simply-connected real-analytic patch determined by that jet. For this public paper the same object is presented with an explicit split between the metric lump and the choice of time direction, as follows.

**Metric germ.** Write $O = j_p^\infty$ for the infinite jet of a real-analytic Lorentzian metric $g$ at a point $p$. Equivalently, $O$ is the unique simply-connected real-analytic patch determined by that jet (unique analytic continuation). Smooth is not enough: in the smooth category a jet need not pick a unique lump. Analyticity is required.

**Gauge / lump identity.** Two presentations that differ only by a pointed analytic diffeomorphism renaming the same patch are the same lump. The public object is that lump: jet ≡ simply-connected analytic patch. It is **not** a raw coordinate jet with gauge "deferred forever." Details of the lump quotient remain in-house; the identity claim does not.

**Oriented observer.** Write $O^u = (O,u)$ for that lump together with a unit timelike vector $u$. Nothing in the *bare metric jet* prefers a particular $u$ among the unit hyperboloid at $p$. A physical instantaneous observer still includes a time direction. **Branching and EPP1 are about oriented observers.** Write $B(O^u)$. Bare $O$ is used when orientation is not yet chosen.

**Orientation in spacetime.** The vector $u$ lives in $T_pM$.

**Orientation in observer space.** Write $V = \mathrm{Lift}(u)$ for the lift of $u$ along a path in $\mathrm{Obs}$. For v0.2.1 the default reading is **path-space**: curves and $C^\infty$ matching along paths, without locking a tangent-space structure on $\mathrm{Obs}$. Whether $\mathrm{Obs}$ admits charts or a Fréchet tangent space remains open (§9). Do not smuggle a locked chart.

**Observer space.** $\mathrm{Obs}$ is the space of such lumps (oriented or not as marked). It is not Gielen and Wise's 7-manifold of future-directed unit 4-velocities — same English phrase, different object. It is not Larsson's Quantum Jet Theory: those objects are $p$-jets of fields about an expansion point or trajectory, not the infinite jet of the metric, and they carry no counting measure of the kind used here.

**Spacetime.** An $M$ is a connected real-analytic Lorentzian 4-manifold. Topology (for example $\pi_1$) may vary across the ensemble. Einstein's equation is **not** assumed on $M$ in v0.2.1.

**World.** A world is $W = (M,p)$: a spacetime together with an anchor point (point of view) $p$.

**World ensemble.** $E_W(O)$ (or $E_W(O^u)$ when orientation matters) is the set of pointed worlds compatible with the germ (and with $u$ when oriented). The same $M$ may appear more than once if several distinct anchors carry the same germ. This is a **relative ensemble**: not a second elementary observer, not a vote of global topology on when evolutions split, and **not** a reconstruction of a whole $W$ from $O$.

**Spacetime ensemble.** $E_M(O)$ is the corresponding multiset of underlying manifolds $M$ (with multiplicity when several anchors share a germ). The same firewall applies.

**Germs in a manifold.** $S(M)$ is the set of germs that occur in $M$.

**Sibling points.** Other anchors in the same $M$ with the same germ are sibling points to the chosen anchor. They are multiplicity in the ensemble, not a fork of the elementary object.

**Compatibility.** Write $O_i \Leftrightarrow O_j$ when each is an allowable future of the other via some causal geodesic in some $M$: there exist $p_1,p_2$ in some $M$ with germs $O_i$ and $O_j$ and a finite geodesic connecting them, and the reverse. Compatibility is not automatically transitive. Its transitive closure is reachability if world switching is allowed.

**Note.** The intersection of distinct *world* ensembles is empty (distinct germs cannot share the same pointed world). Co-occurrence of manifolds lives in $E_M$.

## 3. Type (i) vs type (ii)

**Type (i).** One-world hitchhiking: the Obs curve is the germ along a causal geodesic in a fixed $M$. There is no world switching. Analytic lifts in a single $M$ do not delayed-fork: the identity theorem kills a *delayed* fork after a thick shared prefix of proper time (agreement of the Obs-lift on a positive-length initial segment of $\tau$ forces unique analytic continuation). It is not the claim that "germs still agree" at a purported fork instant — that would already be no split.

**Type (ii).** Across-worlds motion on $\mathrm{Obs}$: paths need not stay in one $M$. World switching is the main example.

In-house work explored type-(ii) leftovers on a relation $R$. This public paper takes a **concrete** type-(ii) launching pad — CWS — not the full leftover inventory. That inventory is not restarted here.

## 4. Continuous World Switching (CWS)

CWS is a set of **working postulates**, chosen, not derived.

1. Allowed paths in $\mathrm{Obs}$ are **piecewise geodesic lifts**: on each piece, the Obs curve is the lift of a causal geodesic in some world.
2. The traveler may **switch** from world $W_1$ to world $W_2$ at a shared germ.
3. The Obs path is **$C^\infty$**.
4. **Orientation does not jump**: $V$ varies continuously; there is no Direction Switching.

**Substance note.** $C^\infty$ together with piecewise lifts is strong. At a switch the two lifts must match to **infinite order in $\tau$**, not merely share a germ. That may force fewer switches than a naive Echeverria–Klinkhammer–Thorne (EKT) billiard cartoon suggests. EKT remains a picture of hitchhiking and of classical non-uniqueness, not the mechanism of the ontology.

**Alternative (not current).** Direction Switching: orientation may change, but only infinitesimally, and any change must be $C^\infty$. This is not the present paradigm.

CWS constrains which paths are allowed. It is not yet an ODE on $\mathrm{Obs}$.

## 5. Branches $B(O^u)$

An equal-probability postulate needs a definite notion of branch before it has anything to act on. This section fixes that notion for CWS; it does not yet assign weights.

**Allowed continuation.** Fix an oriented observer $O^u=(O,u)$. An allowed continuation at $O^u$ is a germ of a path $\gamma:[0,\varepsilon)\to\mathrm{Obs}$ with $\gamma(0)=O$ (the underlying lump), carrying the orientation $u$ separately, such that:

1. $\gamma$ is $C^\infty$ as a map into $\mathrm{Obs}$ in the informal path-space sense of §2 (no locked atlas or Fréchet $T_O$; the primary cut for branching is infinite-order agreement of path-jets at $0$, which does not need charts — see §9);
2. $\gamma$ is a piecewise geodesic lift in the CWS sense: world switches are allowed at shared germs;
3. orientation is preserved: $V(\tau)=\mathrm{Lift}(u(\tau))$ does not jump.

Match strength is aligned with CWS: infinite order in $\tau$ at a switch and at the branch germ (working default).

**Equivalence.** Two allowed continuations are equivalent if they agree to infinite order in $\tau$ at $0$ (same jet of the Obs-path). A weaker working option is agreement, after reparameterization, on some common initial interval. "Same measured bit" (for example spin up versus spin down) is an allowed *further* quotient when defining arms of a measurement branch.

**Branch set.** $B(O^u)$ is the set of equivalence classes of allowed continuations. Do not write ambiguous $B(O)$ for the EPP story.

**Branch point.** An oriented observer is a branch point when $|B(O^u)|\ge 2$.

**What $B(O^u)$ is not.** It is not a Fermi-slice neighborhood of the lump (typically a continuum). It is not $E_M$ or $E_W$. It is not a delayed analytic fork inside one $M$. A split in $B(O^u)$ is a split among allowed world-switching continuations in $\mathrm{Obs}$.

**Hard gate.** EPP1 applies **only** when $B(O^u)$ is **discrete** (finite or countable). A continuum of arms is not an equal-probability story.

**Stress test (EKT / spin).** Let $O^{u*}$ be a candidate break where a shared tube ends and some $M$ leaves the ensemble. Before trusting equal weight, ask: is $|B(O^{u*})|\ge 2$? Is $B(O^{u*})$ finite, countably infinite, or a continuum under CWS matching? Does strong $C^\infty$ matching collapse the cartoon to one continuation, force a discrete split, or leave a continuum? If $|B(O^{u*})|$ is countably infinite, what exhaustion yields well-defined ratios (§9)?

## 6. Measurement cartoon (optional picture — not ontology)

This section is **picture / cartoon only**. It is not ontology and not a Paper 1 lock.

One may *picture* particles as 4-geons, with type loosely tied to topology (especially $\pi_1$), and allow microscopic twisting as rhetoric. That rhetoric is Hadley-adjacent. This paper does **not** adopt 4-geons as the definition of $O$. Topology-as-particle-type is picture or later hope, not §2.

An optional extra stipulation — named, not forced — is that distinct $M$ in an ensemble may be required to have distinct topology.

**Interesting branches.** The recorded bit enters the **local** germ while the system may be **far**. Before the bit is recorded, the ensemble is mixed with respect to that bit; after, the arms are purified in that bit.

**Same spin bit.** Many worlds may sit in each arm. $B(O^{u*})$ counts arms (for example two), not every manifold in $E_M$.

**Locality slogan.** Branching that is local in $\mathrm{Obs}$ and nonlocal in spacetime is a **program claim**, not a theorem. It is not a claim that Bell is refuted. It is not a spacetime-local hidden-variable theory that "beats Bell."

## 7. EPP1 vs EPP2 and the product ansatz

Prefer the labels **EPP1** and **EPP2**. Do **not** reuse the 2008 word "egalitarianism" (desk-reject risk against Deutsch–Wallace vocabulary).

**EPP1 (working postulate for narrative).** At a branch point, equal weight on the discrete arms of $B(O^u)$. This is chosen for the v0.2.1 story. It is **not** forced by the ontology of germs alone.

**EPP2a.** Weight proportional to $|E_W|$ on each arm's post-branch world ensemble.

**EPP2b.** The same with $|E_M|$ (spacetime ensembles).

EPP1 and EPP2 **agree** only under extra structure.

**Product ansatz (hope, not theorem).** Suppose post-branch ensembles factor as $\{\mathrm{A},\mathrm{B}\}\times\mathcal{R}$ with the same reservoir $\mathcal{R}$ of "irrelevant" topology on both arms. Then a bijection or matching density can make EPP2 collapse to EPP1. That is a hope. It is not automatic: it fails if the measured bit locks other topology, or if the arms admit different external decoctions. Even when $\mathcal{R}$ is shared, $\infty/\infty$ still needs a named **exhaustion**.

**Not Stoica.** Counting here is not Hilbert-space ontic counting with $|\psi|^2$ already in the density. No $|a|^2$ is put in by hand as multiplicity.

## 8. No-no-go sketch (not a Born derivation)

The purpose of the toy is an **existence sketch**: an Obs-local branching model need not be a spacetime-local hidden-variable theory of the Bell sort, and non-50-50 patterns can be drawn as trees on $\mathrm{Obs}$.

It is **not** a derivation of Born. Hand-built trees that match $|a|^2$ are illustrations only.

It is **not** "Bell is refuted" or "Bell's hypotheses fail as a theorem of CWS."

It is **not** an Everett-versus-Deutsch–Wallace probability paper.

**Circularity wall.** EPP plus an engineered tree is not a proof. A later hope is that CWS, together with whatever forces discrete $B(O^u)$, *forces* weights. That hope is not claimed here.

Place this section so reviewers cannot confuse the no-no-go with the prize.

## 9. Open problems

- **Tangent space on $\mathrm{Obs}$.** Three options remain open: (1) none — path space and $C^\infty$ curves only, $V$ informal; (2) Fermi $k$-jets or infinite jets — charts, ordinary or Fréchet tangent spaces; (3) topology only (Fermi-slice) — neighborhoods without $T_O$. CWS matching may need (1) or (2). EPP needs discrete branches more than it needs $T_O$. v0.2.1 defaults to (1) for prose.
- What makes $B(O^u)$ discrete under CWS? (Hard gate before EPP1.)
- Exhaustion / cancellation for countable arms or countable $|E|$.
- Compatibility's transitive closure versus one-$M$ motion.
- Analytic germ versus small-ball / hyperbolic diamond (in-house tension): the public object in v0.2.1 is the analytic jet / simply-connected patch; the small-ball alternative is deferred.
- **Born** as hope; **Einstein's equation** as dream goal. Do not assume Einstein on $M$ yet; adding it by hand would be a conscious extra.

## 10. What this paper does not claim

- No Born derivation; no slide from the no-no-go or from hand-built trees into Born.
- No Einstein derivation.
- No solution to the preferred-basis problem beyond "arms of $B(O^u)$ under a named equivalence."
- No grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$ from continuity alone.
- No $|a|^2$ as multiplicity; not Stoica-style Hilbert counting.
- No assertion that Bell is refuted — only a program-level Obs-locality move and a no-no-go sketch.
- No Everett-versus-Deutsch–Wallace framing. Nearby, not this paper: Wallace / Deutsch–Wallace decision-theoretic Born inside Everett; Carroll–Sebens self-locating / branch-weight talk; Vaidman typicality talk — arms here are CWS classes on $\mathrm{Obs}$, not decoherence branches with Born already in the quantum state.
- Objects are analytic jets / oriented observers as named — not decoherence branches with Born already in the quantum state.
- Not Gielen–Wise observer space (phrase collision only).
- Not Larsson Quantum Jet Theory (same English "jet," different object).
- 4-geons are picture, not lock-side ontology.
- In-house theorem numbers from `papers/` are background, not required reading for v0.2.1.
