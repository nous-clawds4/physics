## 3. Type (i) vs type (ii)

- **Type (i):** hitchhiking along a timelike geodesic in a fixed $W$; one oriented curvelet $\gamma_{O^u}$; no analytic delayed fork in one $M$.
- **Type (ii):** edges that are **not** successive oriented germs along $\gamma_{O^u}$; jumps discontinuous in $\mathrm{Obs}$.
- **Oriented split:** $(O^u,O'^{u'})$ is type (i) iff $O'^{u'}=\gamma_{O^u}(\tau)$ as oriented germ for some $\tau$; every other edge is type (ii), including bare $u$-jumps.

- **Not restarted:** leftover $R$-property inventory.

## 4. Motion: CWS baseline; Option E residue as public join reading

### 4.1 Type (i) baseline — CWS singleton

- CWS 1–4: piecewise geodesic lifts; switch at shared germ; Obs path $C^\infty$; orientation does not jump.
- **Proposition (CWS singleton):** under CWS and either named path-germ equivalence, $|B_{\mathrm{CWS}}(O^u)|=1$. EPP1 empty under CWS alone.
- Demotion: CWS is **not** the branching law.
- Escape from the singleton for branching is **not** “reject infinite-order matching.” Among geodesic lifts the singleton is shared germ + uniqueness + equivalence at the start. Matching-order story deleted.

### 4.2 Option E residue — public join / branching pad (working, abandonable)

- **Pieces:** on each open graph edge, analytic-geodesic lift in some analytic $W$ (type i on the open interval).
- **Joins:** discontinuous type-(ii) jumps + **graph incidence** (Option E). Arms = graph **out-star**.
- **Not Option F as branching mechanism:** truncated-jet / finite-$k$ matching (Option C) is **inert** at $k=1$ between distinct lumps and **prohibitive** at $k\ge 2$ for curvature-changing jumps. Pairing C with E does not buy branching power.
- **Optional housekeeping (not HOLE escape):** on the continue / Thm 7 arm, jet+$u$ agreement is automatic (same oriented germ). If bare $u$-jumps at fixed lump are excluded from $R$, say so as a **selection rule on $R$**, reconciling with §3 (those jumps are already type (ii) by the oriented split).
- **Adopted (working, abandonable):** existence of type-(ii) edges / Option E residue package. Drop it → layer (i) unchanged.
- **Composition (keep — cleared #105 as definition):**
  1. When a discrete vertex set $V^*$ is in force for hard-gate (2), require $R\subseteq V^*\times V$ (edges attach only at selected vertices); otherwise flow-closed $V$ is a continuum of attachment points.
  2. A **history** alternates type-(i) curvelet segments and type-(ii) jumps; Obs-path discontinuous at each jump; proper time only along type-(i) segments.
  3. $R$ contains **no** type-(i) pairs; out-degree = type-(ii) out-degree.
  4. $\mathrm{succ}_R(O^u):=\{O'^{u'}:(O^u,O'^{u'})\in R\}$ (one sentence).
  5. **First-edge equivalence:** two allowed continuations from $O^u$ are equivalent iff they share the same first type-(ii) edge, or both take the continue / type-(i) class with no first type-(ii) edge (one sentence).
  6. $B_R(O^u):=\{[\gamma_{O^u}]\}\cup\mathrm{succ}_R(O^u)$. When a continue segment exists, $|B_R|=1+|\mathrm{succ}_R|$. The continue / Thm 7 arm is the $[\gamma]$ term — **not** listed as a type-(ii) edge in $R$ (no $e_{\mathrm{ext}}\in R$).
  7. Notation $B_R$: property of the pair $(O^u,R)$.
- **Direction (open):** direction hypothesis on $R$ **or** walk semantics with EPP1 per visit — choose in prose.
- **Spacetime-realisation (honesty, not neutral):**
  - **(a)** Every type-(ii) edge realised by a timelike geodesic in some analytic world: by §2 rigidity, confines $R$ to pairs in one analytic continuation of the germ (in-patch continuum candidates); **forbids curvature-changing jumps** (including any Minkowski$\to$FLRW toy edge). Compatibility relation ≈ (a) if kept.
  - **(b)** “Across-worlds” is a label without realisation: allows curvature-changing digraph edges; toy edges (if cited) silently took (b).
  - Outline: name both; do not pretend they are interchangeable; prose must pick or keep both as open with this cost table.

## 5. Vertices, hard gate, toy, gate

### 5.1 Public $\tau$-slice (lock-side preferred)

- **Continuum theorem (state):** under §2’s ensemble as written (no inextendibility), naive extend-vs-not marks a continuum of $\tau$ (hole-punching: for each $\tau_0$ a deleted-point world dies there). Hard-gate clause (2) fails identically under that reading. Inextendibility alone does not restore discreteness in general (cosmic-string families).
- **Public engine for clause (2):** prefer **lock-side $\tau$-slice candidate** — e.g. isolated critical points (or named levels) of a non-constant analytic curvature scalar along $\gamma$ (one-variable analyticity ⇒ discrete in $\tau$ when non-constant). **Choice of scalar and level is named extra** (honest grain-like choice on the curvelet, not a theorem that $\mathrm{Obs}$ is discrete, and not a vote of $E_W$).
- **Ensemble-timed extend-vs-not:** demoted. If cited at all: in-house schematic only, or explicit **chosen discrete sub-ensemble** cost (grain on $E_W$) — not “better motivated than a grain of Obs” without saying that.
- On homogeneous / flat curvelets, “same germ = same vertex” collapses history times; vertices may need $(\mathrm{lump},\tau)$ tags — named extra if used.

### 5.2 $E^*$ lean (honesty, residual)

- Die = ensemble dropout ≠ Obs arm (unless named sink).
- Multiple analytic extenders → one Thm 7 / $[\gamma]$ arm.
- $|B|\ge 2$ needs true type-(ii) outs in $R$, not extend-vs-not alone.

### 5.3 Hard gate (two clauses + exhaustion)

EPP1 applies only when:

1. $B_R(O^u)$ is **finite** at the vertex where EPP1 acts (or: countable with a **named exhaustion** declared as extra — ratios on finite sub-collections); **and**
2. branch points along any history are **discrete in $\tau$** (finite on every compact proper-time interval of each type-(i) segment), enforced by the public $\tau$-slice of §5.1 (or another named extra), not by raw extend-vs-not + §2 ensemble.

### 5.4 Toy and public gate

- **Toy:** `papers/B-ge2-minimal-toy.md` **retired as a $V^*$ demonstration**. At most a **digraph fact**: some abstract $(V,R)$ has $|B_R|=2$ under composition (C). Hand-built in ensemble, vertex, and out if cited; $W_{\mathrm{cut}}$ not a Thm 6 lifetime example; flat curvelet caveat. Prefer non-flat germ if a schematic is rebuilt later.
- **Public controlled-example gate:** an $R$ given by a **stated rule** on lumps that is **uniform** (one rule in germ invariants at every vertex; no finite case-list of named germs) **and** carries a **named motivation** (symmetry / naturality / variational / empirical — menu named; else admit the bar is only “not a hand list,” and relocation ends at Bertrand among admissible rules). Arm count a consequence of the rule; branch points discrete along every history. Hand-listed digraph does not qualify. “Weights forced” remains a later bar (needs law + typicality + circularity — not claimed). Arbitrary lock-side rules (e.g. Kretschmann critical points + $\pm\varepsilon\hat n$ outs) show “stated” alone is not enough — say so.
