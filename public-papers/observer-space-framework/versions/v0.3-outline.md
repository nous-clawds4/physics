- State ontology of observers as analytic jets / simply-connected patches (oriented as $O^u$ for branching), and relative ensembles of worlds/spacetimes compatible with a germ — not a reconstruction of whole $W$ from $O$.

## 2. Ontology

Carry v0.2.1 locks (unchanged object). Outline bullets:

**Public convention.** Paper 1’s locked elementary observer is an infinite jet of a real-analytic metric **plus** a future time direction, equivalently the unique simply-connected real-analytic patch.

- **Metric germ** $O = j_p^\infty$: infinite jet of $g$ at $p$ (equivalently that simply-connected analytic patch). Analytic required; smooth not enough.
- **Gauge / lump identity:** two presentations that differ only by a pointed analytic diffeomorphism renaming the same patch are the same lump. Public object is **not** a raw coordinate jet with gauge deferred forever.
- **Oriented observer** $O^u = (O,u)$: lump plus unit timelike $u$. Bare jet does not prefer a particular $u$; a physical instantaneous observer still includes a time direction. **$B$ and EPP1 are about $O^u$.** Write $B(O^u)$.
- **Orientation in spacetime** $u \in T_pM$.
- **Orientation in observer space** $V = \mathrm{Lift}(u)$: for type-(i) curves, path-space default (tangent structure on $\mathrm{Obs}$ still open). Type-(ii) histories are graph-paths; they need not be curves in $\mathrm{Obs}$.
- **Observer space** $\mathrm{Obs}$: space of lumps. Not Gielen–Wise’s 7-manifold. Not Larsson QJT field $p$-jets.
- **Spacetime** $M$: connected real-analytic Lorentzian 4-manifold; topology may vary. Einstein **not** assumed on $M$ in v0.3.
- **World** $W = (M,p)$.
- **World / spacetime ensembles** $E_W(O)$, $E_M(O)$: **relative ensembles**, not a second elementary observer; not a vote of global topology on splits; **no reconstruction of a whole $W$ from $O$**. Sibling points = ensemble multiplicity, not a fork of the elementary object.
- **Compatibility** $O_i \Leftrightarrow O_j$: mutual allowable future via some geodesic in some $M$; not automatically transitive.
- Note: intersection of distinct world ensembles empty; co-occurrence in $E_M$.

## 3. Type (i) vs type (ii)

- **Type (i) — in-world hitchhiking.** Obs curve is the germ along a causal geodesic in a fixed $M$. One curvelet $\gamma_O$ (resp. $\gamma_{O^u}$) per oriented germ. Analytic lifts in one $M$ do not delayed-fork (identity theorem). Relative ensembles stay relative.
- **Type (ii) — across-worlds.** Extra edges of a transition structure that are **not** successive germs along a geodesic lift. Histories are finite **graph-paths**. Delayed forks allowed as graph-paths (common prefix, then distinct vertices). Paths need **not** be analytic curves in $\mathrm{Obs}$.
- **Not restored:** 2007 geodesic-generated tree across $M_O$ (still killed as a theorem of layer (i)).
- **Not restarted:** leftover $R$-property inventory (acyclicity, finite Desc, connectedness, …) inside the public paper.

## 4. Motion: CWS as baseline; type-(ii) $R$ as branching pad

### 4.1 Type (i) baseline — CWS / geodesic hitchhiking (null result)

CWS 1–4 as in v0.2.1 (piecewise geodesic lifts; switch at shared germ; Obs path $C^\infty$; orientation does not jump) remain a coherent type-(i) package.

**Proposition (CWS singleton) — state, do not hide.** Under CWS 1–4 and the §5-of-v0.2.1 equivalence options, $|B_{\mathrm{CWS}}(O^u)|=1$: the unique geodesic curvelet. World switching can change lifetime; it does **not** change the Obs-path. EPP1 has empty domain under CWS alone.

**Demotion.** CWS is **not** the public branching law. It is the honest type-(i) null baseline. The old substance note (“infinite-order matching may thin EKT cartoons”) is backwards under CWS: matching is automatic and switches are invisible in $\mathrm{Obs}$.

**Alternative Direction Switching / $u$-jumps:** not primary (continuum of arms unless a grain is invented). Named only as rejected primary options.

### 4.2 Type (ii) launching pad — transition structure $R$ (working, abandonable)

**Definition 7 (transition structure; public language).** A pair $(V,R)$ with $V\subseteq\mathrm{Obs}$ and $R\subseteq V\times V$: a directed graph on lumps. A **type-(ii) edge** is a pair $(O,O')\in R$ that does **not** arise as successive germs along a geodesic lift of $O$ in a real-analytic $W$.

**Definition 8 (delayed fork).** Two graph-paths delayed-fork if they share a common prefix of positive length, then occupy distinct vertices.

**Adopted (working, abandonable).** Existence of type-(ii) edges as the across-worlds / branching stand-in (`type-ii-adopted.md`). Not a theorem of hitchhiking. Drop type (ii) and layer (i) is unchanged.

**Proposition 13 cost (name in outline).** Locked data at $O$ (lump, patch, curvelet, ensemble) do **not** determine type-(ii) edges. The law of $R$ is extra. No neighborhood-law until $\mathrm{Obs}$ is a space (gauge-as-space open). No $1/N$ on edges.

## 5. Branches $B(O^u)$ under type (ii)

- **Redefine** allowed continuations as germs of **graph-paths** in $(V,R)$ starting at $O^u$ (orientation carried as data on vertices/edges as needed).
- **Equivalence:** one-step stars or path-germs — state explicitly in prose; not CWS infinite-order path-jets.
- **Branch set** $B(O^u)$: equivalence classes of those continuations. Branch point: out-degree $\ge 2$ (or $\ge 2$ inequivalent one-step successors).
- **Not:** Fermi-slice continuum; not $E_M$/$E_W$; not delayed analytic fork inside one $M$; **not** $B_{\mathrm{CWS}}$ (that is a singleton).
- **Hard gate:** EPP1 applies **only** when $B(O^u)$ is **discrete** (finite or countable). Continuum of arms ≠ equal-probability story.
- **Stress test / honesty:** exhibit a controlled $O^u$ with $2\le|B(O^u)|<\infty$ (or countable with named exhaustion) under an **explicit** $R$ — **or say none is claimed yet.** v0.3 claims **none yet**. Adopting type (ii) makes $|B|\ge 2$ *possible*; it does not exhibit it.
- **Firewall:** no sentence treats $|B|\ge 2$ under CWS.
