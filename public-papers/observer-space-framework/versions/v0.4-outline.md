## 3. Type (i) vs type (ii)

- **Type (i):** hitchhiking along a timelike geodesic in a fixed $W$; one oriented curvelet $\gamma_{O^u}$; no analytic delayed fork in one $M$ (identity theorem / CWS singleton).
- **Type (ii):** edges that are **not** successive oriented germs along $\gamma_{O^u}$; jumps discontinuous in $\mathrm{Obs}$ at the edge.
- **Oriented split (fix):** $(O^u,O'^{u'})$ is type (i) iff $O'^{u'}=\gamma_{O^u}(\tau)$ as oriented germ for some $\tau$ in the lifetime; every other edge is type (ii), including bare $u$-jumps. (Fixes v0.3 misclassification.)
- **Not restored:** 2007 geodesic-generated tree as a theorem of hitchhiking.
- **Not restarted:** leftover $R$-property inventory in the public paper.
- **Pad pedigree (honesty):** type-(ii) pad is the author’s 2005 / Strayhorn3 transition / random-walk hypothesis under a new name — names the HV desk-reject smell concretely.

## 4. Motion: CWS baseline; Option F as public join reading

### 4.1 Type (i) baseline — CWS singleton (null result)

- CWS 1–4 as before: piecewise geodesic lifts; switch at shared germ; Obs path $C^\infty$; orientation does not jump.
- **Proposition (CWS singleton):** under CWS and either named path-germ equivalence, $|B_{\mathrm{CWS}}(O^u)|=1$. Switches change lifetime, not Obs-path. EPP1 empty under CWS alone.
- Demotion: CWS is **not** the branching law.
- Rejected as primary: Direction Switching; orientation jumps alone (continuum unless grain).

### 4.2 Option F — public join / branching pad (working, abandonable)

- **Pieces:** on each open graph edge, analytic-geodesic lift in some analytic $W$ (type i on the open interval).
- **Joins:** at vertex $O^{u*}$, require truncated jet $j^k g$ + $u$ through order **fixed finite $k\ge 1$** (Option C) **and** incidence in the discrete graph (Option E). **Not** $C^\infty$ / infinite-order matching (HOLE escape).
- **Reject Option D** as primary ($C^k$ spacetime metric glue / single-$W$ kink): clashes with locked analytic $W$ (Prop. 7.3) or collapses into Direction Switching.
- **Arms:** graph **out-star** (distinct outgoing edges), not infinite-order Obs-path jets.
- **Adopted (working, abandonable):** existence of such type-(ii) edges / Option F package. Drop it → layer (i) unchanged.
- **Composition (Claude reading C — write it):**
  1. $V$ closed under type-(i) flow along curvelets that meet vertices.
  2. A **history** alternates type-(i) curvelet segments and type-(ii) jumps; Obs-path discontinuous at each jump; proper time only along type-(i) segments.
  3. $R$ (or $E^*$) contains **no** type-(i) pairs; out-degree = type-(ii) out-degree.
  4. $B_R(O^u):=\{[\gamma_{O^u}]\}\cup\mathrm{succ}_R(O^u)$ under **first-edge** equivalence (the type-(i) term is one class by §4.1). Notation $B_R$: property of the pair $(O^u,R)$, not of the observer alone.
  5. **Direction (open, named):** either a direction hypothesis on $R$ (e.g. irreflexive/acyclic on reachable set) or walk semantics with EPP1 applied per visit — choose in prose; outline insists one is named.
  6. **Spacetime-realisation (open, named):** either every type-(ii) edge is realised by a timelike geodesic in some world (constraint on $R$; not the 2007 tree-as-theorem), or “across-worlds” is a label without realisation — choose in prose; outline insists one is named.

### 4.3 Cost of $R$ / Option F (loud — HV smell)

## 5. $V^*$, $E^*$, $B_R$, hard gate, toy

### 5.1 $V^*$ by extend-vs-not (adopt with caveats)

- Along $\gamma_{O^u}$, candidate vertex times $\tau_*$ where some ensemble members **extend** analytically through $\tau_*$ and some **die** (finite lifetime): Thm 7(3) loci.
- Vertex = oriented germ $\gamma(\tau_*)$ (same germ = same vertex).
- **Caveats:** vertices mark **ensemble events on one history**, **not** Obs forks by themselves (Thm 7(3): extend-vs-not ≠ two next germs). Discreteness of $V^*$ along $\gamma$ inherits discreteness of death times $T_{\mathrm{die}}$ (further structure / selection; continuum risks if $E_\gamma$ or lifetimes are wild). Choosing this rule is still extra (Prop. 13 pattern), better motivated than an arbitrary grain, not a theorem that $\mathrm{Obs}$ is discrete.

### 5.2 $E^*$ lean (honesty)

- **Die = ensemble dropout**, not a second Obs arm (unless an explicit terminal sink vertex is added and named).
- Multiple analytic extenders forced by Thm 7 to the **same** next germ = **one** arm.
- Therefore $|B|\ge 2$ needs **disagreement** among outs: inequivalent post-join pieces, hence at least one **true type-(ii)** edge off the unique Thm 7 continuation — not extend-vs-not alone.

### 5.3 Hard gate (two clauses)

EPP1 applies only when:

1. $B_R(O^u)$ is **discrete** (finite or countable) at the vertex — countable stars; **and**
2. the set of branch points along any history is **discrete in $\tau$**: finite on every compact proper-time interval of each type-(i) segment.

Clause (2) is a further extra (a $\tau$-slice), distinct from countable out-degree and from a grain of all $\mathrm{Obs}$. Name it in costs. (Prose may note lock-side *candidates* for $\tau$-slices, e.g. critical points of analytic curvature scalars — choice of scalar/level still extra.)

### 5.4 In-house toy = existence-in-form; public gate

- Point to `papers/B-ge2-minimal-toy.md`: exhibits $|B(O^{u*})|=2$ under $V^*$ extend-vs-not + Option F + $E^*$ lean, with outs = Thm 7 Minkowski continuation + a **named** type-(ii) edge to an FLRW comoving germ; Prop. 13 honesty; no $1/N$.
- That is **existence-in-form** (including a hand-named type-(ii) out). It does **not** clear the public gate.
- **Public controlled-example gate:** an $R$/$E^*$ given by a **stated rule** on lumps (function of $O^u$ and at most named extra structure declared as such), applied to a computable germ, arm count a consequence of the rule, branch points discrete along every history. A hand-listed digraph does **not** qualify. “Weights forced, not assumed” remains a later bar (needs law + typicality + circularity work — not claimed here).

## 7. EPP1 vs EPP2

- Prefer EPP1 / EPP2; forbid “egalitarianism.”
- **EPP1 (named postulate):** equal weight $1/|B_R|$ on discrete arms of $B_R(O^u)$ under the hard gate. This **is** the per-branch equal-weight / $1/N$ rule (2005 pedigree), adopted as a **postulate** with empty domain until a law-given controlled example exists. It is **not** implied by existence of $R$, and it does **not** choose multiplicities to reproduce $|a|^2$.
- **Reading of the weight (name in prose):** typicality over co-existing arms (Paper 1 spirit) vs single-outcome chance; outline prefers typicality for consistency with Paper 1 — one sentence in prose.
- **EPP2:** weight $\propto |E_W|$ or $|E_M|$ per arm — **ensemble vote**. Label as **departure** from Paper 1’s “ensemble does not vote / each evolution counts as one,” or as a foil against which EPP1 is stated — choose in prose; not co-equal silent hope.
- Product ansatz: hope; Bertrand / exhaustion still needed.
- Not Stoica.
