## 1. Abstract / purpose

- State an ontology of observers as analytic jets / simply-connected patches (oriented as $O^u$ for branching), and of relative ensembles of worlds/spacetimes compatible with a germ — not a reconstruction of whole $W$ from $O$.
- State a working type-(ii) motion law on observer space: **Continuous World Switching (CWS)**.
- Define branches $B(O^u)$ so an equal-probability postulate has something to act on.
- Separate **EPP1** (equal weight on arms) from **EPP2** (counting worlds/spacetimes per arm) and name a product ansatz as the hoped hinge between them.
- Offer a **no-no-go** sketch (illustration only): Obs-local branching need not be a Bell-local HV theory; non-50-50 trees can be drawn on $\mathrm{Obs}$. Not a Born derivation; not “Bell refuted.”
- Mark Born (and Einstein) as goals for later versions, not theorems of v0.1.1.

## 2. Ontology

Pull precise definitions from `mathematical-framework.md`. Outline bullets:

**Public convention (clears Ontology HOLD).** Paper 1’s locked elementary observer is an infinite jet of a real-analytic metric **plus** a future time direction, equivalently the unique simply-connected real-analytic patch. For this public paper:

- **Metric germ** $O = j_p^\infty$: the infinite jet of $g$ at $p$ (equivalently that simply-connected analytic patch). **Gauge / lump identity:** two presentations that differ only by a pointed analytic diffeomorphism renaming the same patch are the same lump. Details of the lump quotient stay in-house; the public object is **not** a raw coordinate jet with gauge “deferred forever.”
- **Oriented observer** $O^u = (O,u)$: that lump plus a unit timelike $u$. Nothing in the *bare metric jet* prefers a particular $u$ among the unit hyperboloid; a physical instantaneous observer still includes a time direction. **$B$ and EPP1 are about oriented observers $O^u$.** Write $B(O^u)$. Bare $O$ is used when orientation is not yet chosen.
- **Orientation in spacetime** $u \in T_pM$.
- **Orientation in observer space** $V = \mathrm{Lift}(u)$: for v0.1.1, default to the **path-space** reading (tangent structure on $\mathrm{Obs}$ still open; see §9). Do not smuggle a locked chart.
- **Observer space** $\mathrm{Obs}$: space of lumps (oriented or not as marked). Not Gielen–Wise’s 7-manifold of 4-velocities (phrase collision only). Not Larsson QJT field $p$-jets.
- **Spacetime** $M$: connected real-analytic Lorentzian 4-manifold; topology (e.g. $\pi_1$) may vary across the ensemble. Einstein’s equation is **not** assumed on $M$ in v0.1.1.
- **World** $W = (M,p)$: spacetime plus anchor / point of view.
- **World ensemble** $E_W(O)$ / $E_W(O^u)$: pointed worlds compatible with the germ (and $u$ when oriented). **Relative ensemble**, not a second elementary observer; not a vote of global topology on when evolutions split; **no reconstruction of a whole $W$ from $O$**.
- **Spacetime ensemble** $E_M(O)$: underlying $M$’s (with multiplicity if several anchors). Same firewall.
- **$S(M)$**: germs that occur in $M$.
- **Sibling points**: other anchors in the same $M$ with the same germ — multiplicity in the ensemble, not a fork of the elementary object.
- **Compatibility** $O_i \Leftrightarrow O_j$: mutual allowable future via some geodesic in some $M$; not automatically transitive; transitive closure = reachability if world switching is allowed.

Note: intersection of distinct *world* ensembles is empty; co-occurrence lives in $E_M$.

## 3. Type (i) vs type (ii)

- **Type (i):** one-world hitchhiking — Obs curve is the germ along a geodesic in a fixed $M$. No world switching. Analytic lifts in one $M$ do not delayed-fork (identity theorem).
- **Type (ii):** across-worlds motion on $\mathrm{Obs}$ — paths need not stay in one $M$. World switching is the main example.
- In-house stack explored type-(ii) leftovers on a relation $R$; this public paper takes a **concrete** type-(ii) launching pad (CWS), not the full leftover inventory.

---

## 4. Continuous World Switching (CWS)

Working postulates (chosen, not derived):

1. Allowed paths in $\mathrm{Obs}$ are **piecewise geodesic lifts**.
2. The traveler may **switch** from world $W_1$ to world $W_2$ at a shared germ.
3. The Obs path is **$C^\infty$**.
4. **Orientation does not jump** ($V$ continuous / no Direction Switching).

Substance note: $C^\infty$ plus piecewise lifts is strong — at a switch the lifts must match to **infinite order in $\tau$**, not merely share a germ. That may force fewer switches than a naive EKT cartoon.

**Alternative (not current):** Direction Switching — orientation may change only infinitesimally and $C^\infty$.

CWS constrains allowed paths; it is not yet an ODE on $\mathrm{Obs}$.

## 5. Branches $B(O^u)$

- **Allowed continuation** at oriented $O^u$: germ of a $C^\infty$ CWS path starting at $O^u$ with preserved orientation. Match strength aligned with CWS: infinite order in $\tau$ at a switch / at the branch germ (default).
- **Equivalence** of continuations: agree to infinite order in $\tau$ at $0$ (working default); weaker option = agree on a common initial interval after reparameterization. “Same measured bit” (e.g. spin up vs down) is an allowed *further* quotient when defining arms of a measurement branch.
- **Branch set** $B(O^u)$: equivalence classes of allowed continuations. (Do not write ambiguous $B(O)$ for the EPP story.)
- **Branch point:** $|B(O^u)| \ge 2$.
- **Not:** a Fermi-slice neighborhood (typically a continuum); not $E_M$; not $E_W$; not a delayed analytic fork inside one $M$.
- **Hard gate:** EPP1 applies **only** when $B(O^u)$ is **discrete** (finite or countable). A continuum of arms is not an equal-probability story.

**Stress test (EKT / spin):** candidate break $O^{u*}$ where shared tube ends; ask whether $|B(O^{u*})|\ge 2$ and whether $B$ is finite, countable, or continuum under CWS matching.

## 6. Measurement cartoon (optional picture — not ontology)

- **Picture / cartoon only (not ontology, not Paper 1 lock):** particles as 4-geons; type loosely tied to topology (especially $\pi_1$); microscopic twisting. Hadley-adjacent rhetoric is nearby; this paper does **not** adopt 4-geons as the definition of $O$. Topology-as-particle-type is picture or later hope, not §2.
- Stipulation (extra, optional): distinct $M$ in an ensemble may be required to have distinct topology — named, not forced.
- **Interesting branches:** the recorded bit enters the **local** germ while the system may be **far**. Mixed ensemble before; arms purified in that bit after.
- “Same spin bit” equivalence: many worlds per arm; $B(O^{u*})$ counts arms (e.g. 2), not every $M$.
- **Local in $\mathrm{Obs}$, nonlocal in spacetime:** **program claim**, not a theorem. Not a claim that Bell is refuted. Not a spacetime-local hidden-variable theory that “beats Bell.”

## 7. EPP1 vs EPP2 and the product ansatz

Prefer the labels **EPP1** / **EPP2**; do **not** reuse the 2008 word “egalitarianism” (desk-reject risk vs Deutsch–Wallace).

- **EPP1 (working postulate for narrative):** equal weight on discrete arms of $B(O^u)$ at a branch point. Not forced by the ontology of germs alone.
- **EPP2a:** weight $\propto |E_W|$ on each arm’s post-branch world ensemble.
- **EPP2b:** same with $|E_M|$ / spacetime ensembles.
- They **agree** only under extra structure.
- **Product ansatz (hope, not theorem):** post-branch ensembles factor as $\{\mathrm{A},\mathrm{B}\} \times \mathcal{R}$ with the same reservoir $\mathcal{R}$ of “irrelevant” topology on both arms → bijection / same density → EPP2 collapses to EPP1.
- Not automatic: fails if the measured bit locks other topology, or arms admit different external decoctions.
- $\infty/\infty$ still needs a named **exhaustion** even when $\mathcal{R}$ is shared.
- **Not Stoica:** counting here is not Hilbert-space ontic counting with $|\psi|^2$ already in the density. No $|a|^2$ in by hand as multiplicity.

## 8. No-no-go sketch (not a Born derivation)

- Purpose of the toy: **existence sketch** that an Obs-local branching model need not be a spacetime-local hidden-variable theory of the Bell sort; and that non-50-50 patterns can be drawn as trees on $\mathrm{Obs}$.
- **Not** a derivation of Born. Hand-built trees that match $|a|^2$ are illustrations only.
- **Not** “Bell is refuted” or “Bell’s hypotheses fail as a theorem of CWS.”
- **Not** an Everett-vs-Deutsch–Wallace probability paper.
- Circularity wall: EPP + engineered tree ≠ proof. Later hope: CWS (+ whatever forces discrete $B(O^u)$) **forces** weights.
- Place this section so reviewers cannot confuse no-no-go with the prize.

## 9. Open problems

- **Tangent space on $\mathrm{Obs}$:** (1) none — path space / $C^\infty$ curves only, $V$ informal; (2) Fermi $k$-jets or infinite jets — charts / Fréchet; (3) topology only (Fermi-slice) — neighborhoods without $T_O$. CWS matching may need (1) or (2); EPP needs discrete branches more than it needs $T_O$.
- What makes $B(O^u)$ discrete under CWS? (Hard gate before EPP1.)
- Exhaustion / cancellation for countable arms or countable $|E|$.
- Compatibility transitive closure vs one-$M$ motion.
- Analytic germ vs small-ball / hyperbolic diamond (in-house tension): public paper should say which object $O$ is in v0.1.1 (currently analytic jet per framework file).
- **Born** as hope; **Einstein equation** as dream goal (do not assume it on $M$ yet; adding it by hand is a conscious extra).

## 10. What this paper does not claim

- No Born derivation; no slide from no-no-go or hand-built trees into Born.
- No Einstein derivation.
- No solution to the preferred-basis problem beyond “arms of $B(O^u)$ under a named equivalence.”
- No grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$ from continuity alone.
- No $|a|^2$ as multiplicity; not Stoica-style Hilbert counting.
- No assertion that Bell is refuted — only a program-level Obs-locality move and a no-no-go sketch.
- No Everett-vs-Deutsch–Wallace framing.
- Objects are analytic jets / oriented observers as named — not decoherence branches with Born already in the quantum state.
- Not Gielen–Wise observer space (phrase collision only).
- 4-geons are picture, not lock-side ontology.
- In-house theorem numbers from `papers/` are background, not required reading for v0.1.1.
