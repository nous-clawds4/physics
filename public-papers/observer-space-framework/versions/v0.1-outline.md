# Observer space, continuous world switching, and a no-no-go sketch

David Strayhorn

**Status.** v0.1 outline — working, not submitted. Public-facing cut of the launching pad in [`mathematical-framework.md`](../../../mathematical-framework.md).

**Firewall.** This version does **not** claim a derivation of the Born rule, does **not** claim a derivation of Einstein’s field equations, does **not** invent a grain of observer space, and does **not** put $|a|^2$ in by hand as multiplicity.

---

## 1. Abstract / purpose

- State an ontology of observers as metric germs and of ensembles of worlds/spacetimes compatible with a germ.
- State a working type-(ii) motion law on observer space: **Continuous World Switching (CWS)**.
- Define branches $B(O)$ so an equal-probability postulate has something to act on.
- Separate **EPP1** (equal weight on arms) from **EPP2** (counting worlds/spacetimes per arm) and name a product ansatz as the hoped hinge between them.
- Offer a **no-no-go** sketch: Obs-local branching with a far particle is not automatically a Bell-local hidden-variable theory; a toy non-50-50 tree can exist as illustration, not as a Born derivation.
- Mark Born (and Einstein) as goals for later versions, not theorems of v0.1.

---

## 2. Ontology

Pull precise definitions from `mathematical-framework.md`. Outline bullets:

- **Observer** $O = j_p^\infty$: infinite jet of a real-analytic Lorentzian metric $g$ at $p$ (local germ).
- **Oriented observer** $O^u = (O,u)$: germ plus unit timelike $u$.
- **Orientation in spacetime** $u \in T_pM$; nothing in the bare germ prefers a particular $u$.
- **Orientation in observer space** $V = \mathrm{Lift}(u)$: tangent to a path in $\mathrm{Obs}$, if $\mathrm{Obs}$ has a tangent space (open; see §9).
- **Observer space** $\mathrm{Obs}$: set of local germs (gauge/lump issues deferred; Fermi-slice topology exists in-house as background, not required for the outline’s path-space reading).
- **Spacetime** $M$: connected real-analytic Lorentzian 4-manifold; topology (e.g. $\pi_1$) may vary across the ensemble.
- **World** $W = (M,p)$: spacetime plus anchor point / point of view.
- **World ensemble** $E_W(O)$: pointed worlds whose germ at $p$ equals $O$ (multiplicity of anchors allowed).
- **Spacetime ensemble** $E_M(O)$: underlying $M$’s (with multiplicity if several anchors).
- **$S(M)$**: set of germs that occur in $M$.
- **Sibling points**: other anchors in the same $M$ with the same germ.
- **Compatibility** $O_i \Leftrightarrow O_j$: mutual allowable future via some geodesic in some $M$; not automatically transitive; transitive closure = reachability if world switching is allowed.

Note: intersection of distinct world ensembles is empty; co-occurrence lives in $E_M$.

## 3. Type (i) vs type (ii)

- **Type (i):** one-world hitchhiking — Obs curve is the germ along a geodesic in a fixed $M$. No world switching. Analytic lifts in one $M$ do not delayed-fork (identity theorem).
- **Type (ii):** across-worlds motion on $\mathrm{Obs}$ — paths need not stay in one $M$. World switching is the main example.
- In-house stack explored type-(ii) leftovers on a relation $R$; this public paper takes a **concrete** type-(ii) launching pad (CWS), not the full leftover inventory.

---

## 4. Continuous World Switching (CWS)

Working postulates (chosen, not derived):

1. Allowed paths in $\mathrm{Obs}$ are **piecewise geodesic lifts**.
2. The traveler may **switch** from world $W_1$ to world $W_2$ at a shared germ.
3. The Obs path is **$C^\infty$**.
4. **Orientation does not jump** ($V$ continuous / no Direction Switching).

Substance note: $C^\infty$ plus piecewise lifts is strong — at a switch the lifts must match to **infinite order in $\tau$**, not merely share a germ. That may force fewer switches than a naive EKT cartoon.

**Alternative (not current):** Direction Switching — orientation may change only infinitesimally and $C^\infty$.

CWS constrains allowed paths; it is not yet an ODE on $\mathrm{Obs}$.

## 5. Branches $B(O)$

- **Allowed continuation** at oriented $O^u$: germ of a $C^\infty$ CWS path starting at $O$ with preserved orientation.
- **Equivalence** of continuations: agree to infinite order in $\tau$ at $0$ (working default); weaker option = agree on a common initial interval after reparameterization.
- **Branch set** $B(O)$ (or $B(O^u)$): equivalence classes of allowed continuations.
- **Branch point:** $|B(O)| \ge 2$.
- **Not:** a Fermi-slice neighborhood (typically a continuum); not $E_M(O)$; not a delayed analytic fork inside one $M$.
- Equal probability applies only when $B(O)$ is **discrete** (finite or countable).

**Stress test (EKT / spin):** candidate break $O^*$ where shared tube ends; ask whether $|B(O^*)|\ge 2$ and whether $B$ is finite, countable, or continuum under CWS matching.

## 6. Measurement cartoon (4-geons / far particle)

- Motivating ontology: particles as 4-geons; type tied to topology (especially $\pi_1$); microscopic twisting / exotic topology allowed as picture.
- Stipulation (extra): distinct $M$ in an ensemble may be required to have distinct topology — named, not forced.
- **Interesting branches:** the recorded bit enters the **local** germ while the geon may be **far**. Mixed ensemble before; arms purified in that bit after.
- “Same spin bit” equivalence: many worlds per arm; $B(O^*)$ counts arms (e.g. 2), not every $M$.
- **Local in $\mathrm{Obs}$, nonlocal in spacetime:** program claim. Not yet a theorem that Bell’s hypotheses fail. State carefully.

---

## 7. EPP1 vs EPP2 and the product ansatz

- **EPP1:** equal weight on arms of $B(O)$ at a branch point.
- **EPP2a:** weight $\propto |E_W|$ on each arm’s post-branch world ensemble.
- **EPP2b:** same with $|E_M|$ / spacetime ensembles.
- They **agree** only under extra structure.
- **Product ansatz (hope):** post-branch ensembles factor as $\{\mathrm{A},\mathrm{B}\} \times \mathcal{R}$ with the same reservoir $\mathcal{R}$ of “irrelevant” topology on both arms → bijection / same density → EPP2 collapses to EPP1.
- Not automatic: fails if the measured bit locks other topology, or arms admit different external decoctions.
- $\infty/\infty$ still needs a named **exhaustion** even when $\mathcal{R}$ is shared.
- Working weight rule for v0.1 narrative: **EPP1**; EPP2 and EPP1⇔EPP2 under factorization = named hopes.

---

## 8. No-no-go sketch (not a Born derivation)

- Purpose of the toy: **existence sketch** that an Obs-local branching model need not be a spacetime-local hidden-variable theory of the Bell sort; and that non-50-50 patterns can be drawn as trees on $\mathrm{Obs}$.
- **Not** a derivation of Born. Hand-built trees that match $|a|^2$ are illustrations.
- Circularity wall: EPP + engineered tree ≠ proof. Later hope: CWS (+ whatever fixes discrete $B(O)$) **forces** weights.
- Place this section so reviewers cannot confuse no-no-go with the prize.

## 9. Open problems

- **Tangent space on $\mathrm{Obs}$:** (1) none — path space / $C^\infty$ curves only, $V$ informal; (2) Fermi $k$-jets or infinite jets — charts / Fréchet; (3) topology only (Fermi-slice) — neighborhoods without $T_O$. CWS matching may need (1) or (2); EPP needs discrete branches more than it needs $T_O$.
- What makes $B(O)$ discrete under CWS?
- Exhaustion / cancellation for countable arms or countable $|E|$.
- Compatibility transitive closure vs one-$M$ motion.
- Analytic germ vs small-ball / hyperbolic diamond (in-house tension): public paper should say which object $O$ is in v0.1 (currently analytic jet per framework file).
- **Born** as hope; **Einstein equation** as dream goal (do not assume it on $M$ yet; adding it by hand is a conscious extra).

## 10. What this paper does not claim

- No Born derivation.
- No Einstein derivation.
- No solution to the preferred-basis problem beyond “arms of $B(O)$ under a named equivalence.”
- No grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$ from continuity alone.
- No assertion that Bell is refuted — only a program-level locality move and a no-no-go sketch.
- In-house theorem numbers from `papers/` are background, not loaded into v0.1 as required reading.
