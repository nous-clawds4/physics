## How three leans in #154 are neutralised (#155 "Tilts")

1. **R7 toward (M1).** v0.7 does not call the §9 (M1) line "overstated". It scopes **both** cost lines as $R$-dependent and states what each case does to **both** options:
   - (M1): a continuum of completed histories in the model without (a) (C16); countable under the (a)-restriction, where its unequal weights ($2^{-k}$ at $c=0.1$) meet Paper 1 §6's refusal case.
   - (M2): completed histories may be infinite (countably or not), so the count needs a cut or grain to act at all. This holds under the (a)-restriction too, since countably infinite sets carry no uniform countably additive measure (Paper 1 §6).

   The countable case sharpens a cost of (M1) and leaves a cost of (M2) in place. Neither line gains, and no choice is made.
2. **R3's T1 label.** v0.7 discloses the fact only: $(O^u,a)$ carries one bit of history data that §2's lock-side definition excludes. It does not say which Foundations T1 option v0.6 or v0.7 is in. §9 lists all three options (enlarge the state; drop (A); carry the tension) with their costs. It records two facts, neither a T1 choice:
   - The observer is $O^u$ (Paper 1's object, a lock), and the flag lives in the process state.
   - The flag's only effect is to suppress branching at jump-reached states. So T1 concerns **whether** EPP1 applies, not what weights it gives when it does.
3. **#154 §4.4's T2 remarks.** Claude's observation, that EPP1 is the count at the depth-1 cut anchored at the current state while (M2) counts at a distant named cut, is recorded as a fact about **both** rules, with both costs attached:
   - The depth-1 anchor is the only cut whose weights are functions of the current state. That fits note 6, but EPP1 is a named postulate, which strains note 9.
   - A distant cut is what gives co-existing distinct evolutions equal weight (Paper 1). But the cut is a named extra, which strains note 9, and unless it is anchored, its implied next-step weight depends on the cut, which strains note 6.

   "Fits note 6" and "makes co-existing evolutions equal" appear together, and neither is used to decide.
