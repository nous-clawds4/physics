Here the direction is read as a future unit timelike $u$, **modulo germ isotropy** (§2). No “when branching is discussed”: the direction is always part of the object.
- **Paper 1, stated plainly (one sentence, to appear verbatim in prose §1):** Paper 1’s **object is kept** unchanged; its **motion is revised** (Option E residue: discontinuous histories whose jumps carry no proper time); its **measure is revised** (per-vertex EPP1 replaces the count, so Paper 1’s uniqueness-from-countability thesis is **not carried**) — unless the §9 open alternative (count at a named cut) is chosen instead.

## 2. Ontology

Carry Paper 1 object locks untouched. Precise working definitions for review are here; `mathematical-framework.md` yields where it disagrees.

- **Metric germ** $O=j_p^\infty g$; equivalently a local simply-connected analytic patch (germ-level identity of presentations; not maximal-$M$ uniqueness). Smooth is not enough; analyticity required.
- **Gauge / lump identity:** pointed analytic diffeomorphism renaming the same local patch = same lump.
- **Oriented observer (isotropy restored):** $O^u$ is the class of (presentation, $p$, $u$), with $u$ future-directed unit timelike, under pointed analytic diffeomorphisms carrying $u$ — i.e. **$u$ modulo germ isotropy**. Null observers excluded. Without the quotient a lump has no canonical tangent space, and $V$, $\mathrm{succ}_R$, $|B_R|$, EPP1 weights are not functions of physical data (C9: flat lump, $H^3=SO^+(1,3)/SO(3)$ collapses to one point; $|B_R|$ 3 vs 2).
- **Vertex space** $V$ := the space of oriented observers $O^u$. $V$ **is Paper 1’s observer space**, with Paper 1’s “time direction” **read as** $u$ (a reading, stated as such: Paper 1 distinguishes its observer from Gielen–Wise’s 4-velocity on a *given* metric; here $u$ sits on a lump, not on a given spacetime, and $V$ is not Gielen–Wise’s 7-manifold).
- $\mathrm{Obs}$ := forgetful image (lumps without $u$), used only where orientation is not in play. Not Gielen–Wise; not Larsson QJT.
- **$M$:** connected real-analytic Lorentzian 4-manifold; Einstein not assumed. **Analyticity cost (restored, both halves):** mathematically, one infinite jet rigidly determines $g$ on connected analytic $M$ — why layer (i) is a singleton and type (ii) is extra. **Physically:** no localised geometric degrees of freedom of the smooth kind; generic smooth metrics (and generic smooth GR solutions) excluded; **matter fields open, as in Paper 1** (“Matter fields are open”).
- **World** $W=(M,p)$; **$E_W/E_M$:** relative ensembles; occurrences of the same lump, not extra vertices, not a second observer; no whole-$W$ reconstruction from $O$; sibling points = multiplicity, not fork.
- **Ensemble size vs measure (§4.4 item 6):** by the §5.1 continuum theorem $E_W(O)$ is at least a continuum; Bertrand concerns ratios on it, not its cardinality.
- **Identity vs timing:** the ensemble does not vote on identity (Paper 1 claim kept). Ensemble-timed vertex selection would be a timing vote / E-smuggling — not used.
- **Ensemble hygiene (open):** inextendible/maximal members? Inextendibility alone does not restore discreteness (C25, cosmic string).
- **Lock-side (defined, §4.4 item 3):** a property is lock-side iff it is determined by the oriented germ $O^u$ alone (jet data and $u$; hence also by the curvelet $\gamma_{O^u}$, which $O^u$ fixes), without reference to members of $E_W$ or to history data (e.g. an origin of $\tau$).
- **Compatibility (sketch, time-oriented, §4.4 item 5):** $O'^{u'}$ is an allowable future of $O^u$ iff some connected analytic world has occurrences $p$, $p'$ joined by a **future-directed** timelike geodesic from $p$ to $p'$. Not assumed transitive. If kept, it is realisation (a) (§4.2). (“Mutual compatibility” deleted.)

## 3. Type (i) vs type (ii)

- **Type (i) (defined by path, §4.4 item 3):** a segment whose oriented-observer path is the curvelet $\gamma_{O^u}$ (the germs along the timelike geodesic with initial data $O^u$). World-independent: CWS, which switches worlds, is a type-(i) package because its path is still $\gamma_{O^u}$. No analytic delayed fork.
- **Type (ii):** pairs that are not successive oriented germs along $\gamma_{O^u}$. At a type-(ii) jump the history is **discontinuous in oriented observer space** $V$; the lump path in $\mathrm{Obs}$ may be constant (C10: ESU bare boost).
- **Oriented split (§4.4 item 4):** $(O^u,O'^{u'})$ is type (i) iff $O'^{u'}=\gamma_{O^u}(\tau)$ as oriented germ for some $\tau>0$ in the curvelet’s **maximal analytic domain** (not a world-dependent lifetime; hole-punching makes lifetimes arbitrary). $\tau=0$ is the identity, not a pair. Pairs reached only at $\tau<0$ are backward, not future continuations; whether $R$ may contain them is not decided here (it is not needed: (a), if adopted, is future-directed). Every other pair is type (ii), including bare orientation jumps at fixed lump (which are ordinary type-(ii) candidates; no separate selection rule).
- **Not restored:** 2007 geodesic-generated tree as theorem of hitchhiking. **Not restarted:** leftover $R$-property inventory.
- **Pad pedigree:** type-(ii) pad = author’s 2005 / Strayhorn3 transition / random-walk hypothesis under a new name (HV smell concrete; SHPMP 2008 referee’s hidden-variable reading kept loud, §4.3, §10).
- **Terminology:** “segment” = type-(i) piece; “edge” = element of $R$ only.

## 4. Motion: CWS baseline; Option E residue

### 4.1 Type (i) baseline — CWS singleton

- CWS 1–4: piecewise geodesic lifts; switch worlds at a shared oriented germ; path in $V$ is $C^\infty$; orientation does not jump.
- **Proposition (CWS singleton):** under CWS and either named path-germ equivalence — (E1) equal infinite-order path-jet at the start, or (E2) agreement on a common initial interval — $|B_{\mathrm{CWS}}(O^u)|=1$. EPP1 empty under CWS alone.
- **Proof sketch (restored to the outline, §4.4 item 2):** (1) every CWS piece starting at $O^u$ is a geodesic lift through the shared oriented germ $O^u$; (2) the geodesic with initial data $(p,u)$ is unique and its germ path is $\gamma_{O^u}$ (Prop. 3.2 of `papers/geodesic-lifts.md`), independent of the world; (3) so all allowed paths agree on an initial interval (E2) and hence in infinite-order jet (E1): one class. World switching changes lifetime, not path. **Minkowski instance (C28):** flat lump modulo isotropy is one oriented germ; its curvelet is constant; one class.
- CWS is not the branching law. The escape is not “reject infinite-order matching”; the singleton is shared germ + uniqueness + equivalence at the start.

### 4.2 Option E residue — public join / branching pad (working, abandonable)

- **Segments:** analytic-geodesic lifts (type (i)) between jumps. **Joins:** discontinuous type-(ii) jumps + graph incidence. **Arms are the elements of $B_R$** (not the out-star $\mathrm{succ}_R$; otherwise EPP1 weights sum to $|\mathrm{succ}_R|/(1+|\mathrm{succ}_R|)$).
- **Not Option F as branching mechanism (C27):** finite-$k$ matching is automatic on the continue arm, vacuous at $k=1$ between distinct lumps, prohibitive at $k\ge2$ for curvature-changing jumps. It cannot create a branching edge. Pairing C with E buys no branching power.
- **Adopted (working Postulate, abandonable):** $R\ne\emptyset$ — existence of type-(ii) edges, with no $R$ specified. This files Paper 1’s non-uniqueness premise (#128 §4.1 “Motion”). $R=\emptyset$ is CWS. Drop the package → layer (i) unchanged, and Paper 1’s “the evolution is not unique” is false.
- **Composition:**
  1. $R\subseteq V^\tau\times V$ — a **declared constraint on $R$’s source set** (the “when” half of any star law, never the targets); it inherits named-extra status from the §5.1 scalar choice. Without it every point of a curvelet is a potential attachment point (continuum).
  2. **History (strict alternation, option (A) of #128, in Geometry’s form — see item 7):** after a type-(ii) jump, a type-(i) segment of **positive** proper time is traversed before the next branch decision. Proper time is carried only along segments; a history is therefore no longer Paper 1’s $\tau$-parametrised curve — it is a sequence of segments in $V$ separated by jumps that carry no $\tau$ (discontinuous in oriented observer space).
  3. $R$ contains no type-(i) pairs; out-degree = type-(ii) out-degree.
  4. $\mathrm{succ}_R(O^u):=\{O'^{u'}:(O^u,O'^{u'})\in R\}$.
  5. **First-edge equivalence (#117 wording):** two allowed continuations from $O^u$ are equivalent iff both open with the same edge of $R$, or both open with a positive-length segment along $\gamma_{O^u}$ — whatever they do later (C11: 3 classes at the Kretschmann periastron, not 5).

This enlarged state is the cost of (A) and is stated, not hidden. **[Geometry over #128:** #128 offered (A) as removing arrival dependence; #129 shows it moves it into EPP1’s domain.]
  8. **No-Zeno (named clause):** along each history, branch points are finite in every bounded interval of accumulated proper time; a history whose branch points accumulate at finite accumulated $\tau$ (including at a singular endpoint of one segment, C8) **ends there** and is outside EPP1’s domain.

- **Direction vs walk:** under (A) the kernel acts per visit, so walk semantics (EPP1 per visit) is the reading in force. The Schwarzschild loop (successive periastra are one oriented germ) shows a direction hypothesis on $R$ could not make histories acyclic anyway. No acyclicity clause imported.
- **Spacetime realisation (honesty cost table; both kept open, not interchangeable):**
  - **(a)** Every type-(ii) edge $(O^u,O'^{u'})$ is realised: some connected real-analytic world contains occurrences $p$ of $O$ and $p'$ of $O'$ joined by a future-directed timelike geodesic from $p$ to $p'$. **Endpoint-orientation convention:** the realising geodesic leaves $p$ along some $u''$, necessarily $u''\ne u$ (if it left along $u$ it would be $\gamma_{O^u}$, the pair would be type (i), and $R=\emptyset$; C3), and **arrives at $p'$ with tangent $u'$** (the target orientation is the arrival tangent). So every (a)-edge factors as a bare orientation change at $O$ followed by type-(i) transport. **The realising geodesic labels admissibility; it carries none of the history’s proper time** (jumps carry no $\tau$, item 2). By §2 rigidity $O'$ lies in the **analytic-continuation class** of $O$ (up to monodromy; not “the maximal analytic world”, which need not be unique). Hence (a) **confines $R$ to one analytic-continuation class**: it **forbids cross-continuation jumps** (flat→FLRW, the retired toy’s edge; Schwarzschild $M=1\to M=2$, C1) and **permits curvature-changing jumps within the class** (Schwarzschild $M=1$, $r=10\to8$, C2). The true cost is at least as heavy as v0.5 stated, but different: the forbidden jumps are exactly the across-continuation jumps a measurement picture would want.
  - **In-patch continuum (soft Prop. 13, restored):** the (a)-admissible targets of one $O^u$ form a continuum within the class (C4). (a) is a necessary condition, not a law; it does not select a countable star.
  - **(b)** Cross-continuation edges allowed; any edge between distinct continuation classes has taken (b).
  - Compatibility (§2), if kept, is (a).
  - **[Geometry over #128]** #128’s second (a)-route (static recurrence of the $c=0.01$ target $r=10.108$ via Avez–Seifert) is valid at lump level but not shown at oriented level with arrival tangent $=u'$; it is not used as a pin.

### 4.3 Cost of $R$ (loud — hidden-variable smell)

- **Soft Prop. 13 (honest report, qualifier restored):** no law of $R$ written. Lock-side candidates examined in-house give trivial maps or a continuum of successors. Locked data *can* determine off-curvelet stars — the Kretschmann rule’s stars are a readout of the germ — but **kinematics does not single out a dynamics**: what the germ does not supply is *which* readout. A countable star is a new physical choice of readout, not forced by the germ.
- **Singleton-or-continuum dilemma** in the target direction lives in the law of $R$; escapes only by unspecified or selection extras. Motivation problem, not a no-go.
- **Hidden-variable reading (loud):** $R$ + EPP1 under the chance reading is a stochastic hidden-variable jump process on $V$ (2005 pedigree; SHPMP 2008 referee). Not a local HV that beats Bell.
- No $1/|B_R|$ implied by mere existence of $R$.

## 7. EPP1 vs EPP2

- The word “egalitarianism” is banned.
- **EPP1 (named postulate; SHPMP loud):** at a state $(O^u,a)$ where the hard gate holds, weight $1/|A(O^u,a)|$ on each arm (after a segment: $1/|B_R(O^u)|$). 2005 per-branch pedigree. **Domain empty because no $R$ is adopted** (§4.4 item 1). Not implied by bare $R$; not $|a|^2$-tuned; not Everett-vs-DW.
- **Reading of the weight (restored, re-costed):**
  - **Co-existence (typicality) reading — adopted:** arms co-exist; EPP1 is a measure on co-existing histories (a product measure on completed histories, projectively consistent, Ionescu-Tulcea). **Cost:** it gives co-existing distinct evolutions **unequal** weight — on the uneven tree $A_i$ get $1/6$ each and $B_1$ gets $1/2$ (C12). The co-existence *ontology* matches Paper 1; the *weighting* does not. (“Can still match Paper 1’s spirit” deleted.) Rideout–Sorkin sits beside this reading too (measure on completed histories).
  - **Chance reading — defined, not adopted:** one history is followed; at each decision it takes each arm of $A(O^u,a)$ with chance $1/|A(O^u,a)|$ — a Markov jump rule on $(O^u,a)$, the random-walk pedigree (§3); neighbours Kent / GRW / Bell-type jumps.
- **Label vs Paper 1 (named cut, per Geometry):** per-vertex **departs** from Paper 1’s count. For a named finite cut $C$ (every root-to-end path meets it once; depth and proper-time cuts both qualify) with $D(v)=\prod_{w<v}\deg(w)$: per-vertex gives $1/D(v)$, count gives $1/|C|$; **they agree on $C$ iff $D$ is constant on $C$** (then $D\equiv|C|$). They agree at every depth $\le N$ iff the tree is generation-regular to depth $N-1$ (C13); agreement is not monotone in depth; at proper-time cuts they can disagree even on generation-regular trees (C14). The count’s value for a fixed prefix changes with the cut; per-vertex does not. Every comparison names its cut.
- **Dead-end convention (named, per Geometry):** (i) truncated paths are included in $C$ with $D$ along their actual length (consistent with unconditioned EPP1); (ii) survivors only, per-vertex conditioned on survival, is the alternative. Default (i); (i) and (ii) can disagree.
- **Limit (per Geometry):** with finite stars, depth-$n$ counts converge to per-vertex on every cylinder iff siblings are asymptotically balanced ($D_n(w)/D_n(w')\to1$); exact constancy along a cofinal sequence of cuts is sufficient, not necessary (C15; bounded-degree case not settled).
- **Finite cuts:** once clause (2) holds per history, König’s lemma makes every proper-time cut a finite tree (finite stars), so both rules can be compared at a named $\tau$-cut (C29).
- **Named constraint (not a law) — no-jump-dominance (J), from #129 §4:** with recurrent outs, $|\mathrm{succ}_R|=m$ at every decision, the pure-geodesic history has EPP1 weight $(1+m)^{-n}$ after $n$ decisions ($3^{-10}=1.69\times10^{-5}$ for $m=2$), and the number of jumps is Binomial$(n,m/(1+m))$. EPP1 weights cannot be tuned. So if typical histories are to show GR geodesic motion over an observed window $[0,T]$, any future $R$ must satisfy: $J(T)=\sum_{\text{decisions in }[0,T]}m_v/(1+m_v)$ small along typical histories (each decision with $m_v\ge1$ contributes $\ge1/2$), so either **(J1)** vertices with $\mathrm{succ}_R\ne\emptyset$ are sparse (their number over observed windows $\lesssim$ tolerance, far below the number of $V^\tau$ vertices), or **(J2)** jumps are observationally null: every target lies within a stated tolerance of the continuation in a named topology **on curvelets** (not merely on germs at the vertex). The Kretschmann rule fails both (C17). Recorded as a constraint, not a demand to write $R$. The continue arm has no privilege under EPP1 (§4.4 item 10).
- **Exhaustion:** if countable stars ever allowed, name the exhaustion; default finite.
- **EPP2 (foil; label restored):** weight $\propto$ post-branch $|E_W|$ or $|E_M|$ — an ensemble vote. **Departure from Paper 1 if promoted** (Paper 1: “each distinct evolution counts as one”; extra weighting is new theory).
- **Product ansatz (Hope; content restored from v0.4, §4.4 item 7):** if post-branch ensembles factor with a shared reservoir, EPP2 might collapse toward EPP1; fails if the measured distinction locks other topology or arms admit different external structure; even then $\infty/\infty$ needs a named exhaustion. Not Stoica ($|\Psi|$ not in the count).
