## 1. Abstract / purpose

This paper states an ontology of observers as analytic metric jets, equivalently local simply-connected analytic patches, oriented as $O^u$ when branching is discussed. Alongside the elementary object it introduces *relative* ensembles of worlds and spacetimes compatible with a germ. It does not reconstruct a whole spacetime $W$ from an observer $O$.

It states two motion layers. **Type (i)** is geodesic hitchhiking, with Continuous World Switching (CWS) kept only as a null baseline: under CWS, $|B|=1$. **Type (ii)** is a transition structure $R$ on oriented lumps — the across-worlds / branching launching pad, working and abandonable. The branch set $B(O^u)$ is redefined under type (ii) so that an equal-probability postulate can have a domain *in principle*. No controlled discrete example is claimed yet; EPP1’s domain is empty until one exists.

**EPP1** (equal weight on discrete arms of $B(O^u)$) is separated from **EPP2** (weight proportional to post-branch ensemble size). A product ansatz is named as a hope, not a theorem. A no-no-go sketch appears only with type (ii) on the table: illustration, not a Born derivation, not a claim that Bell is refuted.

Born and Einstein remain goals for later versions. They are not theorems of v0.3.

## 2. Ontology

Precise working definitions for review are the ones in this section. Informal living notes are kept in `mathematical-framework.md`; where that file disagrees, this section wins.

### Public convention

Paper 1’s locked elementary observer is an infinite jet of a real-analytic metric **plus** a future time direction, equivalently a simply-connected real-analytic patch determined by that jet. For this public paper the same object is presented with an explicit split between the metric lump and the choice of time direction.

**Metric germ.** Write $O = j_p^\infty$ for the infinite jet of a real-analytic Lorentzian metric $g$ at a point $p$. Equivalently, $O$ may be presented as a simply-connected real-analytic patch obtained by analytic continuation of that jet — a *local* identification of presentations of the same germ, not a claim that a global manifold $M$ is uniquely determined, and not a claim about maximal analytic extensions of $M$. Smooth is not enough: in the smooth category a jet need not pick a unique local lump. Analyticity is required for the jet/patch identification used here.

**Gauge / lump identity.** Two presentations that differ only by a pointed analytic diffeomorphism renaming the same local patch are the same lump. The public object is that lump. It is **not** a raw coordinate jet with gauge “deferred forever.” Details of the lump quotient remain in-house; the identity claim does not.

**Oriented observer.** Write $O^u = (O,u)$ for that lump together with a unit timelike vector $u$ (null observers are excluded in this draft). Nothing in the *bare metric jet* prefers a particular $u$ among the unit hyperboloid at $p$, up to the isotropy of the germ. A physical instantaneous observer still includes a time direction. **Branching and EPP1 are about oriented observers.** Write $B(O^u)$. Bare $O$ is used when orientation is not yet chosen.

**Orientation in spacetime.** The vector $u$ lives in $T_pM$ at an occurrence of the germ.

**Orientation in observer space (type i).** For type-(i) curves, write $V = \mathrm{Lift}(u)$ in the **path-space** reading: curves and $C^\infty$ matching along paths, without locking a tangent-space structure on $\mathrm{Obs}$. Whether $\mathrm{Obs}$ admits charts remains open (§9).

**Orientation on the type-(ii) graph (convention).** Graph vertices are **oriented observers** $O^u$. An edge is a pair $(O^u,O'^{u'})$. A type-(ii) edge is one whose underlying lumps $(O,O')$ do not arise as successive germs along a geodesic lift. Orientation is therefore vertex data, not an afterthought on bare-$O$ vertices. (Other conventions are possible; this is the one used below so that $B(O^u)$ is not schematic.)

**Observer space.** $\mathrm{Obs}$ is the space of lumps (oriented or not as marked). It is not Gielen and Wise’s 7-manifold of future-directed unit 4-velocities — same English phrase, different object. It is not Larsson’s Quantum Jet Theory: those objects are $p$-jets of fields about an expansion point or trajectory, not the infinite jet of the metric, and they carry no counting measure of the kind discussed here.

**Spacetime.** An $M$ is a connected real-analytic Lorentzian 4-manifold. Topology (for example $\pi_1$) may vary across the ensemble. Einstein’s equation is **not** assumed on $M$ in v0.3.

**World.** A world is $W = (M,p)$: a spacetime together with an anchor point (point of view) $p$.

**World ensemble.** $E_W(O)$ (or $E_W(O^u)$ when orientation matters) is the set of pointed worlds compatible with the germ (and with $u$ when oriented). The same $M$ may appear more than once if several distinct anchors carry the same germ. This is a **relative ensemble**: not a second elementary observer, not a vote of global topology on when evolutions split, and **not** a reconstruction of a whole $W$ from $O$. Ensemble members are occurrences of the *same* lump, not extra vertices of $\mathrm{Obs}$.

**Spacetime ensemble.** $E_M(O)$ is the corresponding multiset of underlying manifolds $M$. The same firewall applies.

**Sibling points.** Other anchors in the same $M$ with the same germ are sibling points to the chosen anchor — multiplicity in the ensemble, not a fork of the elementary object.

**Compatibility.** Say $O_j$ is an *allowable future* of $O_i$ if there exist a spacetime $M$, occurrences of the two germs at points $p_1,p_2\in M$, and a finite **timelike** geodesic from $p_1$ to $p_2$. Write $O_i\Leftrightarrow O_j$ when each is an allowable future of the other. Compatibility is not automatically transitive. Its transitive closure is reachability if world switching (or type-(ii) edges) is allowed. This remains a sketch; refinements are open (§9).

**Note.** The intersection of distinct *world* ensembles is empty. Co-occurrence of manifolds lives in $E_M$.

## 3. Type (i) vs type (ii)

**Type (i) — in-world hitchhiking.** The Obs curve is the germ along a timelike geodesic in a fixed $M$. One curvelet $\gamma_O$ (resp. $\gamma_{O^u}$) per oriented germ. Analytic lifts in a single $M$ do not delayed-fork: the identity theorem forbids a split that appears only after a finite proper-time delay while the germs still agree. Relative ensembles stay relative.

**Type (ii) — across-worlds.** Extra edges of a transition structure that are **not** successive germs along a geodesic lift. Histories are finite **graph-paths** on oriented lumps. Delayed forks are allowed as graph-paths: common prefix, then distinct vertices. Those paths need **not** be analytic curves in $\mathrm{Obs}$.

**Not restored.** The 2007 picture that ran a geodesic in each world compatible with $O$ and superimposed the lifts as a tree is *not* this layer. That construction remains killed as a theorem of hitchhiking alone.

**Not restarted.** The leftover inventory of formal properties of $R$ (acyclicity, finite descendant sets, connectedness, and so on) is not imported into this public paper.

## 4. Motion: CWS as baseline; type-(ii) $R$ as branching pad

### 4.1 Type (i) baseline — CWS / geodesic hitchhiking (null result)

CWS as written in v0.2.1 remains a coherent type-(i) package: allowed paths in $\mathrm{Obs}$ are piecewise geodesic lifts; the traveler may switch worlds at a shared germ; the Obs path is $C^\infty$; orientation does not jump.

**Proposition (CWS singleton).** Under CWS 1–4 and either named equivalence on path germs (infinite-order path-jet at $0$, or agreement on a common initial interval), $|B_{\mathrm{CWS}}(O^u)|=1$: the class of the unique geodesic curvelet through $O^u$. World switching can change lifetime; it does **not** change the Obs-path. EPP1 has empty domain under CWS alone.

**Demotion.** CWS is **not** the public branching law. It is the honest type-(i) null baseline. The older substance note — that infinite-order matching at switches might thin EKT cartoons into fewer Obs arms — is backwards under CWS: matching is automatic, and switches are invisible as forks in $\mathrm{Obs}$.

**Rejected as primary branching options.** Direction Switching (non-geodesic $C^\infty$ pieces with orientation changing only infinitesimally) and instantaneous jumps in $u$ are not the main public story. Both tend toward a continuum of arms unless a grain is invented.

### 4.2 Type (ii) launching pad — transition structure $R$ (working, abandonable)

**Definition 7 (transition structure).** A pair $(V,R)$ with $V\subseteq\{O^u\}$ (oriented lumps, per the convention of §2) and $R\subseteq V\times V$: a directed graph. A **type-(ii) edge** is a pair $(O^u,O'^{u'})\in R$ whose underlying lumps do **not** arise as successive germs along a geodesic lift of $O$ in a real-analytic $W$.

**Definition 8 (delayed fork).** Two graph-paths delayed-fork if they share a common prefix of positive length, then occupy distinct vertices.

**Adopted (working, abandonable).** Existence of type-(ii) edges as the across-worlds / branching stand-in. This is extra structure, not a theorem of hitchhiking. If the hypothesis is a blind alley, drop type (ii); layer (i) is unchanged.

**Proposition 13 (cost — keep loud).** The locked data at $O$ — the lump, the local patch, the curvelet $\gamma_O$, and the relative ensemble $E(O)$ — determine at most type-(i) successive pairs. They do **not** determine a type-(ii) edge to a lump off $\gamma_O$. The law of which edges exist is extra. A neighborhood-law for $R$ waits on $\mathrm{Obs}$ as a *space* (topology or atlas); gauge-as-space is open. No $1/N$ is placed on edges. Weights are a further hypothesis, not implied by existence of $R$.

This honesty is deliberate. An extra undetermined $R$ raises a hidden-variable / typicality desk-reject smell relative to programs that keep $|\psi|^2$ in charge of the measure. The paper does **not** answer that smell by pretending $R$ is fixed by the germ, by putting $1/N$ on edges, or by claiming to beat Bell with a local HV on $\mathrm{Obs}$. It names the cost and keeps type (ii) abandonable.

## 5. Branches $B(O^u)$ under type (ii)

An equal-probability postulate needs a definite notion of branch. Under CWS that notion collapses to a singleton. Under type (ii) it is redefined as follows.

**Allowed continuation.** Fix an oriented observer $O^u$. An allowed continuation at $O^u$ is a germ of a finite graph-path in $(V,R)$ starting at the vertex $O^u$.

**Equivalence.** Two allowed continuations are equivalent if they determine the same one-step star at $O^u$, or — weaker working option to be fixed in examples — the same path-germ of finite length. This is **not** CWS infinite-order path-jet equivalence. A further quotient by a named discrete invariant (for example a recorded bit) is allowed only if such an invariant is adopted; none is adopted in §2, so any bit language stays in §6 as picture.

**Branch set.** $B(O^u)$ is the set of equivalence classes of allowed continuations. A branch point is a vertex with out-degree $\ge 2$, or with $\ge 2$ inequivalent one-step successors under the chosen equivalence.

**What $B(O^u)$ is not.** It is not a Fermi-slice neighborhood of the lump (typically a continuum). It is not $E_M$ or $E_W$. It is not a delayed analytic fork inside one $M$. It is **not** $B_{\mathrm{CWS}}(O^u)$, which is a singleton.

**Hard gate.** EPP1 applies **only** when $B(O^u)$ is **discrete** (finite or countable). A continuum of arms is not an equal-probability story.

**Honesty / stress test.** Adopting type (ii) makes $|B(O^u)|\ge 2$ *structurally possible*. It does not exhibit it. v0.3 claims **no** controlled example of $2\le|B(O^u)|<\infty$ (or countable $B$ with named exhaustion) under an explicit $R$. Until such an example exists, EPP1’s domain is empty.

**Firewall.** No sentence in this paper treats $|B|\ge 2$ as achieved under CWS.

## 6. Measurement cartoon (optional picture — not ontology)

This section is **picture / cartoon only**. It is not ontology and not a Paper 1 lock. Matter fields remain open; nothing in §2 carries a “bit.”

One may *picture* particles as 4-geons, with type loosely tied to topology (especially $\pi_1$), and allow microscopic twisting as rhetoric. That rhetoric is Hadley-adjacent. This paper does **not** adopt 4-geons as the definition of $O$. Topology-as-particle-type is picture or later hope, not §2.

An optional extra stipulation — named, not forced — is that distinct $M$ in an ensemble may be required to have distinct topology.

**Interesting branches (program talk only, and only with type (ii) on the table).** One may imagine a recorded bit entering the local description while the system is far; mixed ensemble before, arms purified in that bit after. “Same spin bit” would then be a further quotient on arms. None of that is supplied by the ontology of germs alone, and none of it is a claim that Bell is refuted.

**Locality slogan.** “Local in $\mathrm{Obs}$, nonlocal in spacetime” is a **program claim**, not a theorem, and not a spacetime-locality notion that denies Bell’s hypotheses. It must not be read as a local hidden-variable model on $\mathrm{Obs}$ that beats Bell.

## 7. EPP1 vs EPP2 and the product ansatz

Prefer the labels **EPP1** and **EPP2**. Do **not** reuse the 2008 word “egalitarianism.”

**EPP1 (working postulate for narrative).** At a branch point, equal weight on the discrete arms of $B(O^u)$ as redefined under type (ii). This is chosen for story, not forced by the ontology of germs. **Empty domain until a discrete controlled example exists** — v0.3 does not claim one.

**EPP2a.** Weight proportional to $|E_W|$ on each arm’s post-branch world ensemble — once an arm-to-ensemble map is defined. No such map is fixed here; EPP2 remains schematic hope.

**EPP2b.** The same with $|E_M|$.

EPP1 and EPP2 agree only under extra structure.

**Product ansatz (hope, not theorem).** Suppose post-branch ensembles factor as $\{\mathrm{A},\mathrm{B}\}\times\mathcal{R}$ with the same reservoir $\mathcal{R}$ on both arms. Then a bijection or matching density might make EPP2 collapse to EPP1. That hope fails if the measured bit locks other topology, or if the arms admit different external decoctions. Even when $\mathcal{R}$ is shared, $\infty/\infty$ needs a named exhaustion. Ensemble cardinality is itself underdetermined without further structure (Bertrand’s problem); do not treat raw $|E|$ as a finished measure.

**Not Stoica.** Counting here is not Hilbert-space ontic counting with $|\psi|^2$ already in the density. No $|a|^2$ and no $1/N$ are put in by hand as multiplicity.

## 8. No-no-go sketch (not a Born derivation)

The toy is available only with type (ii) on the table. Under CWS alone there are not two Obs arms to draw.

**Purpose.** An existence sketch: an Obs-branching model built from graph-paths need not be a spacetime-local hidden-variable theory of the Bell sort; and non-50-50 patterns can be drawn as trees on $(V,R)$.

**Not** a derivation of Born. Hand-built trees that match $|a|^2$ are illustrations only.

**Not** “Bell is refuted” or “Bell’s hypotheses fail as a theorem of type (ii).”

**Not** an Everett-versus-Deutsch–Wallace probability paper.

**Not** a local HV on $\mathrm{Obs}$ that inherits Born by fiat.

**Circularity wall.** EPP plus an engineered tree is not a proof. A later hope is that a written $R$, together with whatever forces discrete $B(O^u)$, *forces* weights. That hope is not claimed here.

## 10. What this paper does not claim

- No Born derivation; no Einstein derivation.
- No $|B(O^u)|\ge 2$ under CWS; CWS singleton is the null result.
- No controlled discrete branch example yet under type (ii).
- No law of $R$; no grain locked; no $1/N$ by hand; no leftover $R$-property mill.
- No 2007 geodesic-generated tree restored.
- No preferred-basis solution beyond arms of $B(O^u)$ under a named equivalence (once $B$ is under type (ii) and discrete).
- No grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$ from continuity alone.
- No Bell-refuted claim; no Everett-versus-Deutsch–Wallace framing; no local-HV-beats-Bell reading.
- Objects are analytic jets / oriented observers as named — not decoherence branches with Born already in the quantum state.
- Not Stoica; not Gielen–Wise; not Larsson QJT.
- 4-geons are picture, not lock-side ontology.
- Paper 1 untouched; in-house theorem numbers are background, not required reading.
