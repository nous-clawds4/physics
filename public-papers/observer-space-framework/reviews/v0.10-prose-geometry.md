**The Kretschmann sentence (R1).** It reads: "a through-throat path gives it only at apastra with $\varepsilon=2\Delta\ell$ (the Proposition of §8.2), where the germ is recurrent."
- **"Only at apastra" is too narrow.** Under "through", the target equals the source iff $\varepsilon(r_0)=2\Delta\ell(r_0)$. That condition depends on $r_0$ alone, so it holds at every turning point at those radii, periastra included (an unbound orbit's periastron at $r_0=2786.4692$, for instance). #306 R4 made the same correction for #305. "Apastra" is #292's own wording, carried over.
- **"Where the germ is recurrent" is true only for some of these germs.** The near-throat self-target, $\approx2.000361$, is the apastron of a plunging orbit, which is not recurrent. #292 gets recurrence from a step the draft drops: "A decision there is reached along a segment, so it sits on a bound orbit (a plunging orbit's apastron is reached only by the jump that lands on it)". That step rests on (A) (§8.2 items 2 and 7: a vertex reached by a jump carries no decision). Every orbit after the root is entered by a jump that lands on a turning point. Plunging and unbound orbits have only that one turning point, so a decision is possible only on a bound orbit, whose germs recur.
- **The fix.** R1 restores the step, names (A), and says "turning points". The conclusion is unchanged.
