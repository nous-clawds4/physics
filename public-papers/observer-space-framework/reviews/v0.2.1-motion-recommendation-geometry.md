## 1. Confirm the singleton HOLE (formal)

**Proposition (CWS singleton).** Fix $O^u=(O,u)$. Let CWS be postulates 1–4 of `v0.2.1-prose.md` §4, and let $B(O^u)$ be §5 with either named equivalence (infinite-order path-jet at $0$, or agreement on a common initial interval). Then $B(O^u)=\{[\gamma_{O^u}]\}$, where $\gamma_{O^u}$ is the unique geodesic curvelet of Prop. 3.2 in `papers/geodesic-lifts.md`.

**Sketch.**

1. *First piece fixed.* An allowed continuation has a first positive piece that is a geodesic lift in some world from an occurrence of $(O,u)$. Analytic ODE uniqueness on the simply-connected patch (and local uniqueness of the metric germ) gives a unique lift germ $\gamma_{O^u}$ independent of which $W$ realises the piece (`geodesic-lifts.md` Prop. 3.2).
2. *Equivalence at $0$.* Default equivalence: same infinite path-jet at $0$ $\Rightarrow$ one class. Weaker equivalence: agreement on a positive initial interval $\Rightarrow$ one class. Switches unused.
3. *Switches do not fork later.* At a switch time the continuing piece is again a geodesic lift from the then-current oriented germ. By Cor. 3.1 / Thm 3 the lift is real-analytic in $\tau$; identity theorem identifies the $W_2$ lift with the $W_1$ continuation on the common domain (`patch-edge.md` Thm 7 at the patch edge). Switching can change lifetime; it cannot change the Obs-path.

**Escapes that fail under CWS as written.** Patch-edge switch, incomplete geodesic, sibling anchors, distinct topologies/coverings, null pieces, switches accumulating at $0$, EKT matter cartoons — each fails for the reasons Claude §1.3 lists; Geometry does not reopen them.

**In-house alignment.** Prop. 3.2, Cor. 3.1, Thm 7, Thms 8–9 (`phi-straits.md`) already say hitchhiking / analytic-curve motion from one $O$ yields a singleton evolution set. Public CWS requires every piece to be a geodesic lift, so it is type (i) in $\mathrm{Obs}$ with optional change of lifetime — not a type-(ii) fork. Geometry’s earlier v0.2 PASS checked definitional hygiene, not whether $B$ is ever non-trivial. Claude is right to call that a HOLE.

**Kill attempt.** No kill. Do not soften analyticity, drop identity theorem, or smuggle non-geodesic pieces while keeping the CWS label.

## 2. Recommended public motion revision

### 2.1 Primary recommendation: type-(ii) $R$ launching pad

For v0.3 public prose, **stop presenting CWS as the branching motion law.** Adopt, as the public across-worlds launching pad, the structure already named in-house:

- **Layer (i) unchanged.** Geodesic hitchhiking: one curvelet $\gamma_O$ per $O$. Thms 2–9 on those curves stand. Relative ensembles $E_W$/$E_M$ stay relative; no whole-$W$ from $O$.
- **Layer (ii) working, abandonable.** A transition structure $(V,R)$ on lumps (Def. 7): edges $(O,O')\in R$ that **do not** arise as successive germs along a geodesic lift. Histories are finite graph-paths. Delayed forks (Def. 8) are allowed as graph-paths with a common prefix then distinct vertices. Those paths need **not** be analytic curves in $\mathrm{Obs}$, so Thm 2 / Thm 9 do not forbid them (Prop. 12).
- **$B$ under type (ii).** Redefine allowed continuations as germs of graph-paths in $(V,R)$ starting at $O^u$ (orientation carried as data on vertices/edges as needed), with equivalence of one-step stars or path-germs stated explicitly. Branch points are vertices with out-degree $\ge 2$ in the graph sense (or $\ge 2$ inequivalent one-step successors). **Do not claim $|B|\ge 2$ until $R$ is chosen with that property in a controlled example.**
- **CWS demoted.** Keep CWS (or geodesic hitchhiking) as the **type-(i) baseline / null result**: under CWS 1–4, $B=\{[\gamma_{O^u}]\}$; world switching changes lifetime, not path. Useful honesty; not the branching law.

This matches `papers/type-ii-adopted.md` and does not restore the 2007 geodesic-generated tree (Thm 11 still killed).

### 2.2 Alternatives considered (not recommended as primary)

| Option | What it buys | Cost / why not primary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| (a) Direction Switching / non-geodesic $C^\infty$ pieces | Delayed forks as curves | Default infinite-order equivalence at the fork germ is blind to smooth forks after a shared prefix; weaker equivalence typically yields a **continuum** of arms (Thms 17–18 territory). Needs a further grain or discrete cut to get countable $B$. |
| (b) Jump in orientation $u$ | Instant arms on the hyperboloid | Continuum of arms unless a grain on the unit hyperboloid is invented. |
| (c) Keep CWS, state singleton as proposition | Honest null result | Does not give EPP1 a domain; branching program waits on a different postulate anyway. Fine as §4 baseline, not as the branching law. |

Geometry recommends **(c) as baseline + type-(ii) $R$ as branching launching pad**, not (a) or (b) as the main public story.

### 2.3 What not to do

- Do not restart the leftover $R$-property mill (acyclicity, finite Desc, connectedness, …) inside the public paper.
- Do not write a law of $R$, a grain, $1/N$, $\Phi$, or Born.
- Do not touch Paper 1.
- Do not claim discrete countable $B$ is forced by adopting type (ii); only that the *structural* emptiness of delayed forks is lifted (graph-paths can fork), while *countable typicality* remains conditional (Prop. 13 + open grain / open law of $R$).

## 3. Cost paragraph (for the public paper)

**What is lost relative to CWS-as-branching.** The public story no longer gets branching “for free” from continuous world switching along geodesic lifts. CWS does not supply two arms in $\mathrm{Obs}$. The substance note in v0.2.1 §4 (infinite-order matching as a strong constraint that may thin EKT cartoons) is backwards under CWS: matching is automatic and switches are invisible in $\mathrm{Obs}$.

**What is gained.** Across-worlds motion that can delayed-fork in the elementary object: type-(ii) edges and graph-paths, already the in-house working hypothesis. Thm 2 is silent because those paths need not be analytic curves.

**What stays open (must be named in v0.3).**

1. **Law of $R$.** Which type-(ii) edges exist is not written. Locked data at $O$ do not determine them (Prop. 13).
2. **Grain.** Countable $B$ / countable $\mathrm{Path}$ is not forced by existence of $R$; countable out-degree is a further restriction on $R$ (Thm 31), not a grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$, and still extra.
3. **Gauge-as-space.** A neighborhood-law for $R$ waits on $\mathrm{Obs}$ as a space (topology / atlas); still open.
4. **Discrete $|B|\ge 2$ in a controlled example.** Adopting type (ii) makes $|B|\ge 2$ *possible*; it does not exhibit it. EPP1 still has empty domain until such an example (or an explicit finite/countable star) is named — without putting $1/N$ or a grain in by hand.
5. **Abandonable.** Type (ii) remains a working hypothesis; drop it and layer (i) is unchanged.

**Firewall sentence for drafts.** Until the type-(ii) postulate is named and $B$ is redefined under it, do not write as if $|B(O^u)|\ge 2$ under CWS.
