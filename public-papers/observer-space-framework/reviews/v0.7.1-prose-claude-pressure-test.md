**Census claim (ε/width at stuck orbits 0.163–0.999).** It is confirmed. Its definitions are:
- $\varepsilon=c\,K(r_p)^{-1/4}$ at the periastron, with $c=0.01$;
- width $=s(r_a)-s(r_p)$, the proper radial width;
- the population is the 29,119 reachable orbits of the census: 14,478 stuck (no (a)-admissible edge at either turning point) and 14,641 with an edge.

| classifier | stuck: min–max | with an edge: max | with-edge orbits above 0.163 | stuck orbits below the with-edge max |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| corrected (`c33r` = `classify2`) | 0.163087–0.999495 | 0.220089 | 3,850 of 14,641 | 572 of 14,478 |
| `kr.classify` | 0.163087–0.999495 | 0.312040 | 3,851 of 14,642 | 2,760 of 14,477 |

- **The maximum:** 0.999495 is 0.999 to three places. It is below 1 only for this ε and width. With ε taken at the apastron the maximum is 1.019, and with coordinate width it is 1.092.
- **Framing:** it is consistent with #169. Claude gives both ends (stuck orbits down to 0.163, orbits with an edge up to 0.220) and the two overlap counts, and concludes that there is no threshold.
- **Classifier dependence:** the one orbit the classifiers disagree on moves from stuck to "with an edge" under `kr.classify`, at ratio 0.312. That moves the with-edge max to 0.312 and the stuck-below count to 2,760. Claude's 0.220, 572 and 3,850 of 14,641 are the corrected values.
- **Wording (non-gating):** "branching stops at a ratio far below 1" is loose, since there is no stopping ratio. The next sentence says so. None of this is in the draft.
