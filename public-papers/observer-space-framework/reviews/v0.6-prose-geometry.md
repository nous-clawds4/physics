## 4. Rulings on flagged wording: ontology glosses

**Lump (§1 line 79, §2 "Metric germ", "Gauge / lump identity").** Accepted.
- $O=j^\infty_pg$, "equivalently … a local simply-connected analytic patch obtained by analytic continuation of that jet".
- It carries an explicit disclaimer: "a germ-level identification of presentations, not a claim that a maximal global manifold is unique".
- Identity is pointed analytic diffeomorphism, and "Gauge is this identity".
- There is no completed $W$, no chart, no finite-jet sufficiency, and analyticity is required.
- Obs is the lump without $u$, unoriented ✓.
- The oriented observer is $u$ modulo the pointed isometries of the germ: no gauge quotient beyond germ isotropy ✓.
- Nits (N1): in §1, "real-analytic *spacetime* metric" → "real-analytic *Lorentzian* metric". Optionally, "a local … patch" → "the simply-connected analytic patch $P(O)$ obtained by local continuation" (it is unique; the indefinite article undersells it).

**Curvelet (§3 line 121).** Accepted with a nit.
- "the oriented germs along the timelike geodesic with initial data $O^u$" is the right object, and the Oriented split uses the curvelet's maximal analytic domain rather than a world lifetime ✓.
- "This does not depend on which world hosts the geodesic" is true modulo lifetime. But it defines the curvelet through a host world, whereas the locked definition lives in $P(O)$ (Prop. 3.2, Thm 3).
- Suggested wording (N2): "the unique curve in $V$ traced by the geodesic from $(p,u)$ in the patch $P(O)$ (Prop. 3.2; Thm 3). Geodesic lifts in any world agree with it on their common domain; only their lifetimes differ."
- No chart, no quotient beyond isotropy, no finite jet. C27's "$u$-adapted normal coordinates" is a computational device and claims no finite-jet sufficiency.

**Lock-side (§2 line 113). Required (R-B).**
- The prose says "hence also by the **geodesic lift** $\gamma_{O^u}$ that $O^u$ determines". The outline says "curvelet".
- In-house, a geodesic lift is the projection of a geodesic *in a world $W$*, and its domain depends on $W$ (hole-punching). It is not determined by $O^u$ alone.
- So in the one sentence that defines lock-side, the word brings back the world-dependence that the definition excludes.
- Fix: "geodesic lift" → "curvelet". This is the only place a gloss smuggles $W$. The other uses of "geodesic lift" (§4.1 CWS pieces, §4.2 segments) are correct, because they refer to lifts in worlds.

## 5. Ruling on flagged wording: the C15 tree

**The text (Appendix A, C15):** "type $A$ degree $g+2$ at generation $g$; type $E$ degree $g+3$ at even $g$, $g+1$ at odd $g$; type $O$ the reverse; each $A$ node's first child is $E$ or $O$ as needed to start a balanced pair, the rest $A$."

**Rebuild.** The text fixes the degrees and the children of A nodes. It says nothing about:
1. the children of E and O nodes; and
2. the root's type and generation.

I rebuilt the tree under the three readings the text allows. All take the root as type A at $g=0$, whose first child is $O$, since $g=1$ is odd.

| reading | children of E / O | root $O$-share $n=120$, $121$ | limit | property |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| (i) #129 §4 construction | inherit their type (all E / all O) | $0.501278$, $0.498762$ | $1/2$ | limit agreement, $D$ non-constant at every depth $\ge2$ ✓ |
| (ii) | all A | $0.555556$, $0.555556$ | $5/9$ | **no** limit agreement |
| (iii) | A's rule (first child perturbed, rest A) | $0.566873$, $0.566873$ | $0.566873$ | **no** limit agreement |

- Only reading (i) produces the pinned numbers and the claimed property.
- On reading (i), the property is as the prose states it:
  - The degree products are non-constant at every depth $\ge2$, so no cofinal sequence of depth cuts has constant $D$.
  - Siblings become asymptotically balanced; the ratios at depths 1–3 are within $0.003$ of 1 by $n=200$.
  - So counts converge to per-vertex weights, and cofinal constancy is sufficient but not necessary.
  - Degrees grow as $g+2$ (unbounded), so the tree leaves the bounded-degree case open, as the prose says.

**Ruling:** the claim is correct and the numbers are right, but the description as written does not determine them. Required fix (R-A): add "children of an $E$ (resp. $O$) node are all $E$ (resp. $O$); the root is type $A$ at $g=0$". The "balanced pair" phrase would also read more clearly as "(so that, from the child's generation $h$ on, the $E$/$O$ subtree's degrees over generations $h,h+1$ multiply to $(h+3)(h+2)$, matching $A$)", but that is optional.
