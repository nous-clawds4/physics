## What changed since the v0.5 HOLD (#128)

1. **Realisation (a)** (§4.2). The false cost row is replaced. (a) confines an edge to one analytic-continuation class. It forbids cross-continuation jumps and permits curvature-changing jumps within the class. Also added:
   - the endpoint convention: the realising geodesic leaves along $u''\ne u$ and arrives with tangent $u'$;
   - the realising geodesic carries no proper time;
   - a lock-side form, $O'^{u'}\in F(O)\setminus\gamma_{O^u}$;
   - "the Kretschmann example models the schema without (a)";
   - (b) re-glossed as "no realisation required" (broader than #128's wording, per #131).
2. **Clause (2) and arrival** (§4.2 items 2, 7–8; §5.3). #128's option (A) is taken, with the state enlarged to $(O^u,a)$, per #129. Also added: root $a=\mathrm{seg}$, a named no-Zeno clause, and clause (2) restated per history.
3. **Object** (§1, §2, §3). The isotropy clause is restored, the vertex space is named as Paper 1's observer space (a reading), and §3 says "discontinuous in oriented observer space".
4. **Paper 1** (§1, §7, §10). A §1 sentence says the object is kept, motion revised and measure revised, and that the countability thesis is not carried. §10 has a row for the thesis. The reading of the weight is restored, the chance reading defined, and "Paper 1's spirit" deleted. EPP2 is relabelled "departure from Paper 1 if promoted".
5. **No-no-go** (title, §1, §8, §9). The title is now "Observer space and graph joins". §1 says none is exhibited, §8 restores the list of what a sketch would need, and Obs-local Bell compatibility is a §9 Hope.
6. **Disclosures** (§2, §9, header). The physical cost of analyticity and "matter open" are restored. The header carries an outline-to-prose audit list.

Two further concerns raised by the Chief of Staff:

- **(i) A flat lab never branches under the example slice.** §5.1 records this as a named constraint, not a law: no branch points on Minkowski, de Sitter or the Einstein static universe (ESU) for any lock-side scalar, and none on flat ΛCDM for $K$, $R$, $R_{ab}R^{ab}$. The text says "Under this engine the flat laboratory never branches." It is recorded as a cost; the slice is not changed.
- **(ii) Pure-geodesic weight $3^{-n}$.** §7 records "no jump dominance" as a named constraint, not a law: (J1) sparse branching vertices, or (J2) observationally null jumps. The Kretschmann rule fails both (C17). No rule meeting either is given.
