**(f) §7's Binomial sentence.** The claim is correct only under a supposition the sentence does not state.
- Let $n$ be a **fixed** number of decisions.
- Suppose every history takes at least $n$ decisions, and each of them has $m$ outgoing edges.
- Then under EPP1 each decision jumps with weight $m/(1+m)$, independently of the past. So the number of jumps in the first $n$ decisions is **exactly** Binomial$(n,m/(1+m))$, unconditionally. It is a measure statement, not a sampled one.
- It needs EPP1's equal arm weights and the no-stopping supposition.

If histories can stop earlier, the jump count is not Binomial, either unconditionally or conditioned on survival. Histories stop, for example, under the rule as stated, where the next paragraph applies this: a jump that leaves the bound sector ends the decisions.
- **Exact check** (rule as stated, $c=0.1$, $r_p=10$, `binom_check.py`): the histories that survive $3$ decisions have jump counts $0$–$3$ with weights $1/16, 5/16, 7/16, 3/16$.
- Binomial$(3,2/3)$ gives $1/27, 2/9, 4/9, 8/27$.
- The pure-geodesic weight $(1+m)^{-n}$ is right as it stands, because it needs only its own $n$ decisions.

Recommended patch (§7, "Named constraint, not a law: no jump dominance"):

OLD:
```text
Suppose every decision has $m$ outgoing edges. Then under EPP1 the pure-geodesic history has weight $(1+m)^{-n}$ after $n$ decisions ($3^{-10}\approx1.69\times10^{-5}$ for $m=2$), and the number of jumps is Binomial$(n,m/(1+m))$.
```
NEW:
```text
Suppose every history takes at least $n$ decisions and every decision has $m$ outgoing edges. Then under EPP1 the pure-geodesic history has weight $(1+m)^{-n}$ after $n$ decisions ($3^{-10}\approx1.69\times10^{-5}$ for $m=2$), and the number of jumps in the first $n$ decisions is exactly Binomial$(n,m/(1+m))$, since each decision jumps with weight $m/(1+m)$ independently of the past. If a history can stop deciding earlier, as under the rule as stated when a jump leaves the bound sector, the jump count is in general not Binomial, conditionally on survival or not.
```

The OLD string occurs exactly once in v0.7.1 at `36f7986`.
