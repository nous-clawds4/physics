- **Not a second elementary observer.** Type-(ii) targets are points of $\mathrm{Obs}$ (oriented lumps), not ensemble labels and not reconstructed worlds.
- **Composition does not reinstate a singleton via bad equivalence.** First-edge / out-star is the right cut; path-germ “weaker option” from v0.3 is gone.
- **Abandonable.** Drop Option F → layer (i) / CWS singleton unchanged; Paper 1 non-uniqueness claim then fails — outline says so (§10). Correct.
- **Spacetime-realisation open does not restore 2007 tree-as-theorem.** §4.2 (6) names the fork; either choice must stay a constraint/label, not a derivation of branching from hitchhiking alone.
- **Direction open** is fine at outline; prose must pick one before examples claim “post-branch” or Def. 8 prefixes.

## $V^*$ / $E^*$ — no fold of $E$ into $\sim$

This is the sharpest ontology risk in the promotion.

- $V^*$ **reads** the hitchhiking ensemble $E_\gamma$ to mark extend-vs-not times. That is selection of vertices, not individuation of observers.
- Caveats are loud enough at outline level: Thm 7(3) loci are **ensemble events on one history**, **not** Obs forks; die ≠ second arm; multiple analytic extenders = one next germ.
- Therefore extend-vs-not alone never yields $|B|\ge 2$; a true type-(ii) out is required. That blocks smuggling “ensemble size votes on which observer you are.”
- Soft Prop. 13 pattern still applies: choosing the extend-vs-not rule is extra, not a theorem that $\mathrm{Obs}$ is discrete.

**Prose watch (nit, not HOLD):** when writing §5, keep “ensemble marks a vertex” and “ensemble does not vote on identity / does not create a second elementary observer” in the same breath so a cold reader cannot confuse $V^*$ with EPP2.
