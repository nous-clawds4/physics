## Math notes (PASS, not HOLD)

### 1. Delayed-fork wording (nit)

§3 says the identity theorem forbids “a split that appears only after a finite proper-time delay while the germs still agree.” Substance is right: agreement on a positive-length initial segment of $\tau$ forces continuation in a single analytic $M$ / along a single analytic Obs lift. Slightly sharper for a math reader: the killed object is a *delayed* fork after a thick shared prefix, not “germs still agree” at the fork instant (that would already be no split). Non-blocking.

### 2. “$C^\infty$ map into $\mathrm{Obs}$” under path-space default (named open, not smuggle)

Allowed continuations require $\gamma$ $C^\infty$ as a map into $\mathrm{Obs}$ (§5), while §2/§9 default to path-space without a locked atlas or Fréchet $T_O$. That is **informal but honest**: the primary rigorous cut for $B$ is infinite-order agreement of path-jets at $0$, which does not need charts. Do not read the $C^\infty$ bullet as locking option (2). Geometry does not HOLD on this; §9 already owns the gap.

### 3. Geodesic lifts vs analytic Obs-curves

In-house: a geodesic in a real-analytic $W$ lifts to a real-analytic curve in $\mathrm{Obs}$ (Fermi coordinates; not a claim that $\mathrm{Obs}$ is an analytic manifold). Public CWS asks piecewise geodesic lifts and $C^\infty$ Obs-paths. Compatible: analytic lift $\Rightarrow$ $C^\infty$; the public paper does not claim $\mathrm{Obs}$ is an analytic manifold. Keep it that way.

### 4. Compatibility (§2)

$O_i\Leftrightarrow O_j$ via a finite causal geodesic in some $M$, and reverse; not automatically transitive. Geometrically fine as a working relation. Transitive closure = reachability under world switching is correctly left open (§9).

### 5. Public lump identity vs Paper 1 gauge

Paper 1 left Diff/frame quotient open in-house. This public paper *settles* lump identity for its own convention (pointed analytic diffeomorphism = same lump). Ontology already PASSed that as public convention, not a Paper 1 rewrite. Geometry does not reopen it. Do not fold the public lump reading back into Paper 1.

## Stress test (Geometry read)

The §5 EKT/spin questions are the right geometric gate before EPP1:

1. Is $|B(O^{u*})|\ge 2$ under infinite-order matching, or does $C^\infty$ collapse the cartoon to one class?
2. If $\ge 2$, is $B$ finite, countable, or a continuum?
3. If countable infinity, what exhaustion yields ratios?

Until (1)–(2) have a controlled example with discrete $B$, EPP1 has nothing forced to act on. That is already the paper’s hard gate and §11 line. Well-posed. Not a Born claim.
