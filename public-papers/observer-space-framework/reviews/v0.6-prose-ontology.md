## Ontology hygiene notes

1. **Fixed in #135 before filing: a lean in the new §3 history sentence.** r2 added a one-sentence history, drawn from #136's own summary: 2005–06 used both rules, SHPMP 2008 separated them, Paper 1 returned to the count. As first written it ended "EPP1 returns to the 2008 choice, and (M2) would revisit the rule SHPMP rejected." That is true as history. But "the rule SHPMP rejected" hands the reader a reason against (M2), in the section a referee reads for the paper's stance. That is a lean, and §1 and §9 promise none. It now reads "EPP1 takes the 2008 rule as its working postulate and (M2) would take Paper 1's; neither is chosen here (§9)". The cut-dependence cost stays where it belongs, listed with (M2)'s costs in §9, without a verdict. This is logged in the r2 block.
2. **$P(O)$ naming.** N1 and N2 replace "a local simply-connected analytic patch" with "the simply-connected analytic patch $P(O)$". They anchor the curvelet in $P(O)$ through Prop. 3.2 and Thm 3 of `papers/geodesic-lifts.md`. That is the locked definition, and it is world-free. The §2 disclaimer ("a germ-level identification of presentations, not a claim that a maximal global manifold is unique") still stands. So the definite article claims uniqueness of the local patch, which is Paper 1's Theorem A–B reading, not of any global continuation. No change to the object.
3. **SHPMP citation and the frame.** The bullet cites SHPMP for its rules only, and disowns its frame in the same bullet: "That submission argued inside the Everett-versus-decision-theory frame; this paper does not". The Referee 2 sentence in §7 is also filed as belonging to "the frame this paper does not use". So SHPMP now appears three times (§1/§7 pointer, §3 bullet, §7 referees), and each appearance is framed. There is no Everett-vs-DW framing. I cannot check the SHPMP definition numbers and quotes against the manuscript. They are #136's text verbatim, and #136 read the manuscript.
4. **Length and accessibility.** The file is now about 10.1k words including header and Appendix A, against a cap of "about 10k". The body is about 8.5k. The r2 block is the growth. The body stays in the v0.5 plain register. The hardest passages for a lay reader are the (a)/$F(O)$ paragraph in §4.2 and the named-cut criteria in §7. The new leaf example (N7) helps with the latter. The presentation pass could fold the header's r1/r2 lists into one short "changes since v0.5" paragraph, which would bring the file under 10k.
5. **The Bell Hope sentence (#136 nit 3).** "Any route for this Hope would run through the co-existence reading" states where the obstruction lies. It claims no route. It is consistent with the Hope being unshown, and it keeps "Obs-local" (Markov locality of (A)) apart from Bell's spacetime locality, as #133 note 6 asked.
6. **C16 as numerical evidence (#136 nit 4).** This weakens how C16 is labelled, not what it is used for. The §10 countability row rests on C12 alone, so "not carried" does not depend on C16.
