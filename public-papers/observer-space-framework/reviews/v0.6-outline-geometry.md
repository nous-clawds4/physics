## Rulings on Ontology's seven judgment calls

**(1) Arrival tangent $=u'$. Consistent, with a consequence the outline must state (R1).**

*Consistency.* The convention agrees with the endpoint rule: leave along $u''\ne u$. It also agrees with (A): after a jump, the next segment leaves along $u'$, which is exactly the continuation of the realising geodesic. So "jump, then segment" traces one geodesic of $(O,u'')$, whose first stretch is not counted in the history's $\tau$. That matches "labels admissibility; carries no proper time".

*Consequence.* By Prop. 3.2 the geodesic arriving with tangent $u'$ is unique, so (a) with this convention is **lock-side and world-free**:
$$(O^u,O'^{u'})\ \text{is (a)-admissible}\iff O'^{u'}\in F(O)\setminus\gamma_{O^u},\qquad F(O):=\{\gamma_{(O,u'')}(\tau):u''\ \text{future unit},\ \tau>0\}.$$
That is, the target curvelet must pass through the source lump in its past.

On the Kretschmann witness:

| Edge | Target orbit | Meets $r=10$ in its past? | (a)-admissible |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| $c=0.1$ outward, $r'=11.0813$ | $[11.0813,39.0029]$ | no | **no** |
| $c=0.01$ outward, $X$ at $10.1075$ | $[10.1075,21.3322]$ | no | **no** |
| $c=0.1$ inward, $r'=8.9332$ | $[8.9332,10.1029]$ | yes | yes |
| $c=0.01$ inward, $r'=9.8926$ | $[9.8926,18.7504]$ | yes | yes |

Further:

- The inward $c=0.01$ chain stays (a)-admissible for 12 steps. At step 13 the target $8.7383$ becomes an apastron (below $v_{\rm circ}=v$ at $r=8.75$), its orbit $[8.664,8.738]$ never reaches the source $8.8259$, and admissibility fails.
- So under (a) plus this convention, the C5 chain and C6's $X$ are not edges. The outward targets are reachable as *lumps* (#128's static-recurrence route), but not with arrival tangent $u'$.
- My #129 phrase "not shown at oriented level" is too weak. The route **fails** at oriented level under this convention.

Required (R1):

1. State the $F(O)$ characterisation.
2. Say that the Kretschmann witness is a model of the schema **without** (a): its outward edges need realisation to be dropped.
3. Re-gloss (b) as "no realisation required: admits cross-continuation edges **and** same-class edges that fail (a)". Not-(a) is not the same as cross-continuation.
4. Change "not shown" to "fails at oriented level".

Optional: re-pin C6 on an (a)-admissible germ, $X'$ at $r=9.8926$ (orbit $[9.8926,18.7504]$, radial period $400.528$), so the arrival-dependence pin survives if (a) is later adopted.

**(2) "$R\ne\emptyset$" as a working Postulate. Acceptable only with added text (R2); otherwise re-file as Open.**

What the outline has now:

- ✓ It is labelled working and abandonable.
- ✓ It is not used to assert $\lvert B_R\rvert\ge2$ at any named vertex.
- ✓ EPP1's domain is empty.

What is missing:

- **(i)** A statement that it is *not derivable*: locked data do not determine any type-(ii) edge (Prop. 13, `papers/type-ii-adopted.md`).
- **(ii)** A statement that it is *existence only*: it implies $\lvert B_R\rvert\ge2$ at no named vertex and gives EPP1 no domain. This also removes the apparent clash with "domain empty because no $R$ is adopted": existence is asserted, no $R$ is adopted.
- **(iii)** "This files Paper 1's non-uniqueness premise" overstates. $R\ne\emptyset$ is an existential over all of $V$. Paper 1's "the evolution is not unique" needs $\mathrm{succ}_R\ne\emptyset$ along the histories in question. Write "files that premise in its weakest, existential form".

With (i)–(iii) the Postulate adds one abandonable existential hypothesis, and that is justified as the minimal filing. Without them, keep it Open.

**(3) Dead-end convention (i). Fine as default.** The criterion holds verbatim with $D$ taken along each path's actual length, and (i) conserves unconditioned EPP1 mass. Small trees, depth-2 cut, leaf $a$ at depth 1:

| Tree | (i) truncated included | (ii) survivors, conditioned |
| --- | --- | --- |
| root→$\{a,\ b\to\{b_1,b_2\}\}$ | $a{:}\,1/2,\ b_i{:}\,1/4$ vs count $1/3$; $D=(2,4,4)$; **disagree** | $1/2,1/2$ vs $1/2,1/2$; $D=(4,4)$; agree |
| root→$\{a,\ b\to\{b_1\}\}$ | $1/2,1/2$ vs $1/2,1/2$; $D=(2,2)$; agree | agree |
| root→$\{a,\ b\to\{b_1,b_2,b_3\}\}$ | $1/2,1/6^{\times3}$ vs $1/4$; **disagree** | $1/3$ each; agree |

So under (i), a leaf before the cut breaks agreement unless its $D$ matches. Under (ii) it never matters. The table and the "can disagree" clause are consistent with this.

(i) does not conflict with §5.2's "ending and continuing through the same germs are one evolution". §5.2 concerns ensemble dropout, where a continuing sibling exists. A tree leaf here is a history with no continuation (a singular endpoint, or a no-Zeno end).

**(4) Scalar class = lock-side analytic functions of curvature, its derivatives and $u$. Accept. It catches the plane wave.**

Computation on the #129 plane wave ($H=e^{-u^2}(x^2-y^2)$):

- $k=\partial_v$ is covariantly constant: $\Gamma^a{}_{bv}\equiv0$, and $k_a=\delta^u_a$.
- $k\cdot U=U^u$.
- The full Bel–Robinson $T(U,U,U,U)$, computed from $C$ and ${}^*C$ with generic $U$ at a generic point, is $4(U^u)^4e^{-2u^2}$, i.e. $(k\cdot U)^4\times4\times\text{profile}^2$.
- On a numerically integrated geodesic with transverse motion ($U^u=0.8$, $x$ running from $0.3$ to $8.36$), $U^u$ stays $0.8000000000$ throughout. $W$ has exactly **1** critical point on $\tau\in[0,5]$, at $\tau=2.501$ where $u=0.00000$; predicted $2.5$.

Lock-side: the scalar is a full contraction of $C$ (jet data), ${}^*C$ (parity enters twice) and $u$. It is invariant under every germ isometry fixing $u$, so it is well defined on $O^u$ modulo isotropy.

What the class does not fix:

- Minkowski, dS and ESU stay empty.
- More generally, on **every locally symmetric lump** ($\nabla\mathrm{Riem}=0$), transvections carry $(\gamma(0),\dot\gamma(0))$ to $(\gamma(\tau),\dot\gamma(\tau))$, so the oriented germ is constant along every curvelet and every lock-side scalar is constant.
- Example: the constant-profile (Cahen–Wallach) plane wave $H=x^2-y^2$. Sympy finds 0 nonzero components of $\nabla\mathrm{Riem}$, and $W=4(k\cdot U)^4$ is constant along geodesics, so $V^\tau=\emptyset$ even though $W>0$.

Nits:

- Add "locally symmetric lumps" to the residuals. It unifies Minkowski, dS, ESU and Cahen–Wallach.
- In vacuum, $T_{abcd}u^au^bu^cu^d$ *is* $W=E_{ab}E^{ab}+B_{ab}B^{ab}$ (both computed as $2k^4e^{-2u^2}$). List it once.

**(5) Backward-only pairs left Open. Fine, but the parenthetical overstates.**

"(a), if adopted, is future-directed" does not by itself exclude backward-only pairs. (a) needs only *some* future-directed realisation, and a germ on $\gamma_{O^u}(\tau<0)$ could also lie in $F(O)$ via another $u''$.

On the instance nothing arises: bound Schwarzschild curvelets are periodic in $V$, so every germ on them is reached at some $\tau>0$. Radial-from-rest outgoing germs are not in $F(r{=}10)$, because their past never meets $r=10$.

Reword to: "not needed for any pin; not excluded by (a) as such."

**(6) Bare-$u$-jump selection rule deleted. Consistent; nothing relies on it.**

- ESU: $V^\tau=\emptyset$, so under $R\subseteq V^\tau\times V$ the bare boost is never an edge. C10 remains a classification example only; nit: say so in the pin.
- Flat lump: bare $u$-jumps are the identity modulo isotropy (C28).
- At a Schwarzschild vertex a bare $u$-jump is an ordinary candidate. It is governed by (a) or (b) like any other. Under (a) plus arrival $=u'$, e.g. the inward radial germ at $r=10$ is admissible (up and back down), while "at rest at $r=10$" is not: that apex's past never revisits $r=10$.
- The factorisation sentence in (a) uses bare orientation change as part of realisation, not as an edge. It is unaffected.
- No remaining sentence needs the rule.

**(7) C4 pin. Verified. Wording is fine, with two nits.**

- Radial fall from rest at $r=10$ has $\dot r=-\sqrt{2/r-1/5}<0$ for $\tau>0$, and it is monotone inward.
- $\tau(r)$: $r=9$: $13.903$; $8$: $19.312$; $6$: $26.265$; $4$: $30.768$; $2^+$: $33.701$; $1$: $34.638$; $r\to0$: $35.124$.

"From rest at the periastron event" is the right realising geodesic under the endpoint convention. Given arrival tangent $u'$, it is *forced* (the backward extension of the target curvelet), and it leaves along $u''=\partial_{\hat t}\ne u$.

Nits:

- The pin exhibits a 1-parameter slice of $F(O)$. The full admissible set is open in the 3-parameter $(r,v_r,\lvert v_\perp\rvert)$ space.
