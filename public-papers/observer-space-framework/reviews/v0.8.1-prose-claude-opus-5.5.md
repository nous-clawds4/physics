**#203's lemma (in the review record only).** At every turning point, the edge into the orbit's radial range fails (a). The proof: a turning point is a periastron iff $v^2>1/(r-2)$, and the rule keeps $v$, so the into-range target keeps its turning type and its orbit lies wholly beyond the source. That argument is correct. My own spot-check on 3,000 turning points of random bound orbits, with $c$ from $0.001$ to $0.2$ and short of the throat, gives **0** into-range edges passing (a). The other edge passed in only 2,304 of 3,000 cases, which confirms that the passing half is instance-specific.

The patch's instance-scoped wording is therefore correct and adds no claim. Keeping the lemma out of a patch that promises "no physics claim added" is sound. It would be worth carrying in the next full version with its two-line proof: it makes C33's "$m\le1$ at every turning point" a theorem, and it states the general failing half. That is a suggestion, not a gate.
