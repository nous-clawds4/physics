## What changed from v0.7-prose

1. **Required change 1 (§9, calls table, App. C13; P1–P5).** The paragraph "Both rules as counts at a cut" is rewritten. State-function weights are available at any fixed anchored depth, not only depth 1. Depth 1 is particular only in reading nothing of $R$ beyond the current arms and needing no named depth. A distant fixed cut gives equal weight, but its implied next-step weights depend on where the cut sits. Re-anchoring removes that dependence but in general gives up equal weight at a fixed cut (C13 now pins a re-anchoring example). The two properties hold together only where the tree's shape allows it. "Unless it is anchored" is gone. The (M2) cost line and the calls-table (M1)/(M2) and T2 cells follow. No (M1)/(M2) choice is made.
2. **Required change 2 (§4.2, §9 T3 paragraph, App. C33; P6–P8).** "13 or 14 jumps" is replaced by the exact EPP1 law of the jump count under the (a)-restriction at $c=0.01$ (from the $[10,20]$ periastron; $m\le1$, so each decision is a fair coin): 12, 13 or 14 jumps with probabilities $26/3^{12}\approx4.9\times10^{-5}$, $9338/3^{10}\approx0.158$, $447373/3^{12}\approx0.842$. The stuck count is 14,478.
3. **Your recommended edits (#168 §4.5 / §5, as #169 ruled).** R-b (reworded), R-c (reworded as a ratio of expectations, with the unchecked 0.719 dropped), R-d (with its optional §7 regular-or-biregular criterion), R-e, and the one-line #163 header log. Declined: the T3 circularisation clause, because the census does not show that branching stops once the jump exceeds the orbit's width.
4. **Literature #161 nits 2–3.** Your review noted that the v0.7 cover wrongly said these were applied. They are now applied: the T1 row label, and "(a) = T3, with its proper-time part under T1" (header item 2; the §9 calls-table introduction in parentheses).
5. **Panel patch (f) (#171).** §7's Binomial sentence now states that it assumes every history takes at least $n$ decisions, and notes that, when histories can stop earlier, the count is in general not Binomial, conditionally on survival or not.
6. **Literature #172 nits 1–3 (#172).** R-c's use of Wald's identity states its hypotheses (each decision jumps with weight 2/3 under EPP1 regardless of the past; a stopping rule with finite expected decisions, such as a fixed cap) and cites A. Wald, Ann. Math. Statist. 15, 283–296 (1944). R-b says "turning point". §11 says the file patches v0.7-prose.
7. **Consistency.** The Status line, §11 and README name v0.7.1 as current and v0.7-prose as prior, and record that the diff's panel check passed. That status edit came after the panel, was made by the Lead, and appears in the changelog.
