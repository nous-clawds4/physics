## HOLD items (ontology clarity — fix labels before prose)

### 1. $O$ vs $O^u$ vs the locked elementary observer

Paper 1’s locked elementary observer is **one** object: infinite jet of a real-analytic metric **plus** a future time direction, equivalently the unique simply-connected real-analytic patch.

The outline (and `mathematical-framework.md`) split that into:

- bare $O = j_p^\infty$ with **no** preferred $u$;
- oriented $O^u = (O,u)$.

That split can be a public convenience. It is still an **ontology change relative to Paper 1** unless the outline says, in §2:

- which object is a point of $\mathrm{Obs}$ for counting / typicality talk;
- that $B$ is defined at $O^u$ (the outline already wobbles: “$B(O)$ (or $B(O^u)$)”);
- that “nothing in the bare germ prefers a particular $u$” does **not** retract the Paper 1 claim that a physical observer at an instant includes a time direction.

**HOLD:** one sentence in §2 naming the public convention ($O$ vs $O^u$) and which one the equal-probability story is about. Do not leave both as interchangeable defaults.

### 2. Gauge / lump deferred is not a small deferral

§2: “gauge/lump issues deferred.” For Paper 1 the lump *is* the object (jet ≡ simply-connected analytic patch; two presentations of the same lump; Diff that moves the lump ≠ rename). A public ontology section that defines $O$ as a jet and postpones lump identity will look, to outsiders, like raw coordinate jets.

**HOLD:** either (a) one sentence: public $O$ is the jet/patch pair as in Paper 1, gauge as lump identity, details in-house; or (b) explicit “coordinate-jet placeholder, not the final object.” Do not expand §§2–5 while “observer = jet” and “lump deferred” sit side by side without a flag.

### 3. Ensembles vs elementary observer

$E_W(O)$, $E_M(O)$, sibling points, and co-occurrence in $E_M$ are useful. They must not become a second elementary observer or a vote for global topology.

Paper 1 firewall: extra topologies around one jet are a **relative ensemble**, not a second elementary observer; ambient $M$ / global solution-label does not get a vote on when evolutions split; no reconstruction of a whole $W$ from $O$.

**HOLD:** in §2, one breath:

- $E_W$ / $E_M$ are ensembles compatible with a germ, not additional points of $\mathrm{Obs}$;
- sibling points and multiple anchors are multiplicity in the ensemble, not a fork of the elementary object;
- the 2008 global-$W$-from-$O$ reconstruction stays off (outline §10 is close; say it at the ensemble definition, not only at the end).

Also keep the outline’s own note: intersection of distinct world ensembles is empty; co-occurrence lives in $E_M$. That is easy to lose in prose.
