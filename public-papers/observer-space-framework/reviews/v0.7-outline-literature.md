### Item 1. SHPMP Axiom 3 (verified verbatim)

From `2008-03-31-Submit-SHPMP.pdf`:
- **Axiom 1**, printed p. 17. It makes the ensemble generated by a state machine whose transitions follow Definition 3 equal to the observer collection $U$. That gives equivalence (1), Definition 1 ⇔ Definition 3.
- **Axiom 2**, printed p. 18: "Given any two states ψ̄i and ψ̄j immediately downstream of state ψ̄, then the number of sub-branches from ψ̄ to ψ̄i equals the number of sub-branches from ψ̄ to ψ̄j." That gives equivalence (2), Definition 3 ⇔ Definition 4.
- **Motivation for Axiom 3**, printed p. 28. Counting every world as equally probable gives 1/3 on Fig. 4B, and "Upon reflection we see that this would essentially resurrect [Definition 2], which we have already rejected! Do we reject [it] too, or is there a way to save it? We will argue that we can save it with the following axiom:". Brackets replace the 2008 rule names.
- **Axiom 3**, printed p. 28 (PDF p. 32), verbatim: "**There is one (and only one) Everettian world for each Everettian sub-branch.**" It restores 1/4, 1/4, 1/2 on Fig. 4B.
- **Definition 5**, printed pp. 28–29: "Given a branch that splits into N branches, each of which is represented by ψ̄j, each branch inherits a share of probability measure from its parent branch in proportion to the number of worlds in the ensemble Mψ̄j." The text adds that it is transitive.
- **Equivalence (3)**, printed p. 29: "Axiom 3 allows us to conclude that [Definition 5] is equivalent to [Definition 4] … – and hence to outcome and observer [counting] as well."
- **Axiom 4**, printed p. 29: "No two sub-branches are globally identical." It is added to answer the objection that N identical sub-branches should not mean N worlds.

**Is the precedent claim accurate?** Yes, for the *agreement*: in 2008, the EPP2 form (Definition 5) equalled the EPP1 form (Definition 3) by Axioms 2 and 3 together. v0.7 §3 says exactly that.

It is not a precedent for the *mechanism*. The Hope is that post-branch ensembles factor with a shared reservoir. SHPMP instead postulated an identity (one world per sub-branch, with sub-branches split evenly). The prose should say "precedent for the agreement … reached by postulate". The changelog and §7 should say "Axioms 2 and 3", not "Axiom 3" alone.

v0.7's "Definition 5 (the world count)" is loose. The plain world count ("all worlds are equally probable") is the rule SHPMP says would bring back Definition 2. Definition 5 is the per-split, world-proportional rule. Text under L7.

The rest of the SHPMP record is unchanged from my r2 comment:
- Definition 2, p. 15, is the count at the end.
- Definition 3, p. 16, is the per-split rule. Footnote 6 gives the product along a path.
- Fig. 2A shows 1/3 against 1/4.
- The count is rejected "in its simple form" on Wallace's slice objection, pp. 16–17.
- Definition 4 is the sub-branch count, set equal to Definition 3 by Axiom 2.

**Correction to my own r2 comment:** "a giant classical stochastic state machine" is on printed p. 17, not p. 16 (#154 had it right). The prose gives no page, so nothing changes.

### Item 4. MERW

Verified against the arXiv text of Burda, Duda, Luck and Waclaw (PRL 102, 160602 (2009)):
- MERW is the choice of $P_{ij}$ "such that all trajectories of given length t and given endpoints are equiprobable".
- The stationary density is $\pi^*_i=\psi_i^2$ for the leading eigenvector of the adjacency matrix.
- MERW "is equivalent to generic random walk if it takes place on a regular lattice".

v0.7 gets the scope right:
- "between given endpoints";
- the depth-cut count from a root matches MERW's transition law only in the long-path limit (C36);
- the Born hazard is carried as a hazard, not a claim.

The biregular addition is correct. I checked $K_{2,3}$: max |P_MERW − P_generic| = 2.2×10⁻¹⁶. Regularity is sufficient, not the criterion. This corrects the phrase "coincide on regular graphs, exactly the D-constant criterion" in my #132.
