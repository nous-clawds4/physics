## Postulate / hope / open / firewall

### Postulate (chosen launching pad)

- Observer = infinite jet of a real-analytic Lorentzian metric (oriented \(O^u\) optional).
- CWS: piecewise geodesic lifts; switch at shared germ; Obs path \(C^\infty\); orientation does not jump.
- Branches \(B(O)\): equivalence classes of allowed CWS continuations; equal weight only when \(B(O)\) is discrete.
- Working narrative weight: **EPP1** (equal weight on arms).

### Hope (not theorems of v0.1)

- Product ansatz \(\{\mathrm{A},\mathrm{B}\}\times\mathcal{R}\) so EPP2 collapses to EPP1.
- Later: CWS (+ whatever forces discrete \(B(O)\)) *forces* weights (Born as goal, not claim).
- Einstein equation as dream goal; do not assume it on \(M\) yet.

### Open

- Tangent space on Obs (none / jets / topology only).
- What makes \(B(O)\) discrete under CWS (incl. EKT / spin stress test).
- Exhaustion / cancellation for countable arms or countable \(|E|\).
- Compatibility transitive closure vs one-\(M\) motion.
- Analytic germ vs small-ball object (public v0.1 currently analytic jet).

### Firewall (must not claim)

- No Born derivation.
- No Einstein derivation.
- No grain of Obs from continuity alone.
- No \(|a|^2\) put in by hand as multiplicity.
- No assertion that Bell is already refuted — only Obs-local branching + no-no-go sketch.
- No slide from no-no-go / hand-built non-50-50 tree into a Born proof.
- In-house theorem numbers from `papers/` are background, not required reading for v0.1.

**Kill list (already named in outline; keep in prose):** hand-built trees matching \(|a|^2\) as illustrations only; circularity wall (EPP + engineered tree ≠ proof); “not automatically a Bell-local HV theory” ≠ “Bell is refuted.”

## Desk-reject risks (flag early)

1. **Everett-vs-decision-theory framing.** SHPMP-D-08-00021 was rejected as egalitarianism vs Deutsch–Wallace under Everett, plus circularity / hidden-variable smell. Outline currently avoids that frame. Keep it that way in the abstract and intro: ontology of germs + CWS + no-no-go, not “Everett probability without DW.”
2. **Hidden-variable cousin smell.** Referee 1 (2008) read the program as inferior Bohmian typicality. §6’s “local in Obs, nonlocal in spacetime” helps if stated as *program claim, not theorem*. Do not let the no-no-go section read as “we have a local HV theory that beats Bell.”
3. **Stoica collapse.** Without a one-liner, readers map “counting + equal weight” to Stoica 2209.08621. Firewall already says no \(|a|^2\) by hand; say explicitly this is not Hilbert-space ontic counting.
4. **4-geon costume.** Fine as cartoon; dangerous if §2 ontology sounds like Hadley. Paper 1 stay untouched; this public paper may mention 4-geons only as picture.

## Nits (not holes)

1. **EPP / “egalitarianism.”** If later prose reuses the 2008 word “egalitarianism,” expect DW-comparison desk-reject. Prefer EPP1 / EPP2 as labeled here.
2. **\(C^\infty\) switch matching.** Outline already notes infinite-order matching in \(\tau\) is strong. In prose, keep that as a *constraint that may thin switches*, not as a free EKT cartoon.
3. **Discrete \(B(O)\).** EPP1 is vacuous or continuous-measure territory if \(B(O)\) is a continuum. §5 and §9 already say this; make it a hard gate before any weight talk.
4. **Product ansatz \(\infty/\infty\).** Shared reservoir \(\mathcal{R}\) still needs a named exhaustion. Keep as hope.
5. **Orientation \(V=\mathrm{Lift}(u)\).** Depends on tangent structure marked open in §9. Path-space reading (option 1) should be the default sentence in v0.1 prose so \(V\) is not smuggled as a locked chart.
6. **In-house \(R\) leftovers.** §3 correctly declines the full inventory. Do not load theorem numbers from `papers/` into v0.1.
