## Claim A — join clause

### ACCEPT

Pressure-tested against `piecewise-geodesic-Ck-graph.md` (Options C/E/F), ontology F adoption, `B-ge2-minimal-toy.md`, Prop. 3.2.

1. **Continue arm (same oriented germ).** Outgoing analytic-geodesic piece from $O^{u*}$ is the unique curvelet (Prop. 3.2), for every $k$. Finite $k$ does not reopen a star of type-(i) lifts. Claude is right that #92 Step 1 is shared oriented germ + geodesic uniqueness, and Step 2 is equivalence at $0$ — not “$C^\infty$ matching order” as the singleton engine among geodesic pieces. Public prose’s “Infinite-order / $C^\infty$ matching at joins is rejected: that was the HOLE under CWS” **misattributes** the singleton among geodesic lifts to matching order.

2. **Type-(ii) jump to a distinct lump (the edges that give $\|B\|\ge 2$).**
   - **$k=1$:** In Riemann normal coordinates adapted to $u$, every Lorentzian germ has the same 1-jet ($\eta$, vanishing first derivatives) with $u=\partial_0$. Along a geodesic piece $\nabla_uu=0$, so there are no $u$-derivatives to match. Truncated matching through order $1$ holds between **any** two lumps: **vacuous**.
   - **$k\ge 2$:** The 2-jet encodes curvature at the point. Matching $j^kg$ across distinct lumps forces equal curvature (under any identification that makes the comparison defined). That **forbids** Minkowski$\to$FLRW and every curvature-changing jump — including the toy’s $e_{\mathrm{ii}}$.
   - **Identification:** Germ-isometry / isotropy identification is for the **same** lump. Across distinct lumps there is no canonical tangent-space ID; the toy already said matching is “impossible across distinct germs anyway.”

3. **What actually escapes the CWS singleton for branching.** Discontinuous Obs jump + discrete graph incidence + out-star / first-edge equivalence — i.e. **Option E residue**, with analytic-geodesic labels on open edges. Pairing E with C (Option F) does not buy branching power: C is automatic on the continue arm and empty or prohibitive on distinct-lump outs.

### QUALIFY (narrow — does not save F as branching mechanism)

Truncated-jet language can still be written as **housekeeping**, not as the HOLE escape:

- On the **continue / Thm 7 arm**, state that jet+$u$ agreement is automatic (same oriented germ).
- If kept to **exclude bare $u$-jumps at a fixed lump**, say that explicitly and reconcile with §3 (those jumps are already type (ii) by the oriented split). That is a selection rule on $R$, not finite-$k$ magic.
- It does **not** rehabilitate F for curvature-changing measurement jumps at $k\ge 2$, nor give $k=1$ any bite between distinct lumps.

### Recommendation (A)

For public **v0.5**: **demote F → E residue** in the title and join paragraph. Rewrite: analytic-geodesic pieces on open edges; joins = discontinuous type-(ii) jumps + graph incidence; arms = out-star. Delete “that was the HOLE under CWS” as matching-order story. Optional: keep a one-liner that continue-arm matching is automatic / bare $u$-jump policy is separate. Do **not** invent a law of $R$.

## Claim B — $V^*$ continuum under §2 ensemble

### ACCEPT

Pressure-tested against public v0.4-prose §2 + §5.1, `Vstar-extend-vs-not.md`, `B-ge2-minimal-toy.md`, `patch-edge.md` Thm 6, `obs-as-a-set.md` (flat germ), Thm 23(2a).

1. **Hole-punching.** Under §2 as written ($E_W$ = pointed compatible worlds; $M$ connected real-analytic Lorentzian; no inextendibility/completeness), for any $(M,p)$ and $\tau_0\in(0,\tau_M)$, $M'=M\setminus\{\sigma(\tau_0)\}$ is a compatible world whose geodesic dies at $\tau_0$. Distinct $\tau_0$ survive pointed-isometry quotient. Hence $T_{\mathrm{die}}\supseteq(0,\tau_M)$ and $V^*$ is the whole curvelet under naive extend-vs-not. Hard-gate clause (2) fails identically. Geometry’s earlier “if lifetimes are wild, continuum risks return” understated a **theorem** under the public ensemble.

2. **Inextendibility alone does not restore discreteness.** Cosmic-string / conical-flat families: inextendible, flat germ everywhere, continuum of geometrically distinct death times by axis distance. So “require inextendible $E_\gamma$” kills $W_{\mathrm{cut}}$ and hole-punches, but **not** continuum $T_{\mathrm{die}}$ in general.

3. **$P(O)$ alone.** Nothing dies along the maximal simply-connected patch in the Minkowski case ($\tau_\partial=\infty$); $V^*$ empty. Extend-vs-not then supplies no $\tau$-slice.

4. **Motivation.** Discrete $V^*$ under extend-vs-not is a **chosen discrete sub-ensemble** (grain on $E_W$), or an equivalent selection of death times — not earned as “better motivated than an arbitrary grain of $\mathrm{Obs}$” without saying that. Timing of splits by which worlds die is **E-smuggling** in the sense of Thm 23(2a) (extra $W$ used to timestamp).

5. **Toy.** See next section: $\{W_{\mathrm{M}},W_{\mathrm{cut}}\}$ hand-picked; $W_{\mathrm{cut}}$ is restricted Minkowski **killed** by Thm 6 as a finite-lifetime *patch* example; flat curvelet $\Rightarrow$ $\gamma(1)$ same Obs point as root under “same germ = same vertex.”

### QUALIFY — what extra restores discreteness *in principle*?

| Extra on $E_W$ / $V^*$ | Restores discrete $T_{\mathrm{die}}$? | Still “better than a grain”? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Inextendible (or maximal analytic) members only | Kills hole-punch / $W_{\mathrm{cut}}$; **no** (cosmic-string continuum) | No |
| Inextendible + discrete topology / parameter family (named finite list of deficits, …) | Yes, by hand | No — grain on ensemble parameters |
| Drop ensemble-timed $V^*$; use **lock-side $\tau$-slice** (e.g. critical points / levels of a non-constant analytic curvature scalar along $\gamma$; outline already named this candidate) | Discrete in $\tau$ by 1-variable analyticity when the scalar is non-constant | **Closer:** uses lock-side curvelet data, not $E_W$ votes; choice of scalar/level still **extra** (honest grain-like choice on the curvelet, not on all of $\mathrm{Obs}$) |
| History-time tagged vertices $(\mathrm{lump},\tau)$ on homogeneous curvelets | Makes discrete marks possible as history data | Extra; not a theorem |

Geometry lean for v0.5: **do not** sell extend-vs-not + raw §2 ensemble as supplying discrete $V^*$. Either (i) move public $\tau$-slice to a **lock-side candidate** (curvature critical points) with scalar/level named extra, or (ii) keep extend-vs-not only as an in-house schematic and name **sub-ensemble selection** as the extra that discreteness requires.

### Recommendation (B)

Public v0.5: state Claude’s continuum theorem under §2 ensemble; demote ensemble-timed $V^*$ as the public hard-gate clause (2) engine; prefer lock-side $\tau$-slice language (already in §5.3 candidates) **or** explicit “chosen discrete sub-ensemble” cost. Import ensemble hygiene (inextendibility choice) into §2 if $E_W$ remains load-bearing. Reword “not a vote of global topology on when evolutions split” vs timing vote — or drop ensemble-timed $V^*$ from the public draft.
